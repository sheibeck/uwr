## 3. Design-guard risks

1. **Literal colours.** SVG strokes `#595d6c`, `#7a6fc0`, `#9184d9`, `#6b6f80`. Danger `#4dabf7`, `#ffd43b`, `#ff6b6b`, `oklch(0.78 0.12 150)`. Wait and bad `oklch(0.8 0.13 75)`, `oklch(0.72 0.14 25)`. Page `#0f1019` and `rgba(0,0,0,.35)`. Map them to `--color-con-*`, the accent and neutral tokens, and GraphPlane's scoped svg classes.
2. **Off-scale type.** The mock uses 9.5, 10.5, 11, 11.5, 12.5, 13, 15, 16 and 18px, and weight 600 on the YOU pill. Snap to 10, 12, 14 and 20 with weights 400 and 500. The strip headings, sub-lines and N take 10; names and rows take 12.
3. **Off-scale spacing.** Gaps and padding of 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 14, 18 and 22px. Snap to 4, 8, 16 and 24. Strip widths (156/92) and the label width (112) are sizes, not spacing, but the label width should reuse `LABEL_WIDTH` (144) or a named constant.
4. **Mobile targets under 44px.** The region-name button is 40px, the search field 36px, the up pill 32px, and the gate pills 26px (ours are already 44). All need 44px.
5. **Clickable non-buttons.** Node labels are `<div onClick>` and the popover closes on a backdrop `<div onClick>`. Keep one button per node and close on Escape or an outside pointerdown. The `all:unset` buttons drop the focus ring, so add `:focus-visible` outlines.
6. **Opacity dimming.** Locked neighbour chips use 0.6, methods 0.7, and other-region nodes 0.75. MapMeta's rule is "dimmed without opacity" (the element stays operable), so use token colours instead.
7. **Uppercase and letter-spacing** in the strip and section headings, and in `YOU`. Check these against the UI-SPEC caption style before copying them.
8. **Positioning.** There are no negative margins. The mock uses negative `top`/`left` offsets for the pill and labels, which should come from `graphLayout` like every other position.
9. **Server text.** Quotes, NPC names and item names in `What you know` and `Heard about it from` must be text nodes (no `v-html`).
10. **Icons.** Phosphor web-font classes become `@phosphor-icons/vue`: PhMapTrifold, PhCaretDown, PhMagnifyingGlass, PhEar, PhCheckCircle, PhArrowUp, PhCompass, PhLink, PhBinoculars, PhScroll, PhChatCircleDots, PhFootprints, PhFlagBanner, PhSignpost, PhStack, PhLadder, PhSunHorizon, PhUser, PhEnvelopeSimple. The canvas labels' `ui-monospace` is mock chrome only.
11. **SVG.** The outline and edges stay in GraphPlane under `src/map` (allowed). The north arrow and strip are HTML and icons, not `<svg>`. The popover and sheet live in `src/map`.
12. **Motion.** The 1000ms glide and the 1200ms ring transition need a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback (Phase 51.5.1 owns the motion tokens).
