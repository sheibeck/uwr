12Cartographer map: 11a plus an Unplaced strip, and dungeon levels

Same screen as 11a: regions, entrances, travel checks and cooldown all work the same way. The party bar is a placeholder here. What changes: the server holds every true position, but only sends a position once this character learns how the place relates to somewhere already on their map. Until then the place sits in the Unplaced strip at the left of the map, grouped by region, so it never hints at a direction. A place you've heard of without knowing its region goes under Region unknown, which shows on every region's map until you learn either its region (it moves to that region's list) or a bearing (it goes on the map). Travelling a road to it, an NPC's bearing, a map item or a survey fixes it, and it glides to its true spot and its roads fade in. If a bearing is measured from a place you haven't placed yet, it's held until that place is fixed. Levels: a dungeon can have a level beneath it, mapped separately and measured from its entrance stair. The dungeon gets a stack badge, its panel links to the level below, and the underground view shows a breadcrumb and a level list. Places on different levels never share bearings. Server: character_map_places (character, location, source, from_location, learned_at) and character_map_bearings for held bearings. The map payload only includes x/y for rows in the first table.

12aLive. Click the region button (or "All 16 regions") to search and switch regions. Travel to Saltmarsh Docks, then take the road to the Drowned Chapel, and Lorelle's bearing places the Eel Market too. The strip only lists places in the region you're in, plus Region unknown. Select the Sunken Crypt and view the level below, then read the sexton's rubbing. Tweaks: slow transition, cooldown, low stamina.

11Map & travel: region entrances you can see, and a clear answer to "can I go?"

Moved out of Ledger 2d. Your character and party stay on the left. The map, regions and destination panel live in the rail. Server rules (helpers/travel.ts, travel_config.ts): moving within a region costs 5 stamina per person. Crossing into another region costs 10 stamina and starts a 5-minute region-travel cooldown, which the Wayfarer renown perk shortens by up to 80%. The trip itself is instant. Party travel is all-or-nothing: if one follower is short on stamina, nobody moves. Combat and gathering also block travel. Region entrances sit where a route crosses the dashed border, and each one names the region beyond. Every location shows its terrainType as an icon (town, city, dungeon, woods, plains, mountains, swamp, uncharted). Its ring and label show danger against your level, from the region's dangerMultiplier plus the location's levelOffset. isSafe, bindStone and craftingAvailable show as badges.

11aMap (live). Click a node or a region entrance. Crossing starts the cooldown, shown in the rail header and the destination panel. Use the Tweaks to try an active cooldown or a party member low on stamina.
