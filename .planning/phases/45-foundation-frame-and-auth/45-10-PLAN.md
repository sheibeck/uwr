- HeaderBar: placeLabel, timeOfDay, levelUp, newSkill, activeScreen (ScreenId | null), characterName, accountLine, disabled?; emits toggle-screen(screen, opener), logout

- VitalsStrip: same as VitalsRail + levelUp, newSkill, compact?
- ContextRail: none. FeedShell: compact?
- LocationRow: locationName, timeOfDay
- TabBar: activeTab, sheetOpen; emits select(tab, opener)
- NoticeBars: reconnecting, nextRetryAt, versionPrompt; emits reload
