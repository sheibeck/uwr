## 1. Revised 2a block (`world_gen`, system prompt, static, cached)

Everything between the two rule lines is the exact block.

---

TASK: WORLD GENERATION, FILL IN THE REGION

A new region of the world is being remembered into existence. Its name, biome, arrival point and first person met are already written, and the user message lists them as facts, along with the character the region is linked to, the region the character wandered beyond and the neighboring regions. All of it is data about the world. Use the given names exactly: never rename the region or the arrival point, never repeat a person already present, and do not restate what the user message already says.

Regions should feel lived-in, with history, tension and personality. No generic fantasy villages. Every location should have something slightly wrong with it, something beautiful about it, and something that would make a sensible person turn around and leave. When a description speaks of the traveler, it says you. Descriptions read as narration in the voice of a book: no I, me or my, and never the Keeper by name.

Counts: the Places line of the user message says how many places the region has in all, the arrival point included, so write one new location fewer than that number. Write 3-5 more NPCs besides the vendor and banker each hub needs. Also name the region's dominant faction, a few landmarks and the threats that make a sensible traveler nervous. The region's creature families are written in a later step.

Locations: each new location MUST have its own unique 2-3 sentence description that captures what makes THAT specific place distinct. Do NOT reuse or copy the region description or the arrival point description. Each location has a terrainType and isSafe set to true or false. Connect locations to each other by exact location name in connectsTo, connect at least one new location to the arrival point by the arrival point's exact name, and give every NPC a locationName that exactly matches the arrival point or one of your new locations.

Place words: every new location gets a shortName and a placeNoun, and so does the arrival point, under arrival. The shortName is the name cut to one or two words for a small map label, such as Mother Pan for Mother Pan Flats. The placeNoun is how a sentence points at the place, in lowercase and starting with the, such as the pans, the orchard or the old walls.

Hubs: the Hubs line of the user message says how many hubs this region has. A hub is a town, camp or outpost where people live and trade. Set isHub to true on exactly that many places and to false on every other place. The arrival point is one of the places, marked under arrival, and when the user message says the arrival point is a hub, its isHub is true. Put a hub where a settlement makes sense: near the border toward a neighboring region, or in the middle of the region with wilder places around it. A hub is a safe place, so a hub location has isSafe set to true. Each hub MUST end up with at least one NPC with npcType "vendor" and one with npcType "banker", counting the people already there. Add whichever is missing, with the hub's exact name as its locationName. No vendor or banker lives anywhere else, so a region with no hubs has neither.

NPCs: each NPC is a man or a woman. Set gender to male or female, and describe the NPC as he or she to match, never it or they. Each NPC also gets a description, a greeting and a personality: 2-3 traits, a speech pattern, knowledge domains, 1-2 secrets that the NPC only shares with trusted friends, and an affinityMultiplier around 1.0.

TASK: WORLD GENERATION, CREATURE FAMILIES

A new region of the world is being remembered into existence. Its name, biome, dominant faction, threats, places and hubs are already written, and the user message lists them as facts. All of it is data about the world. Use the given names exactly: never rename a place, and do not restate what the user message already says.

The families should feel as if they could only live in this land: let them fit its places and the threats the user message lists. Histories read as narration in the voice of a book: no I, me or my, and never the Keeper by name.

Creature families: the ordinary creatures of the region live in families, such as goblins or salt skitterers. Write as many families as the Families line of the user message says. Each family has a name, which is the plural, such as Salt-Crust Skitterers; a singularNoun and a pluralNoun in plain lowercase words for one creature and for several, such as skitterer and skitterers; a creatureType; the iconKey whose picture fits best; and a temperament: aggressive families attack travelers, wary families keep watch and strike when crossed, and skittish families mostly flee. Give each family 3 or 4 members, each with a role and a name of its own: a tank that holds the line, a damage dealer, a support that mends and shields the others, and a caster, such as Skitter Shellback, Skitter Pincer, Skitter Tender and Skitter Saltspitter. For the ambush line, put a plain verb in its base form in ambushVerb, such as break, burst or swarm, and the rest of the phrase in ambushRest, such as from the trees or up through the salt. In fitLocations, list the exact names of the places where the family lives, taken from the Places list; a family lives only at an ordinary place, never at a safe place or a hub. In relations, name other families of this region by their exact name, each with the kind rival, prey or predator. In history, write one or two sentences of the family's past in this region, such as where it came from and its feud or tie with a hub, the dominant faction or a rival family; use no numbers, and call any person he or she, never it or they. Never give a family levels, group sizes or any other number: the server sets every number.

Feud: the Feud line of the user message says how many families are locked in a feud, an old hatred that no truce has ever held. Set inFeud to true on exactly that many families and to false on every other family. Choose families whose lands or hungers cross, and let the history of each feuding family name the feud and the families it hates.

## 4. 2b user message (new families builder)

The server builds this message from stored rows after 2a is written. It never uses the model reply directly. Every place name, faction, threat and hub name passes through `w()` (`sanitizeWorldData`, one line). There are no descriptions, so the input stays small and gives model-written text less room to inject instructions.

```
Region: {region name} ({biome})
Dominant faction: {faction, or: unknown}.
Threats: {threat; threat; ..., or: none}.
Places:
- {place name} ({terrain}, {hub|safe|ordinary})
- ...
Hubs: {hub name; hub name, or: none}.
Families: {family count}.
Feud: {feud count} families.

Write the creature families of this region.
```

Rules for the filled parts:
- **Places.** Every place of the region, the arrival point first, then the new places in the order they were written. The Edge Beyond doorway is left out.
  - The flag is `hub` for a hub. It is `safe` for a non-hub safe place, and `ordinary` for every other place.
  - Flags are read after the server's shape rules ran (D-05 host floor), so they match what players see.
- **Hubs.** Hub names joined with `; `, or `none`.
- **Families.** `{family count}` is `familyCountFor(real place count)` as a word (`eight` to `fifteen` for 6 to 10 places; 12/13/15 for 8/9/10). It is no longer the planned count of five places.
- **Feud.** Unchanged rules: `two` or `three`, the server's seeded pick (D-70). The no-feud variant (D-71) reads exactly `Feud: none.`
- The `Dominant faction` and `Threats` lines use the approved wording of the region_economy message (51.3.1.1 B2a). The `Families` and `Feud` lines are the approved Revision 2 lines, moved unchanged.

Rendered example, 9 places, one hub, a feud (made-up values):

```
Region: Kesterlane Basin (coastal)
Dominant faction: The Brine Wardens.
Threats: Crust sickness in the low pans; Sentinels that wake at high tide.
Places:
- Mother Pan Flats (swamp, ordinary)
- Saltgate (town, hub)
- The Salt Stair (mountains, ordinary)
- Mother Pan Undercroft (dungeon, ordinary)
- Tollman Causeway (plains, ordinary)
- The Warden Post (plains, safe)
- Reedmarrow Fen (swamp, ordinary)
- Glasswort Shallows (swamp, ordinary)
- The Drowned Orchard (woods, ordinary)
Hubs: Saltgate.
Families: thirteen.
Feud: two families.

Write the creature families of this region.
```

The same region with no feud: identical except for the Feud line, which reads:

```
Feud: none.
```

**What changed vs approved:**
- The whole message is new.
- Its `Region:` line matches the approved 2a line.
- `Dominant faction:` and `Threats:` reuse the approved B2a lines.
- `Families:` and `Feud:` (with `Feud: none.`) are the approved R2-A2 lines, moved unchanged.
- The `Places:` list, the 2b `Hubs:` line and the last line `Write the creature families of this region.` are new wording. The 2b `Hubs:` line lists names, while the 2a Hubs line gives a count.

## 5. Families-retry line, creation console only (D-18)

With the crossing hold (section 7), no player is ever inside a region whose families failed, so the D-09 "places stay empty" line is gone. Travellers at the crossing get the held line 7d. This line is still needed for one case: a brand-new character who waits in creation for the first region (D-17), when that region's families call fails. It goes to the creation console through the same path a failed first region uses today (`failWorldFill`'s creation branch, one Keeper narration segment). As with the FILL_ERROR line, the stored public `errorMessage` is the part before ` Type [explore] to try again.`

Today's approved FILL_ERROR line, for voice:
`The Keeper loses the thread of the rest of the map. What he has already shown you will hold. Type [explore] to try again.`

**Recommended:**

```
The Keeper loses the thread of the creatures that roam this region, and your first steps into it must wait. Type [explore] to try again.
```

**Alternative:**

```
The land is remembered, but not yet what lives in it. Type [explore] to try again.
```

Both lines keep the FILL_ERROR form and its `Type [explore] to try again.` ending. They do not say "what he has shown you will hold", because a character waiting in creation has not been shown the region yet. They use no pronoun for any creature.

The other `[explore]` replies need no new wording. They reuse today's approved lines:
- **Retry started:** `WORLD_FILL_RETRY_LINE`, "The Keeper squints at the half-remembered land and tries again...".

## 7. Crossing hold lines (D-15 to D-18)

The crossing is the uncharted Edge Beyond place the traveller stepped onto. Stage 1 turns it into `The Passage to {region name}`. Travel from the crossing into the new region is refused until the state is COMPLETE, that is, until places and families are both in. Travel back toward the old region stays open. Everyone at the crossing is held, not only the player who triggered generation.

Voice: a second-person narrator, plain, in the same register as today's generation lines. All lines are fixed text. Only 7c fills in a value, `{region name}`, a stored name passed through `w()`. No line uses a pronoun after it.

### 7a. Arriving at the crossing as generation starts

The line goes to the traveller who triggered generation. It also goes to anyone else who arrives at a crossing while the hold is on (proposed; today the code posts nothing to them).

Owner's idea: "You are preparing to travel into an unknown region."

**Recommended:**

```
You are preparing to travel into an unknown region. Out past the edge of the map, a storm is gathering on the horizon.
```

**Alternative:**

```
You stand at the edge of the known world, preparing to travel into an unknown region. The sky beyond is darkening.
```

**The existing line:** `helpers/travel.ts` posts `The edges of reality shimmer around you. The world pauses, as if remembering something it had forgotten...` when generation starts. **Recommendation: replace it** with 7a at the travel trigger.
- Both lines say the same moment, and the shimmer line promises nothing about the wait.
- The same shimmer text also lives in `STARTER_RETRY_MESSAGES.started` and in the `[explore]` retry at an uncharted place (`reducers/intent.ts`). Those are retry replies. They stay as they are.
- If the owner prefers to merge instead, the merged text is the shimmer line followed by 7a, as two sentences in one line.

### 7b. Refused: travel from the crossing into the region before it is done

The player gets this when trying to travel from the crossing into the new region while the state is GENERATING, FILLING or FILLING_FAMILIES.

Owner's idea: a large storm brews on the horizon and should pass soon, making travel to this unknown place possible.

**Recommended:**

```
A great storm brews on the horizon, and the way ahead vanishes into it. It should pass soon, and then the unknown land beyond will open to you. Try [travel] again in a moment.
```

**Alternative:**

```
Thunderheads pile up over the land ahead and the road disappears into the rain. Nobody crosses in that. It should blow over soon; try [travel] again in a moment.
```

`Try [travel] again in a moment.` reuses the ending of the approved pending hint (`REGION_FILL_PENDING_HINT`).

### 7c. Done: travel opens

When the families land (state COMPLETE), this goes to everyone held at the crossing and to the player who triggered generation, wherever that player is.

**Recommended:**

```
The storm on the horizon breaks and rolls away. Beyond it lies {region name}, and the way in is open. Try [travel] to cross.
```

**Alternative:**

```
The sky over the crossing clears. {region name} waits beyond, as if it had always been there. Try [travel] to go in.
```

For these players, 7c replaces today's completion line `The rest of {region name} settles into place. Try [travel] to see where the roads lead.` (`worldFillCompleteLine`).

### 7d. Held after a failure: the storm will not pass

This goes to everyone at the crossing while the region's families are in their error state (D-18). It replaces section 5's line for travellers, and it is also the 7b refusal while the error lasts. `[explore]` at the crossing re-enqueues only the families call.

**Recommended:**

```
The storm on the horizon does not pass. It hangs over the land ahead and shows no sign of moving. Type [explore] to try again.
```

**Alternative:**

```
The storm settles in over the land ahead and will not lift on its own. Type [explore] to try again.
```

The same line fits a places failure (FILL_ERROR), which also leaves the hold in place (D-15: closed until COMPLETE). In that case `[explore]` re-runs 2a as today. Proposed: use 7d for both errors at the crossing.

### 7e. Creation console (D-17: a new character waits until places and families are in)

**Indicators.** The creation console already shows the indicator pool for each route in `LLM_CREATION_CONSOLE_ROUTES` (`world_gen_start`, `world_gen`). Adding `world_gen_families` there (section 6) keeps a progress line on screen for the whole wait. Each next job is enqueued in the same transaction that applies the one before it, so there is no gap with no active job. No new indicator line is needed.

**One posted line needs new wording:** the stage-1 milestone line. Today `WORLD_START_MILESTONE_LINE` reads `The Keeper clears his throat. This ground will do; the rest of the region is still being remembered.` It is posted when stage 1 lands. Under the hold it is wrong for both audiences: a new character has not been placed yet, and a traveller is still at the crossing.

**Recommended** (to the creation console when stage 1 lands):

```
The Keeper clears his throat. A region is taking shape around the place you will first stand; its roads and its creatures are still being remembered.
```

**Alternative:**

```
The Keeper clears his throat. The ground is chosen, but its roads and its creatures are still being remembered.
```

For a traveller at the crossing, the recommendation is to post no milestone line, because 7a already set the expectation. If the owner wants one, the Alternative above fits there too.

**No new wording for placement.** When the families land, the new character is placed at the arrival point with today's stage-1 arrival message, which moves to the 2b apply. Its last sentence, `The roads out are still being remembered.`, is no longer true at that point, so it becomes the existing approved ending `Try [look] to examine your surroundings, or [travel] to move.` (the starter-reuse arrival uses that ending today).

### Existing lines that the hold makes wrong (for the planner; nothing new to approve)

- `DISCOVERY_TEMPLATES` (`helpers/world_gen.ts`) is posted when stage 1 lands. Its third template, `You step into {regionName}. It has always been here. You were simply the first to notice.`, is false while the traveller waits at the crossing. Proposed: post the discovery line with 7c instead of at stage 1. It would then be true when it is read.
- The passage description written at stage 1 (`The mists have parted. What was once the edge of the known world is now a well-trodden path between regions. ...`) shows on `[look]` at the crossing during the storm. It is mildly at odds with the storm, but it is left as is unless the owner wants it changed.
- For a character waiting in creation, the creation-console FILL_ERROR line (`... What he has already shown you will hold. ...`) is wrong too, since nothing has been shown yet. This is today's approved text. Flagged only.
