# Phase 51.3.1.2 Plan 06: Region hold at the crossing Summary

Travellers now wait at the crossing until the new region's generation state is COMPLETE. Entry into a region still being made is refused with the owner's 7b line, and entry after a failure with 7d. Arrival at a held crossing posts 7a (the owner's Alternative) or 7d to every traveller of the trip. A held crossing never collapses, and the passage sweep leaves it and the people in it alone.

## What changed

- **helpers/region_hold.ts** (new): the four lines plus the frozen step sets IN_PROGRESS (PENDING, GENERATING, FILLING, FILLING_FAMILIES) and FAILED (FILL_ERROR, FAMILIES_ERROR).
  - `regionHoldState(tx, regionId)`: iterates world_gen_state by generatedRegionId and returns 'held', 'held_failed' or 'open'.
  - `crossingHoldState(tx, locationId)`: reads by_source_location and returns 'held', 'held_failed' or null.
  - `travelHoldRefusal(tx, from, to)`: returns null for a trip inside one region or into an open region, else 7b or 7d.
  - The module imports only `../data/llm_layers`, nothing from world_gen or passages.
- **helpers/travel.ts**:
  - The `travelHoldRefusal` check runs after the connection check and isCrossRegion, before party collection, stamina, cooldowns or the leave roll.
  - At the end of performTravel, `crossingHoldState(location.id)` posts 7a or 7d ('system') once to each traveller.
  - The shimmer start line is no longer posted by the uncharted trigger. Its refusal path is unchanged: the state becomes ERROR, so no hold line follows. `reducers/intent.ts` [explore] retries keep the shimmer line and were not touched.
- **helpers/passages.ts**:
  - `collapsePassageIfEmpty` returns false while `crossingHoldState` is not null.
  - `sweepPassages` skips a held crossing before moving anyone.
  - The header comment states the D-15 rule.
