# Phase 51.3.1.2 Plan 11: Two-Stage Region Fill Wired Into the Apply Pipeline Summary

A region is now filled in two paid calls through the real job pipeline. The places reply (world_gen, 2a) writes the places and queues the families job in the same transaction. The families reply (world_gen_families, 2b) writes the families and completes the region through finishRegionFill: COMPLETE, then 7c, then the discovery line, then the economy. Every way 2b can fail, including a lost job found by the sweeper, ends in FAMILIES_ERROR with the places kept. It never goes to FILL_ERROR.

## What was built

### Task 1: the applies, the failure dispatch and the flow tests

`helpers/llm_apply.ts`:

**`applyWorldFillResult`.** It keeps the FILLING guard and the parse, locations, region and start checks; each of those fails to failWorldFill.
- **A reply with a families array** (legacy, queued before the publish) goes through `writeRegionFill`, then `finishRegionFill`.
- **Any other reply is stage 2a:**
  - The count is `storedPlaceCount(context)`: the stored `input.placeCount` when it is a whole number in 8..10. When it is, the apply passes `placeCountFor(region.id)`; when it is not, it passes null.
  - Then `writeRegionPlaces` runs. A `too_few` result becomes failWorldFill with WORLD_FILL_FAILED_MESSAGE.
  - On success, `startWorldFamilies(ctx, <state read again>)` runs inside try/catch. The catch logs only the error name and calls `failWorldFamilies(..., WORLD_FAMILIES_FAILED_MESSAGE)`.
- The inline COMPLETE, the economy start and `worldFillCompleteLine` are gone from the apply.

**New `applyWorldFamiliesResult(ctx, job, resultText)`:**
- It guards on FILLING_FAMILIES.
- A parse failure, a missing region, or a not-ok `writeRegionFamilies(..., { ruleOnlyAllowed: false })` (malformed or empty) goes to `failWorldFamilies` with WORLD_FAMILIES_FAILED_MESSAGE.
- Otherwise it calls `finishRegionFill`.
- `applyLlmResult` dispatches it for `world_gen_families`.

**`applyLlmFailure`:** new `world_gen_families` branch. A FILLING_FAMILIES state goes to `failWorldFamilies`, with LLM_RESTING_LINE for halted/ceiling and WORLD_FAMILIES_FAILED_MESSAGE otherwise. Any other step is left alone.

**`applyWorldStartResult`:** the discovery and milestone private lines are posted only for a starter state (`sourceRegionId === 0n`). The World event line is unchanged.

**Imports:** the now-unused `startRegionEconomy` import was removed, because `noUnusedLocals` is on.

### Task 2: the sweeper, the drills and the three-call chain

`helpers/llm_sweeper.ts` `releaseStrandedLocks`:
- New `worldFamiliesHeld` set, filled from active world_gen_families jobs (`genStateIdOf`).
- A stale FILLING_FAMILIES state that no active families job holds goes to `failWorldFamilies(ctx, state, WORLD_FAMILIES_FAILED_MESSAGE)`.
- FILLING still goes to FILL_ERROR.
- ERROR, FILL_ERROR, FAMILIES_ERROR, HELD and COMPLETE are never touched. The doc comment says so.

## Tests

### New file: `world_gen_flow.test.ts` (34 tests, strict mock, recording schema, no fetch)

**Stage 2a:**
- Stored count 9 with 8 usable places: 9 charted places plus the doorway, no family, FILLING_FAMILIES, one pending world_gen_families job keyed worldGen(5), no economy job, no line.
- The server count wins over the stored count.
- Count 9 with 4 usable places: FILL_ERROR. No place, connection or reply NPC is added and no region facts are written. The only NPCs added are the rule hub services that failWorldFill places.
- No stored count, or one that is out of range or not a whole number, with 3 places: accepted, FILLING_FAMILIES.
- Malformed replies (unparseable text, no locations array, locations not an array, JSON null): FILL_ERROR, no rows written.
- Legacy families reply: COMPLETE, the families are written, 7c goes to the trigger and the crossing, the trigger also gets the discovery line, one region_economy job, no families job, and no old completion line.
- Chain throw (`db.llm_job` access throws): the apply does not throw, the places stay, FAMILIES_ERROR, and the log carries the error name but not its message.
- Refused enqueue (ceiling): FAMILIES_ERROR with the resting line, places kept.
- A player holding the most capped jobs still gets the 2b job.

**Stage 2b:**
- 9 places give 13 families, 3 to 5 creature pools at each host place, COMPLETE, 7c then the discovery line, and one economy job.
- 8, 9 and 10 places give 12, 13 and 15 families.

**2b failure matrix** (10 cases):
- the failed job: overloaded, halted, ceiling and apply_error codes;
- the bad replies: unparseable text, JSON null, no families array, families not an array, families that all fail validation, and an empty array.

Each case checks:
- the state is FAMILIES_ERROR and never FILL_ERROR;
- the place and connection rows are byte-identical;
- no family and no new job;
- 7d (or the resting line) goes to the trigger and the crossing, and to nobody elsewhere.

There are also two guard cases: a non-FILLING_FAMILIES state changes nothing, and a missing region is FAMILIES_ERROR.

**Idempotency:** a second 2a apply after FILLING_FAMILIES and a second 2b apply after COMPLETE leave the database snapshot identical.

**SC4:** a COMPLETE 4-place region gets the full 2a and 2b. Then a second region gets its full 2a and 2b (state 6, another player). Afterwards every old-region row is byte-identical: region, places, connections, NPCs, families, members, templates, links, relations, pools and pool levels.

**Stage 1 for a traveller:**
- The region, arrival point, first NPC and passage are written and 2a starts.
- No milestone line and no discovery line; the World event line is unchanged.
- A starter state keeps narrative, discovery and milestone.
