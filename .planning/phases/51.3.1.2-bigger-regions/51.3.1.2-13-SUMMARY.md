# Phase 51.3.1.2 Plan 13: New characters wait in creation Summary

A new character now stays in the creation console (location 0) until his first region has both its places and its families. He then appears at the arrival point with the old stage-1 arrival message, which ends with the approved "Try [look] to examine your surroundings, or [travel] to move.", followed by the discovery line. A second character of the same race whose starter region is still being built waits too (state HELD) and is placed at the region's home place when it completes. While anyone waits, a failure reaches every waiting creation console, and [explore] from a HELD console retries only the failed stage.

## Gate

`grep -qE "^Status: APPROVED [0-9]{4}-[0-9]{2}-[0-9]{2}"` on the draft passed. The 7e text comes from `node scripts/llm/prompt_draft.mjs chosen` (key 7e, Recommended):
`The Keeper clears his throat. A region is taking shape around the place you will first stand; its roads and its creatures are still being remembered.`

## What changed

### Task 1: stage 1 no longer places; placement at completion
- `WORLD_START_MILESTONE_LINE` holds the 7e text. It is posted only to a new character's creation console:
  - at stage 1 (`applyWorldStartResult`, a starter state whose character is at location 0);
  - on a HELD reuse (Task 2).
- `applyWorldStartResult` no longer places the character, writes no arrival message and posts no private discovery or milestone line for anyone. Unused imports were removed (`pickDiscoveryMessage`, `markLocationVisited`, `ensurePoolsForLocation`, `npcGender`/`npcNoticeLine`).
- `finishRegionFill` calls `placeWaitingCharacter` for a starter state (sourceLocationId 0n). It works only on a character still at location 0, and it:
  - moves him to the arrival point (`findRegionStart`) and sets his bind point there;
  - calls `markLocationVisited` with no origin, then `ensurePoolsForLocation`;
  - writes the arrival message (Keeper segments): `You open your eyes in {arrival}, {region}.`, then the stored stage-1 `regionDescription` (fallback `A {biome} region.`), then the NPC notice, then the approved ending;
  - posts the discovery line.
- The description is read back by `storedRegionDescription`:
  - it builds the key `buildDedupeKey(state.playerId, 'world_gen_start', SOURCE_KEYS.worldGen(state.id))`;
  - it takes the newest `completed` job from `llm_job.by_dedupe_key`;
  - it never throws.
- A starter character the old stage-1 code already placed (in flight at the publish) is skipped: no second placement, no arrival message (T-51.3.1.2-44).
- `worldFillCompleteLine` was deleted; nothing called it after Plan 11.

### Task 2: HELD reuse, placement, failure lines, retry
- `reuseStarterRegion` now returns `'placed' | 'held' | false`. Its pieces are factored into `starterRegionFor`, `regionHomePlace` and `placeAtHome` (the old placement and reuse arrival message, unchanged).
  - When `regionHoldState(region)` is not `'open'`, `holdNewCharacter` sets the state to HELD with the region and posts 7e to the creation console.
  - When the region has already failed, `holdNewCharacter` also posts that stage's existing failure line.
  - `startWorldGeneration` returns `'held'` for this case.
- `finishRegionFill` calls `placeHeldCharacters`. Every HELD state of the region whose character is at location 0 is placed at the home place with the reuse arrival message and becomes COMPLETE. A HELD state whose character is gone or already placed just becomes COMPLETE.
- `postFillFailure` also posts the creation line to the player of every HELD state whose character still waits. Each player is told once.
- `retryStarterWorldGen` sends a HELD newest state to `retryHeldStarter`, which looks at the region's build states (`regionBuildStates`: non-HELD states, newest first):
  - one still running: `'busy'`;
  - the newest failed one (FILL_ERROR or FAMILIES_ERROR): `playerId` is handed to the asker, the characterId is kept, and only the failed stage is restarted (`'fill_started'`, or `'refused'`);
  - otherwise the region is whole: he is placed now (`'reused'`).
- `creation.ts`: comments only. `'held'` posts nothing extra because 7e was already posted. The finalize call still ignores the outcome.
- No change was needed for HELD in the sweeper, `regionFillHint`, `retryWorldFill` or `regionHoldState`. All of them already ignore HELD, and tests now pin that.

## Known limitations
- When a HELD player retries, the generating state's `playerId` becomes the asker's (as the plan says). A later failure of that state then reaches the asker's console but not the first player's, whose identity is no longer on the state. The first player can still [explore], and completion still places him (placement goes by character).
- The creation-console progress indicator shows the player's own active jobs. A HELD player has none, so his console shows the 7e line without a moving progress line until he is placed. The plan said no client change is needed; this is flagged only.
- A new character created while another character's starter region is still GENERATING (no region row yet) generates his own region, as before.
