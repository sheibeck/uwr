### Note for the coordinator: economy start only at COMPLETE

The region economy starts only at COMPLETE (finishRegionFill), not also when 2b ends in FAMILIES_ERROR (CONTEXT's Claude's Discretion had recommended both). Reason: D-15 and D-16 hold every traveller outside the region until COMPLETE, so resources during a families error would serve nobody, and an economy designed with zero families would send all 12-15 families to late jobs.
