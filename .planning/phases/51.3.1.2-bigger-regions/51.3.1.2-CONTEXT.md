# Phase 51.3.1.2: Bigger Regions - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Ready for planning (research exists: `51.3.1.2-RESEARCH.md`)

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A newly generated region has 8-10 places instead of 3-5. Generation is split across AI calls so no reply runs past its budget. Families scale with the real region size (12-15 for 8-10 places, D-66/D-67 of 51.3.1.1), and the region economy grows to match. Existing regions are not touched; the owner will regenerate the world from scratch once all systems exist.

Typed sub-regions, hidden places and sub-locations (sewers, tower floors) stay in backlog 999.26. Admin dials for the new numbers come in Phase 52.5.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

All answers below are the owner's, given 2026-10-09 in the smart discuss over the research's Owner Questions Q1-Q10.

### Generation split (Q1, Q2)
- **D-01:** **Two stages after stage 1.** Stage 1 (`world_gen_start`) is unchanged. Stage 2a keeps the route name `world_gen` and writes places and people: region facts, the new locations, hub marks, place words and NPCs. Stage 2b is a new cap-exempt route `world_gen_families` that writes the creature families (names, members, fit, relations, histories, feud marks). 2b is told the real places and hubs and the real family count. Each call is counted in the daily ceiling and the player budget like `world_gen`, and none auto-retries.
- **D-02:** **SUPERSEDED by D-15..D-18 (owner, 2026-10-09, same session).** Was: the region opens when the places land (2a). Exits work after 2a; creatures arrive when 2b lands (estimated 40-50 s later). Until then a place simply has no creatures. When 2b lands, its creature pools are seeded (find-or-create), including at places players already visited.

### Hold travellers until the region is fleshed out (owner, 2026-10-09, after the discuss)
- **D-15:** **Nobody enters a new region until its places AND families are in.** Owner: "Let's keep anyone in the transition location until a new region is fully fleshed out. We can give messages like 'You are preparing to travel into an unknown region.' Or 'A large [storm] brews on the horizon, it should pass soon making travel to this unknown place possible'. Some in game reason to prevent travel until it's done". The transition location is the crossing (the uncharted Edge Beyond place the traveller stepped onto, which stage 1 turns into a passage). Travel from the crossing into the new region is refused with an in-world reason (weather on the horizon or similar) until the state is COMPLETE; travel back toward the old region stays open. Everyone at the crossing is held, not only the player who triggered it. The hold lines are new player text and go through the owner's wording approval (proposed with the prompt draft).
- **D-16:** **"Fully fleshed out" = places and families.** Travel opens when 2b lands. The economy job (gatherables, recipes, loot, late-family loot jobs) keeps filling in the background afterwards.
- **D-17:** **New characters wait in creation.** A brand-new character's first region: the creation console keeps showing its progress lines until places and families are in, then the character is placed at the arrival point (today the character is placed after stage 1).
- **D-18:** **A families failure keeps the hold.** While the families are in their error state (D-08), travellers stay at the crossing; the hold line changes to say the weather has not passed, naming `[explore]` to try again, and `[explore]` at the crossing re-enqueues only 2b. For a new character in creation, the creation console shows the same retry path (planner: reuse how creation handles a stage-1 failure today). D-09's "no creatures until retry" no longer arises for players, because nobody is inside the region before families land.

### Size and shape (Q3, Q4, Q5)
- **D-03:** **The server rolls 8-10 places** (seeded, arrival point included; the Edge Beyond doorway does not count). The 2a user message tells the AI the exact number. Extra places are trimmed; a short reply is accepted down to a floor of 6 places; below the floor the fill fails (FILL_ERROR, `[explore]` retries 2a as today). Range and floor are named constants (dials in 52.5).
- **D-04:** **Hop gradient for levels.** The server sets each new place's level offset by hop distance from the arrival point: +1 per 2 hops, at most +2 (named constants). The AI no longer writes `levelOffset` (removed from the 2a reply and wording). The starter region uses the same gradient (the flatter-starter variant was not chosen).
- **D-05:** **Deliberate region shape.** The onward Edge Beyond doorway hangs off the place farthest from the arrival point by hops (prefer non-safe, non-hub; ties by lowest id). At most 4 exits per place, trimming only edges that are not bridges (the arrival point's passage back to the source region does not count). A place the AI left unreachable is linked to the least-connected reached place (ties by hop distance, then id), not always the arrival point. At least 4 places can host creatures (the server flips the farthest non-hub safe places to unsafe if needed). All are named constants and pure, tested rules.

### People and hubs (Q6, Q7)
- **D-06:** **3-5 NPCs per region besides the hub services** (each hub's vendor and banker are on top). This changes the owner's D-65 wording ("1-3 more") for new regions; the new wording is approved with the prompt draft.
- **D-07:** **Hub rolls unchanged** (D-62: most regions one hub, a second rare at 10%, deep regions often none). The feel of hub distance in a 9-place region is judged at the milestone UAT.

### Families call failure (Q9, follow-up)
- **D-08:** **A failed families call gets its own retry, not a rule fill.** When 2b fails (call failure, truncation, malformed reply, refused enqueue, apply error after its attempts, or a stranded job found by the sweeper), the region gets a separate families-error state (for example step `FAMILIES_ERROR`). This overrides the roadmap's "fails over to rules as today" for 2b only; 2a failures still use FILL_ERROR and its retry.
- **D-09:** **No creatures until someone retries.** While the families are in error the places stay creature-free and show the `[explore]` retry line. `[explore]` at any place of that region re-enqueues only the families call (2b), never 2a. 2b must never set FILL_ERROR, which would make `[explore]` re-run 2a and duplicate places. The retry line is new player text and goes through the owner's wording approval.

### Economy (Q8)
- **D-10:** **Medium economy for regions of 8+ places:** 5 gatherables and 5 recipes (today `small`, 3 and 3). The approved region_economy wording already supports `medium`; the `region_economy` budget grows (research suggests about 6144, `insufficient_data` until measured).
- **D-11:** **One late loot call per family past the design cap.** The economy call still designs at most 7 families (`ECONOMY_DESIGN_FAMILIES_MAX`). Each remaining family (5-8 in a big region) gets its own late-family loot job with the already-approved B2b wording (about $0.005 each). Rule loot only if that job fails.

### Measurement (Q10)
- **D-12:** **Ship with estimated budgets** (2a about 4096, 2b about 7168, both `insufficient_data`), guarded by a free token-estimate test on maximal schema-valid fixtures (at most 80% of each budget). The owner runs one paid region at the milestone-end UAT (about $0.15) and the budgets are set from it.

### Carried constraints
- **D-13:** Every new or changed prompt's exact wording is approved by the owner before it ships and pinned by tests: the revised 2a block (counts, no families or feud paragraphs, no levelOffset, 3-5 NPCs), the new 2b block (families and feud paragraphs moved), the 2a `Places:` line, the 2b user message (place list, hubs, `Families:`, `Feud:`), the families-retry line and any new indicator lines. Plan 1 is a PROMPT-DRAFT with a `Status:` line; the wiring plan starts with a grep for `Status: APPROVED`.
- **D-14:** Additive schema only (the step is a plain string, so no schema change is expected); local publish only with the key-108 check before and after, never `--clear-database`; no paid calls in tests (the owner's local `aiEnabled` is ON); unit tests in every plan; server is the source of truth; Keeper pronouns; never the banned word for World events.

### Claude's Discretion
- How the 2b prompt names places: research recommends name, terrain and flags (safe, hub, ordinary) per place plus the hub names, no descriptions (smaller input, less second-order injection surface). Every model-written value from 2a passes through the existing sanitizer before it enters the 2b prompt.
- Ordering of the economy chain around D-08/D-09: recommended that the economy job runs once 2b completes, and also when 2b ends in its error state (region-level gatherables and recipes with no families), so a region waiting for a retry still has resources; families that land later (retry) then get late-family loot jobs as in D-11.
- In-flight compatibility at publish: a `world_gen` job queued before the publish still carries a `families` array; keep the legacy one-reply branch so it completes directly.
- Indicator lines for the new route: the two family-flavoured `world_gen` pool lines may move to the new route (still owner-reviewed).

</decisions>

## Specific Ideas

- Owner, 2026-10-08: "Regions needs to be bigger than 3-5. we decided on max 10, but that means we should be looking at 8-10 locations. That still leaves room to go above that for hidden locations and sub-locations within dungeons, towers, cities (like a sewer, etc)".
- Owner, 2026-10-08: "We'll eventually repopulate the whole world from scratch once all the systems are in place. So, no need to fix/grow existing regions."
- Estimated cost about $0.12-0.14 per region plus the late loot calls (D-11, about $0.03-0.04), about 2x today; estimates only until the D-12 measurement.
