## Flagged assumptions (unresolved edge rows, 43-EDGE-COVERAGE.md)

- **[FLAGGED ASSUMPTION, LAT-03, unresolved edge row; confirm at verify]** If stage 2 of world generation fails, expires or is refused, the state is FILL_ERROR and the player keeps the stage-1 region: its start location (safe, bind stone, crafting), its first NPC, and the vendor/banker safety net added by failWorldFill. Nothing retries on its own. The next `explore` at the start location or at the passage retries stage 2 only (never stage 1). The sweeper turns a FILLING state with no active job for 60 s into FILL_ERROR, so the lock is released either way. Input is never locked during FILLING or FILL_ERROR (Plan 43-08). A FILL_ERROR region has no uncharted boundary yet, so exploring beyond it waits for a successful stage 2; the region itself stays playable.
