## Flagged assumptions (unresolved edge rows, 43-EDGE-COVERAGE.md)

- **[FLAGGED ASSUMPTION, LAT-02, unresolved edge row; confirm at verify]** Cache verification makes two identical-prefix calls per swept route, one after the other inside the 5-minute cache TTL, during the user-approved Run B, at the route's derived final effort and unmodified schema. A route whose stable prefix is under the model's 512-token minimum (judged when call 1 reports no cache write and no cache read and its total input tokens are under 512) is recorded as "not cacheable", not as failing; with the Keeper Bible at about 2.4K tokens no route is expected to fall under it. A swept route whose call 2 reads 0 cached tokens with a prefix at or above the minimum is a real failure: Plan 43-13 stops and reports it.
