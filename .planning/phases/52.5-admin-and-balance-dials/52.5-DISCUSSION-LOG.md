## Area 1: Which numbers, how much, how they combine (owner: Accept all)

| # | Question | Accepted answer |
|---|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Which enemy numbers get a dial? | Health, damage, armour and ability chance. Damage covers auto-attacks and abilities alike. XP and flee stay out (XP already scales with the level gap). |
| 2 | How far can each dial move? | Health, damage and armour 25-400%; ability chance 0-100% (today 50% is the default). Every dial starts at today's value. |
| 3 | How do layers combine? | They multiply (global x difficulty x region x enemy type), capped at 10-500% in total. Unlike `/economy`, where the most specific layer replaces the rest. |
| 4 | When does a change take effect? | For enemies that join a fight after the change; numbers lock at join, ability chance included. |

Owner question during Area 1: "Are we going to to individual enemies that show up in combat after we pull from a family? Or are we going to do numbers by family and role?" Answer given: by family and role; an individual enemy only gets the result (base stats for its role and level x global x difficulty x region x type x boss/named). The type layer is keyed by creature type now, with family and role keys once 51.3.1.1 exists. Owner then accepted Area 1 ("Accept all"), with the role layer coming with families.

## Area 2: Difficulty and region layers (owner: Accept all)

| # | Question | Accepted answer |
|---|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Whose level sets an enemy's difficulty band in a party? | The highest-level player in the fight when the enemy joins. |
| 2 | Which bands? | The 7 con colours players see (gray, light green, blue, white, yellow, orange, red), from one shared rule in `data/`; the server's `look` and enemy-list text move onto it. |
| 3 | Region layer key? | Each region by name, as `/economy region <name>`. |
| 4 | Starting values? | 100% everywhere. |
