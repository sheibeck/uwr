## Finding: the local AI economy switch was already on

At the end of Plan 09 the local database had no `economy_dials` row. After this publish it has one with `ai_enabled = true`. This plan did not create it:

- The publish migration was empty and the log shows only "Updated program" and "Database updated" at 19:01 (no table creation, and `init` does not re-run on an update).
- This plan called no reducer and sent no `/economy` command.
- The row can only have been written by an admin `/economy ai on` or `economy_set_ai_enabled` between Plan 09 and this plan (several other "Updated program" publishes happened in that window, at 16:14Z and 17:28Z, none by this plan).

Consequence: with the complete chain now live and the switch on, the next world generation fill, a new quest enemy type, or `/economy design <region>` will enqueue a paid `region_economy` job (budget phase_only; the kill switch, ceiling and ledger still apply). The plan's check "no row or aiEnabled false" therefore does NOT hold. Nothing was changed, because this plan may not call a reducer or touch the switch. The owner should confirm that the switch is meant to be on; `/economy ai off` turns it off.
