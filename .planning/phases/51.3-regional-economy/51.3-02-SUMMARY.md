## What was built

- **Names:** `cleanItemName` keeps `[A-Za-z' -]`, drops leading "scroll" words, keeps at most 4 words and 40 characters, and returns '' under 2 letters. `cleanDescription` removes `[ ] { } < >` and control characters and caps at 240 characters at a word boundary. `uniqueItemName` checks `RESERVED_ITEM_NAMES` (junk, materials, modifiers, reagents, essences, starter gear, basic resources) plus the caller's `isTaken`, case-insensitively.
- **Counts and handles:** `REGION_ECONOMY_COUNTS`, `GATHER_SLOTS`, `GATHER_WEIGHTS` (15/8/3), `REGION_ECONOMY_BIGINT_PATHS`, `gatherRef`/`enemyRef`/`dropRef`/`foreignRef`.
- **Recipes:** `recipeTierSlots`, `FOREIGN_REGIONS_BY_TIER`, `regionalRequirementPlan`, `MAX_RECIPE_REQUIREMENTS` (4), `slotForeignIndexes`.
- **Foreign pick:** `orderForeignRegions` (neighbors first, then rotation by region id) and `foreignOffer` (4 per region, rarest first). Neither depends on input order.
- **Item numbers:** `RARITY_STAT_PCT` (100/110/125/145/170), `scaleStat`, `materialItemTier`, and the builders.
