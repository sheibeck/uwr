# Phase 51.3 Plan 07: Regional gathering and the vendor region rule Summary

**A region's AI gatherables now grow only in that region on matching terrain (resolved by template id, weights 15/8/3), the gather dial scales every `finish_gather` yield with a floor of 1, and vendors stock only their own region's common and uncommon gather or drop materials, so cross-region recipes require travel.**

## Accomplishments

- `helpers/regional_gather.ts` `regionalGatherEntries`: reads `economy_item.by_region`, keeps role `'gather'` on the lowercased terrain, filters by time of day (a row's `'any'` always stays), resolves the template by `itemTemplateId` (a missing template is skipped), weighs by `GATHER_WEIGHTS` and sorts by template id.
- `helpers/location.ts`: 7 changed lines. `getGatherableResourceTemplates(..., regionId?)` appends the regional entries after today's; `spawnResourceNode` passes `location.regionId`. The roll is unchanged, so a region with no economy keeps today's distribution. The enemy-spawn functions (quick 261008-ag8) and the day/night constants were not touched.
- `finish_gather`: after the base quantity of a non-reagent node, `quantity = gatherYield(quantity, loadEffectiveDials(ctx, location.regionId).gatherRatePct)`; reagents stay at exactly 1; the module-identity guard is still the first statement.
- `vendor_stock.ts`: `originAllows` (own region, role gather or drop, rarity common or uncommon; anything else with an origin is refused) and the `selectBaseStock` filter. `isBaseStockCandidate` keeps its signature.
- `index.ts`: `restock_vendors` builds `origins` once per tick from `economy_item` and `restockVendor` passes `originOf` and `vendorRegionId: location?.regionId`.
