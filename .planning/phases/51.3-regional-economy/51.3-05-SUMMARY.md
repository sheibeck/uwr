# Phase 51.3 Plan 05: Victory loot rewrite Summary

**Kills drop loot again: each combat_enemy row rolls from its own seed through `helpers/loot.ts` (AI loot table or rule-based fallback, every roll on its own ROLL_INDEX, dials read once per victory), gold from all enemies is summed and written once, and legendary needs a boss or a fight-exact named foe.**

## Performance

- Duration: about 20 min (10:17 to 10:37)
- Tasks: 2 of 2
- Files: 3 created, 1 modified

## Accomplishments

- `helpers/loot.ts`:
  - `buildVictoryLootContext(ctx, combat, participants)` reads, once per victory: location, regionId, danger, zoneTier, `loadEffectiveDials(ctx, regionId)`, `loadItemPins(ctx)`, the fight-exact named set, the junk / MATERIAL_DEFS drop / own-region material pools, the gear pool, the essence and modifier maps, and the region's scroll recipes.
  - `fightNamedTemplateIds` counts a named_enemy row only for a participant, dead, at the fight location, with `lastKilledAt >= combat.createdAt`.
  - `rollEnemyLoot` makes the common picks, then gear, essence, modifier and scroll. Commons come from the AI non-gear entries or `fallbackCommonPool`. Gear comes from the AI gear entries plus the level-band fallback gear. Rarity uses `rollRarity(rarityMix(...))`, then `jewelryFloor`. Craft quality and affixes use independent `economyRoll` bases. The scroll drop is 10% times the drop rate, bosses and named foes only.
  - `rollEnemyGold` = `goldReward(creatureProfile(type), lootLevelOf(...), lootSeed, dials.goldPct)`.
- `handleVictory` walks the enemy rows in id order per participant and inserts one combat_loot row per rolled item (same columns as before). It sums gold, applies the goldFindBonus perk once, and updates gold once from a fresh `character.id.find`. The announcement, combat_result and XP blocks are unchanged.
- The three research bugs are fixed and covered by tests: identical rolls (per-row seed), correlated rolls (separate indexes) and lost gold (summed and written once).

## Decisions Made

See key-decisions in the frontmatter. In addition:
- The gear band is `levelBand(level)` (level - 1 .. level + 1), as the plan says. The old rule had no lower bound (`requiredLevel <= level + 1`).
- Name lookups (junk, materials, essences, reagents) take the lowest template id per name, from one sorted scan of item_template.

## Deviations from Plan

None that change behavior. Two small additions:
- **[Rule 2] `LootItem.kind`**: an extra field on each rolled item so tests can tell commons from gear, essence, modifier and scroll. handleVictory ignores it.
- **[Rule 2] Scroll pins**: a scroll entry's weight also passes through `itemWeight(…, pin)`, per the SC5 rule that per-item pins apply to entry weights.
