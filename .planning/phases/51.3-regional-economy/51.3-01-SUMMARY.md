# Phase 51.3 Plan 01: Economy rules Summary

One pure module, `spacetimedb/src/data/economy_rules.ts`, now holds every runtime economy number, and at default dials its rarity mix equals today's `rollQualityTier` for levels 1-50 and dangers 100-1000.

## What was built

- **Dials (Task 1):** `DIAL_RANGES`, `DEFAULT_DIALS`, `clampDial` (never throws, reports `clamped`), `effectiveDials` (region replaces global, tier weights from the five global columns), `itemWeight` (item pin).
- **Rolls (Task 1):** `ROLL_INDEX` (unique fixed indexes), `economyRoll` (full 64-bit splitmix64, constants copied from `salvageRoll`), `rollBelow`, `lootSeed` (timestamp, character id, combat_enemy id), `regionTableSeed`.
- **Rarity (Task 2):** `baseMix` (parity with `rollQualityTier`), `shiftMix`, `rarityMix`, `rollRarity`, `jewelryFloor`. `AFFIX_COUNT_BY_QUALITY.legendary` is 3.
- **Amounts (Task 3):** `pickCount`, `gearChancePct`, `goldReward`, `gatherYield`, `scaledChancePct`, `zoneTierOf`, essence, modifier and scroll constants.
- **Profiles and tables (Task 3):** `CREATURE_PROFILES` (numbers confirmed from `git show 9ac55586^:spacetimedb/src/seeding/ensure_enemies.ts`), `canonicalCreature`, `creatureProfile`, `pickWeighted`, `pickWithoutReplacement`, `fallbackCommonPool`, `gearPoolWeight`, `aiLootTable`.

## The two loot bugs fixed at rule level

- **Correlated rolls:** the gear-chance roll and the rarity roll use different splitmix indexes. A test shows T1 uncommon stays near 5% among seeds where the gear roll passes.
- **Identical rolls:** `lootSeed` differs for two enemies of one template in a fight (different `combat_enemy` ids).
