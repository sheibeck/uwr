## Fixed Issues

### A CR-01: Floor division zeroed small tier, pin and scroll weights

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/data/economy_rules.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/loot.ts` (+ tests)
**Commit:** 189b3a07
**Applied fix:**
- `scaleWeights(weights, pcts)` multiplies each weight by its percent after dividing the percents by their common divisor. Ratios are exact, and default dials change no roll.
- `rarityMix` uses `scaleWeights`.
- `pinWeights` applies item pins to a whole pool (commons and gear).
- Scroll weights scale by tier dial times pin.
- `itemWeight` no longer divides; its result is on a x100 scale.
- Tests at 50% and 150%: T2 epic, T2 boss legendary, a legendary scroll and a pinned junk entry.

### A WR-01: The named boost applied to every enemy of the named template

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/loot.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat.ts` (+ tests)
**Commit:** ccc2616b
**Applied fix:**
- `fightNamedEnemyRowIds`: each fight-exact `named_enemy` row claims the lowest-id unclaimed `combat_enemy` row of its template.
- `handleVictory` passes the fight's rows to `buildVictoryLootContext`.
- `rollEnemyLoot` tests the row id.
- Companions never roll legendary or a scroll (tested).

### A WR-02: Salvage was a recipe-scroll faucet. Owner decision: salvage NEVER returns scrolls

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/reducers/items_crafting.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/data/crafting_rules.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/data/combat_scaling.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/salvage_result.test.ts`
**Commit:** 90013243
**Applied fix:**
- The INT salvage scroll roll is removed. Scrolls come only from bosses and named foes at the 10% chance. `SALVAGE_SCROLL_ROLL_INDEX` stays reserved.
- Tests prove that a scroll-roll hit, very high INT and a salvage with no components back grant no scroll.
- Stale roll-index comments refreshed (part of A IN-06).

### A WR-03: Crafted regional outputs were stamped common

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/reducers/items_crafting.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/regional_recipes.test.ts`
**Commit:** bc7c9077
**Applied fix:** `decorateCrafted` writes the output template's own rarity; an unknown value reads as common. Legendary and uncommon regional outputs keep their tier. Rule outputs stay common.

### A WR-04: Item pins were ignored by essence, reagent and gather rolls

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/data/economy_rules.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/loot.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/location.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/economy_admin_commands.ts` (+ tests)
**Commit:** 91cc153a
**Applied fix:** Pins now apply everywhere the drop rate applies.
- **Essence and reagent:** the template is decided first, then `pinnedChanceHit` scales its chance. With no pin this is the plain percent roll; with a pin it rolls in basis points, so 1% pinned at 50 is 0.5%.
- **Gather nodes:** `spawnResourceNode` scales each pool weight by its pin. A fully zero-pinned pool spawns nothing instead of dividing by zero.
- **Admin reply:** the pin reply now states which rolls a pin scales, and says that an item that never drops or grows is unaffected.

### A WR-05: The fallback gear pool held quest rewards and crafted outputs and weighed rare and epic as common

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/data/economy_rules.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/loot.ts` (+ tests)
**Commit:** 469fb096
**Applied fix:**
- **Excluded from the pool:**
  - every `recipe_template.outputTemplateId`;
  - every quest reward name. `isQuestRewardName` matches the forms `questRewardItemName` builds from each quest template's `rewardItemName`: the base, `<NPC>'s <base>`, `Quest-won <base>`, and numbered stems.
  - Seeded gear that only shares a name with a quest reward stays in the pool.
- **Weights by real rarity:** common 6, uncommon 3, rare 1, epic and legendary 0. Jewelry is 1 up to rare.

### A WR-06: The gather dial applied to nodes already found

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/location.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/items_gathering.ts` (+ tests)
**Commit:** 87365c4e
**Applied fix:**
- `spawnResourceNode` stores `gatherYield(roll, effective gather rate)` on the node.
- `finish_gather` yields `node.quantity`. Reagents still yield 1. A node with no stored quantity falls back to the plain roll.
- A dial or region override changed after the spawn keeps the stored yield (tested).

### B WR-01: Category repair kept the model's name ("Salted Wayfarer Jerky" as chest armor)

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/region_economy_validate.ts`, its test, and the new fixture `spacetimedb/src/helpers/__fixtures__/economy/kesterlane_live.reply.json` (job 8206's real reply)
**Commit:** d41579a3
**Applied fix:**
- The primary is chosen for the model's category: first among the listed locals, then among all of the region's locals.
- The category changes only when no local can serve it. In that case the recipe takes the rule name and the rule description.
- Live result: "Kesterlane Basin Jerkin" (armor, rule description). "Wickthread Sash" and "Orchard Glow Pendant" keep their names.

### B WR-02: The armor slot came from the recipe index ("Wickthread Sash" as legs)

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/data/economy_design_rules.ts` (+ test)
**Commit:** 7bf58ed5
**Applied fix:**
- `armorSlotFromName` takes the slot from the last armor word of the cleaned name, plural or singular:
  - chest: sash, robe, vest, jerkin, tunic, cuirass, coat, mantle, shirt, hauberk, breastplate, doublet;
  - legs: trousers, pants, leggings, greaves, breeches, kilt;
  - boots (the codebase's feet slot): boots, shoes, sandals, slippers, treads.
- The index rule applies only when no word matches.
- No schema or prompt change.

### Owner decision: repair job 8206's outputs in place

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/region_economy.ts` (`repairRegionEconomyOutputs`), `spacetimedb/src/helpers/economy_admin_commands.ts` (`/economy repair NAME`), `spacetimedb/src/reducers/economy.ts` (admin-only reducer `economy_repair_region({ regionId })`), plus the tests (`region_economy_repair.test.ts` and others)
**Commit:** 5fce5d90 (bindings regenerated in 87cc969c)
**Applied fix:**
- **Source of truth:** the repair re-validates the region's stored design with the current rules. That design is the request and reply of the `region_economy.jobId` llm_job, so no LLM call is made.
- **What it corrects, in place:** each `recipe:<n>` output's `item_template`, its `economy_item` kind and rarity, its `recipe_template` row (name, type, material, requirements) and its scroll name.
- **Ids never change,** so recipes, loot and bags keep pointing at the same templates.
- **Idempotent.** It refuses a region with no economy, an unfinished economy, or no stored design.
- **Proof:** a test shows the repaired rows equal what the fixed apply writes.

## Deferred (Info)

- **A IN-01** (an empty fallback gear pool above level 2 in a fresh world): a balance and design choice. Either widen the band when the banded pool is empty, or rely on AI gear. WR-05 makes the pool stricter, so a fresh world above level 2 now relies on AI gear or admin-made gear.
- **A IN-02** (full scans per victory): needs a cached id map or name index. WR-05 adds two more scans per victory, of `recipe_template` and `quest_template`. Both are small today; an index-backed lookup is the follow-up.
- **A IN-03** (research checks `learnBy` only): defense in depth, because the tier comes from validated slots. It is a one-line filter for a later pass.
- **A IN-04** (the AI gear entry is diluted and not level-checked): a loot balance change. It needs an owner decision on the AI gear share.
- **A IN-05** (player-sold listings bypass the vendor region rule): a design note. Enforcing it changes the sell flow.
- **B IN-01** (a caught throw in the fill hook can orphan a paid job): reordering pending and enqueue touches the spend path, and it is unlikely today.
- **B IN-03** (the local secondary is not checked against `SECONDARY_KINDS`): changes recipe composition for new designs. Left for the prompt or economy pass.
- **B IN-04** (enemy names from npc_conversation are not capped): needs a cap at enemy creation, which is outside the economy files.
- **B IN-05** (a late-creature job that fails has no retry): needs a new `/economy design` mode for complete regions.
