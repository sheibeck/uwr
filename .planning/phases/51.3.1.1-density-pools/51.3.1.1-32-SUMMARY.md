# Phase 51.3.1.1 Plan 32: Live-test fixes (D-73 rating, D-74 quest targets, NPC replies, golden) Summary

The place rating now reads level first with a crowd nudge (The Drowned Bell reads Risky for a level-3 player, not Deadly), a held kill quest gives its target a seeded 40% chance in every drawn slot and a kill quest is only created when its target lives in a pool the player can reach (its place recorded on the quest), NPC replies run at 1024 tokens with one automatic retry on truncation, see one line per nearby family from the pools, and get the owner-approved D-75 "no unfinished task" line; the golden tests are green.

## What was built

**Task 1: D-73 rating, NPC budget, golden.**
- `placeRating`: Safe, 'Danger unknown' and not-ready Unknown are unchanged. Present families are those with density level above 0; none gives Quiet. Otherwise the gap is the highest present lvHi minus the party's lowest level: Quiet at 0 or below, Risky at 1-2, Deadly at 3 or more. A crowd (any present family at level 3, or 3 or more present families) steps up once. A boss or named enemy steps up once more. Deadly caps.
- Constants in `DENSITY_RULES`: `RATING_GAP_QUIET_MAX 0`, `RATING_GAP_RISKY_MAX 2`, `RATING_CROWD_LEVEL 3`, `RATING_CROWD_FAMILIES 3`. The summed-score constants and the `WeightBand` interface are gone.
- **Owner case:** The Drowned Bell (densities 2, 1, 2, every lvHi 3, level-3 player) reads **Risky**: gap 0 is Quiet, and three living families make a crowd. At 2, 2, 2 it is also Risky. Two families at an even level read Quiet. A wiped-out third family does not count toward a crowd.
- `npc_conversation: entry('low', 1024, 30_000, 'insufficient_data')`, listed in `RESHAPED_ROUTES`.
- Golden: `GOLDEN_SHAPE_CHANGED_ROUTES = ['npc_conversation', 'combat_narration', 'world_gen_start', 'world_gen']` (frozen, documented). `WORLD_START` gained `startLocation.isSafe`. `WORLD_FILL` has `arrival`, location place words and hub marks, and two families (members, relations, history, inFeud); `enemies` is dropped. Both record replays still replay a non-zero number of items.
- UI-SPEC "### Rating rule" carries the superseded note.

**Task 2: D-74.**
- `questTargetHit(seed, slot)` rolls at `QUEST_TARGET_BASE + slot`, below 40 out of 100. `pickQuestTarget(seed, slot, targets)` rolls at `QUEST_TARGET_PICK_BASE + slot`.
- `activeKillTargets(ctx, roster, memberTemplateIds)` reads each distinct roster character's unfinished instances. It keeps kill and kill_loot quests (a missing questType reads kill) whose target is a member, and returns them unique and ascending.
- `drawGroup` takes an optional `roster`. Each slot whose roll hits becomes a held target at its place level. Size, roles and the other slots are unchanged, so with no roster or no target the draw is byte-identical to before.
- Every pool draw now passes its roster: pull (`pullFamilyFor` through `drawForPull`), travel leave (`travelingCharacters`), travel enter (`travellers`), the gather ambush and the quest-item ambush.
- `placesByHops` is a breadth-first walk inside the start region, each hop in ascending id. `questPoolPlace` gives the first hosting place on that walk.
- `resolveKillQuestTarget` tries, in order: a member name, a family name or noun (its front-liner), and a name linked in reach but not pooled (null). Otherwise it invents at `questPoolPlace`, or returns null. With no name it gives the front-liner of the first ordinary family pool.
- `offer_quest` kill/kill_loot resolves before the insert:
  - null: a module log line and the effect is skipped (the dialogue and other effects still apply);
  - invent: today's template and link, then `familyOfOne` and `startFamilyLoot`, skipped if no pool resulted;
  - in every created case `targetLocationId` is the pool's place.
  - boss_kill keeps today's post-insert path, minus the old kill-only family branch.

**Task 3: NPC replies and D-75.**
- `LLM_TRUNCATION_RETRY_ROUTES = ['npc_conversation']`.
- `shouldRetryTruncation(route, earlierStopReason, attempt)` requires all three: the route is listed, the earlier stop reason is not 'max_tokens', and attempts are left.
- In the `persistAttempt` billed branch, a truncated attempt is settled as before. Then:
  - `reserveLlmBudget` runs again: mode 'player' when the job has a budgetDay, else 'phase_only';
  - on ok: pending, errorCode 'truncated', the new reservation and budgetDay, nextAttemptAt now, a dispatch due now, the call logged at the settled cost, outcome 'retry';
  - on refusal: today's terminal failure.
- `getNearbyEnemyContext` reads `pool_level` (creature, level > 0): the NPC's place, then connected places in ascending id. It gives one entry per family, `{ name, level: lvLo, levelHi: lvHi, location }`, capped at `NPC_NEARBY_FAMILIES_MAX` 15.
- Volatile:
  - with `activeQuestFromThisNpc === false` the owner-approved line follows the quest-history sentence;
  - when quests are FULL only its first sentence is kept;
  - `true` keeps today's task line, and an absent field adds nothing;
  - nearby families render `level lo-hi` when levelHi is above level.
