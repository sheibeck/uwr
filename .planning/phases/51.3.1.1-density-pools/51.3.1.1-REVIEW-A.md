# Phase 51.3.1.1: Code Review Report, Part A (server pool systems)

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-09T06:50:53Z
**Depth:** deep (call chains traced into `reducers/combat.ts` clearCombatArtifacts/startCombat/start_combat, `helpers/world_gen.ts` placeRegionHubs, `helpers/llm_apply.ts` kill-quest creation, `helpers/group.ts` fightRoster)
**Files Reviewed:** 75 (30 production, 45 tests and fixtures, reviewed for coverage gaps)
**Status:** issues_found

## Summary

The pool engine holds up well where most of the risk was expected:

- **Single writer.** `setPoolCount` is the only `place_pool` update; the source pin in `pools.test.ts` enforces it. `pruneOrphanPools` and the passage rehome only delete rows.
- **Settling.** Lazy settling carries leftover time correctly, and the stored row is re-read before every write.
- **Kill depletion.** Kills deplete once, at the top of `clearCombatArtifacts`. That is the only place `combat_enemy` rows are deleted, so a second clear finds nothing to deplete.
- **`tick_pools`.** The module-identity guard comes first, the single reschedule comes before the work, and the reducer is in `scheduled_guard.integration.test.ts`.
- **Privacy.** `place_pool`, `pool_harvest`, `pool_state`, `pool_region` and `pool_rumor` are private. `pool_level` carries only the level 0-3, the level range and display strings. `my_harvest_caps` returns only `cappedUntilMicros` for capped rows.
- **Authorization.** `pull_family` and `gather_pool` check ownership first and refuse a missing, foreign or wrong-kind pool with the same "not here" text. Every refusal goes through `fail()`.
- **Travel.**
  - The leave roll comes after every validation and before any stamina or cooldown is spent.
  - The party rolls once, seeded by the leader, using the lowest traveller level (D-56).
  - The enter roll is skipped when a traveller has auto-joined a group fight.
- **Rules.**
  - D-55 is enforced at gather start.
  - D-72 yields 1 per gather, times the multiplier and the 51.3 dial; `gatherYield` floors at 1.
  - D-73 matches the owner's rule exactly.
  - D-74 targets are chosen only from the roster's open kill or kill_loot instances.
- **Determinism.** No `Math.random` or `Date` calls in any production file in scope.

One defect is a blocker:

- **The vacuum brings back families that are already wiped out.** This breaks D-37, and because rule families are all rivals, a contested place can never be cleared (CR-01).

Three warnings:

- The harvest cap is per character, so alts get around it.
- One region that keeps failing migration stops the whole pool tick, permanently.
- The public `pool_level` level range goes stale when a pooled family gains members.

Coverage gaps worth closing alongside the fixes:

- No test wipes a rival that was surged in by a vacuum (CR-01).
- No test uses two characters of one user against the harvest cap (WR-01).
- No test has a region whose migration fails deterministically (WR-02).
- No test refreshes the mirror after `createFamily` joins a member to an existing family (WR-03).

Deferred rows 1-3 are not raised again.

## Critical Issues

### CR-01: The vacuum brings back a wiped-out family and can make a place impossible to clear (breaks D-37, D-13, D-36)

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/pools.ts:479-495` (`runVacuum`)
**Issue:** `pooledHere` collects every creature pool at the place except the one just wiped. That includes pools that are themselves wiped out (count 0 and waiting out the 3-hour `WIPED_RESET_MICROS`). `here` keeps every candidate that has a pool here, whatever its count. When `here` is not empty, it is the only pick list, and the existing-pool branch sets the rival to `max(count, OVERRUN_SURGE_COUNT)`. So a family wiped minutes earlier jumps straight from 0 to 75 (Overrun), and `setPoolCount` clears its `wipedAtMicros`.

Traced with the fixture families (Goblins name Skitterers as a rival, so each is the other's vacuum candidate):

1. Wipe the Goblins: the Skitterers surge to 75.
2. Wipe the Skitterers: the candidates are the Goblins, who are pooled here at count 0. They surge back to 75 at once.

The Goblins return hours before their long reset. This contradicts D-37 ("A Wiped-out family regrows only on the long reset (hours)") and D-36 ("the wiped-out family can return slowly on the long reset").

The rule path (`ruleRelations`, `pool_migration.ts:233-244`) makes every rule family of a region a rival of every other. So at any place with two or more families, each wipe instantly brings back another family at Overrun, and the place never reaches the cleared state that D-13 ("a wiped-out place is safe to gather in") and the design brief ("arrive... to find it already cleared out by rivals") rely on. Every surge also raises the rival's home to Stable permanently, and both World event lines fire, so a hunted place cycles through takeover and surge announcements without end.

`pools.test.ts` covers a rival at count 25 surging, but never a wiped rival.
**Fix:** Only a living rival here can surge. A wiped one keeps its long reset, and the terrain fallback must never pick a family that already has a pool here:
```ts
const liveHere = candidates.filter((family: any) => {
  const row = pooledHere.get(family.id);
  return !!row && settlePool(ctx, row, now).pool.count > 0n;
});
const choices = liveHere.length > 0
  ? liveHere
  : candidates.filter((family: any) => !pooledHere.has(family.id) && fitsTerrain(family.fitTerrains, location.terrainType));
```
Add a test: wipe A, B surges; wipe B; A stays at 0 with its original `wipedAtMicros` until `WIPED_RESET_MICROS` has passed. Also decide whether hunters wiping the surged rival should run the vacuum at all.

## Warnings

### WR-01: The "per-player" harvest cap is per character, so alts get around it (D-27)

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/harvest.ts:28-76`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/pools.ts:97`, `spacetimedb/src/schema/tables.ts` (`PoolHarvest`, keyed by `characterId`)
**Issue:** D-27 caps each *player* so that "bots and hoarders" cannot strip a shared pool. `pool_harvest` rows, `harvestCappedFor` and `recordHarvest` are all keyed by `characterId`. A user can own any number of characters (`character.by_owner_user`, and no character limit was found). Rotating characters at one place gets around the cap entirely, which is exactly the abuse the cap exists to stop. The view `my_harvest_caps` and its comment say "per player".
**Fix:** Key the cap by the owning user. Add an additive `userId` column to `pool_harvest` (default 0n) and look rows up by `(character.ownerUserId, locationId)` in both `harvestCappedFor` and `recordHarvest`, with a `by_user` index. Alternatively, sum the user's characters' rows at the place. Keep the view's projection unchanged. Add a gather_pool integration test where a second character of the same user is refused once the first is capped.

### WR-02: One region that keeps failing migration stops the whole pool tick, permanently

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/pool_migration.ts:430-450`, `spacetimedb/src/index.ts:458-473`
**Issue:** When `migrateRegion` throws, `runMigrationStep` reschedules the same cursor `MIGRATION_CONTINUE_MICROS` (1 s) later, and retries forever. Nothing counts attempts or skips the region. While `pool_state.version < POOL_MIGRATION_VERSION`, `tick_pools` runs only the migration branch. If one region fails deterministically (a bad row, a missing template that throws inside `createFamily` or `addResourcePoolsForRegion`, and so on), the effects are:

- Every later region is never migrated.
- `runPoolTick` never runs anywhere: dirty pools are never settled by the tick, hunters and trends stop, and Overrun pools at places nobody visits never settle.
- The log gets one error line per second, indefinitely.
- Because the error is caught, the failing region's partial writes are committed on every attempt. The writes are idempotent, so they are harmless, but the region never completes.

On the local world this already passed. It matters for maincloud, where the migration runs against older, messier data.
**Fix:** Bound the retries. For example, store a failure count (and the failing region id) in `pool_state` or on the tick row. After N attempts (a named constant), log once at error level, advance the cursor past the region, record it for an admin check, and continue, so that the version can still be set. Also consider running `runPoolTick` for already-migrated regions while the migration is pending.

### WR-03: The public `pool_level` level range goes stale when a pooled family gains members

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/families.ts:202-212` (`createFamily` existing-family branch), `spacetimedb/src/helpers/pools.ts:207-210, 265` (the mirror is written only on create or on a level change)
**Issue:** `mirrorLevel` is called only from `createPool` (new pools) and from `setPoolCount` when the density level changes. The `lvLo` and `lvHi` it publishes come from `familyRangeAt`, which reads the family's members.

Two paths add a member to a family that is already pooled elsewhere:

- `createFamily`'s existing-key branch, reached from `ensurePoolsForLocation` when an ungrouped template of an existing rule creature type appears at a new place.
- The version-2 migration rerun.

After either, the family's real range at its other places changes, but their `pool_level` rows keep the old range until the density level happens to change. `createPool`'s find branch returns the existing row without refreshing its mirror.

The server reads members live (`creaturePoolsHere`, the `look` rating, the encounter level gap), while the client's Nearby, Here card, exits and Map read the `pool_level` range. The two surfaces can show different ranges and ratings, which breaks UI-SPEC P2 ("no two surfaces ever disagree").
**Fix:** After `createFamily` inserts member rows into an existing family, refresh that family's mirrors. For example, iterate `place_pool.by_region.filter(existing.regionId)` where `kind === 'creature' && refId === existing.id` and call `mirrorLevel` (it writes only on a difference). Also call `mirrorLevel(ctx, row)` in `createPool`'s find branch.

### IN-03: Duplicate place and family predicates and member-range logic

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/pool_migration.ts:61-73` and `spacetimedb/src/helpers/families.ts:374-381, 672-674`; `spacetimedb/src/helpers/pools.ts:111-125` and `spacetimedb/src/helpers/encounters.ts:111-141`

- `isUncharted`, `hostsCreatures` and `isQuestFamily` are copied in two files.
- `familyRangeAt` (the mirror) and `membersAt` with `levelRange` (encounters) compute the same range separately.

If one copy drifts from the other, the mirror and the encounter roll can disagree (see WR-03).
**Fix:** Export the predicates from `families.ts`. Have `familyRangeAt` reuse `membersAt` and `levelRange`, or the other way round.

### IN-04: Ordinary spawns that were engaged during migration come back as invisible standing enemies that `start_combat` still accepts

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/pool_migration.ts:146-157`; `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat.ts:986-991, 1026, 1130`; `spacetimedb/src/helpers/combat_rewards.ts:221, 231`
**Issue:**
- `retireStandingState` deletes only `available` spawns.
- A spawn that was `engaged` or `pulling` during the migration returns to `available` when its fight ends or its old pull resolves.
- Its template is a family member, so `individualsHere` hides it.
- `start_combat` still starts a fight against any available spawn at the place.

Nothing retires these rows later, so they stay as hidden enemies that any client sending a spawn id can still fight.
**Fix:** In `start_combat`, refuse a spawn that is not an individual (`isIndividualSpawn`), and delete it there or from `settleDirtyPools`. Alternatively, have the spawn-release paths delete ordinary (family-member) spawns instead of resetting them.

### IN-05: Quest-target slot replacement can break D-11's healer cap and its "never only healers" rule

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/encounters.ts:241-249`
**Issue:**
- D-74 replaces each slot independently (40%) with the roster's kill target.
- If that target is a healer member, a Stable group of 2 becomes two healers about 16% of the time.
- An Overrun group can go over `HEALER_CAP_*`.

The lone-slot case is explicitly allowed by D-74; the multi-slot case is not addressed by either decision.
**Fix:** Either let the owner accept it, or keep slot 0's front-liner role when the target is a healer and the group has more than one slot, and count substituted healers against the cap.

### IN-06: Hunters thin quest families of one like any other pool

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/pool_tick.ts:93-101`
**Issue:** `runHunters` includes quest (`quest:`) families. A Scarce family-of-one pool (25) can be wiped by hunters and then stay gone for 3 hours, which weakens D-74's "always completable" promise (the player cannot progress meanwhile).
**Fix:** Skip quest families in the hunter candidate list, or tell the owner.

### IN-07: A pool whose family is missing is logged every minute, forever

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/pool_tick.ts:62-77`
**Issue:** A creature pool whose family row is missing stays `dirty`. `settleDirtyPools` throws and logs it on every tick, and only the migration (`pruneOrphanPools`) ever deletes it.
**Fix:** Delete the orphan pool and its `pool_level` row in the same place (as `pruneOrphanPools` does), or clear its dirty flag.

### IN-08: `pull_family` and `gather_pool` do not refresh `player.lastActivityAt`

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/reducers/pools.ts:57-168`
**Issue:**
- `start_combat` (`combat.ts:963-966`) and `move_character` bump `lastActivityAt`; the new Nearby Pull and Gather reducers do not.
- The Nearby Pull used to go through `start_combat`, so the old pull bumped it.
- `sweep_inactivity` (`index.ts:308-334`) camps players who have been idle for 15 minutes and are not in combat.
- A player who only gathers is therefore camped mid-session.
**Fix:** Add the same `player.lastActivityAt` update both reducers' siblings use.

### IN-09: A skipped invented kill-quest target leaves an orphan template and a place link behind

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/llm_apply.ts:802-842` (D-74 path; calls `families.ts:866` `familyOfOne`)
**Issue:** The `enemy_template` row and its `location_enemy_template` link at the NPC's place are inserted before `familyOfOne`. If no pool results, the quest is skipped but both rows stay.
- If `familyOfOne` threw before `createFamily`, the template is ungrouped. A later `ensurePoolsForLocation` at a host place would fold it into the region's ordinary family of that creature type.
- The leftover link also makes rule 3 of `resolveKillQuestTarget` refuse future quests for that name.
**Fix:** Resolve and create the pool before inserting the link, or delete both rows on the skip path.

### IN-10: Caught errors commit partial writes, and one log line is not redacted

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/index.ts:468-472`; `spacetimedb/src/reducers/quests.ts` (`questItemAmbush` try/catch); `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat.ts:487-491`
**Issue:**
- In a SpacetimeDB reducer, a caught exception does not roll back the writes made before it. An ambush or pool step that throws partway leaves partial rows; a `startCombat` that throws after inserting `combat_encounter` would leave a fight with no enemies.
- The `tick_pools` catch logs `String(error)` without `redactSecrets`, unlike every other pool log.
**Fix:** Use `redactSecrets` in that log. Where a half-written fight is possible, check what the helper needs before it writes (as `drawGroup` already does for templates), or let the error propagate in user-called reducers.

### IN-11: The legacy hub step does not remove creature pools already at the new hub

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/pool_migration.ts:110-121`; `spacetimedb/src/helpers/encounters.ts:576-577`; `spacetimedb/src/helpers/travel.ts:14-16`
**Issue:**
- `markLegacyHub` sets `isHub` and leaves `isSafe` as it is.
- A start location the old AI marked unsafe may already carry creature pools from an earlier migration or the lazy net.
- `pullFamilyFor` and the travel rolls check only `isSafe`, so pulls and ambushes keep working at a place that is now a hub with services (D-59, D-61).
**Fix:** When marking a hub, delete its creature pools and their `pool_level` rows. Alternatively, make `travelRollsAt` and `pullFamilyFor` also refuse at `isHub` places.
