# Phase 51.3.1.1 Plan 29: Family count, per-place picks, feud and histories in region generation Summary

New regions now get a family count scaled to their size (the AI's families first, the rest by rule), 3 to 5 families at every non-safe, non-hub place, one seeded feud stored as mutual 'feud' relations, and a history for every family. A feud partner now fills a vacuum the way a rival does, and examining a family shows its history.

## What was built

- **`writeRegionFill` step 5, new reply shape** (`helpers/world_gen.ts`, D-66 to D-71):
  - `familyCount = keptFamilyCount(regionPlaces.length)` and `seed = familySeed(region.id)`;
  - `validateFamilies(fill, { ..., familyCount })`, or `[]` when it returns null;
  - `completeRegionFamilies(validated, { regionId, regionName, places, isTaken, familyCount, feudCount: feudCountFor(familyCount, seed), seed })` returns the families and the feud keys;
  - `buildAiFamilies(tx, regionId, families, feudKeys, places, now)`:
    1. createFamily for each family, with its history;
    2. `assignRegionFamilies` over the host places (charted, not safe, not a hub), using `aiFitNames` and `fitTerrains`; each assigned family is linked per host place, in id order;
    3. relations by key, stored both ways with INVERSE_RELATION (this includes the rival relations among the rule-made families);
    4. `storeFeud` with the feud family ids;
    5. `seedRegionPools`.
  - Every other step of writeRegionFill is unchanged.
- **Older reply shape** (`helpers/families.ts` `buildRegionFamilies`): the feud comes from `pickFeud({ keys, markedKeys: [], count: feudCountFor(defs.length, familySeed(region.id)), seed })`. Each definition gets `ruleFamilyHistory` (it names the other feud families for a feuding family). After the relations step, `storeFeud` stores the feud. Linking, relations and pools are unchanged from Plan 09.
- **Vacuum** (`helpers/pools.ts` `vacuumCandidates`): `FAMILY_FEUD_KIND` now counts like 'rival' on both sides, same region only.
- **Examine** (`helpers/examine.ts` `describeFamily`): the trimmed history goes on its own line after the group hint and before `Members:`, with no label. A family with no history reads exactly as before.
- **Unchanged (D-67):** `pool_migration.ts`, the body of `ensurePoolsForLocation`, `familiesFromTemplates` and `familyOfOne`. A source test pins `assignRegionFamilies(` to `helpers/world_gen.ts` among production files.
