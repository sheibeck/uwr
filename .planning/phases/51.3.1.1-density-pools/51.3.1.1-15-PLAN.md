<objective>
Migrate the owner's existing worlds to pools without `--clear-database`:
- group today's enemy types into families;
- make quest kill targets families of one;
- seed creature and resource pools;
- retire standing ordinary spawns and nodes;
- mark each region's current start location as its hub (D-61).

It runs as a cursor-batched step of `tick_pools`, one region per run, and it is idempotent.

Purpose:
- SC1: existing enemy types are grouped into families.
- SC7: the migration of existing enemies and nodes is tested.
- RESEARCH Section 4.

Output: `helpers/pool_migration.ts`, the tick integration, tests.
</objective>
