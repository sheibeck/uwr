# Phase 51.3.1.1 Plan 07: Families, pool seeding and the AI reply validator Summary

This plan adds the family and seeding layer. A family can be built from one definition, either from an AI reply or by rule from today's enemy types. It gets real role members with server stats and rule abilities. Any place can be seeded with creature and resource pools whose home densities are set by rule only. A quest kill target gets a family of one with its own Scarce pool. `ensurePoolsForLocation` is the lazy safety net that Plan 08 wires in. A pure validator turns the AI family reply (PROMPT-DRAFT A3) into server-numbered definitions; Plan 23 wires it in. No prompt wording was changed, and nothing was published.

## What was built

**`helpers/families.ts`**
- Types:
  - `FamilyDefinition`: key, name, singularNoun, pluralNoun, creatureType, temperament, iconKey, ambushVerb, ambushRest, fitTerrains[], and members[].
  - `FamilyMemberDefinition`: { role, name, existingTemplateId?, filler }.
  - `FamilyRelationDef`: { otherKey, kind }.
- `regionBaseLevel(ctx, regionId)` returns the region's dangerMultiplier / 100, at least 1. This is the world-gen rule.
- `createFamily(ctx, regionId, def, baseLevel)` finds or creates the family by `creature_family.key` and returns the family row.
  - A new member gets an `enemy_template` in the world-gen shape:
    - the canonical role in role, roleDetail and abilityProfile;
    - terrainTypes from fitTerrains, socialGroup = the family name, group 1..1;
    - stats from `enemyStatsForLevel(max(baseLevel, 1))`.
  - It also gets one `enemy_role_template` and the `memberAbilities(role)` rows.
  - An existing template has its role fields (and its role template's roleKey, role, roleDetail and abilityProfile) rewritten to the canonical role. No template is inserted for it.
- `linkFamilyToLocation(ctx, familyId, locationId)` adds the `location_enemy_template` links (find-or-create) and returns the number of new links.
- `createRelations(ctx, familyId, [{ otherFamilyId, kind }])` is find-or-create. A self-relation or an unknown kind is ignored.
- `isOrdinaryTemplate(ctx, templateId)` is false for:
  - a missing template;
  - `isBoss`;
  - a World event enemy name (from `WORLD_EVENT_DEFINITIONS`);
  - a `boss_kill` quest target (`quest_template.by_enemy`);
  - a `named_enemy` template (an `iter`, because named_enemy has no template index).
  kill and kill_loot targets stay ordinary.
- `familiesFromTemplates(ctx, regionId, templates)` groups templates by creatureType. The groups are ordered by their lowest template id, and members join in id order:
  - the first melee or tank is the tank; further melee and ranged are damage;
  - caster is caster; healer and support are healer;
  - fillers come from `fillerMemberName(baseName, role)` with `filler: true`;
  - nouns come from `nounsFromTemplateName(base)`; temperament and icon follow the creatureType;
  - fitTerrains come from the templates' comma lists, deduplicated, without 'any';
  - ambushVerb and ambushRest are ''.
- `seedCreaturePools(ctx, location, familyIds, now)` runs only at non-safe, non-hub, charted places. Home levels come from `creatureHomeLevels(n, poolSeed(location.id, location.regionId))`, giving Stable then Scarce and never Overrun. Each pool goes through `createPool` with timeOfDay 'any'.
- `resourceRarity(ctx, template)` returns:
  - the `economy_item` gather row's rarity, when there is one;
  - 'rare' for a modifier reagent;
  - for a MATERIAL_DEFS entry: 'common' at tier 1, 'uncommon' at tier 2 and 'rare' at tier 3 or higher;
  - 'common' for anything else.
- `seedResourcePools(ctx, location, now)` runs at charted places, safe places included:
  - zone tier as `spawnResourceNode` computes it;
  - entries from `getGatherableResourceTemplates(..., 'any', ...)` with the item pins applied (`scaleWeights`; pin 0 removes an entry);
  - one candidate per template; 'any' wins, and day plus night becomes 'any';
  - up to `RESOURCE_POOLS_PER_PLACE` (fewer when pools already exist) picked by `pickWithoutReplacement` on `poolSeed(location.id)` at `POOL_ROLL.RESOURCE_PICK`;
  - home from `resourceHomeLevel(resourceRarity)`; the timeOfDay is kept on the pool (D-55).
- `addResourcePoolsForRegion(ctx, regionId, now)` covers every charted place of the region. Each `regionalGatherEntries` item on its terrain that has no pool there gets one, beyond the per-place count (D-48). Pin 0 is skipped. It returns the new pools.
- `ensurePoolsForLocation(ctx, locationId)`:
  - It leaves uncharted or missing places alone.
  - It returns after one `place_pool.by_location` lookup when the place has a resource pool and either has a creature pool or hosts no creatures.
  - Otherwise it seeds resource pools when the place has none.
  - At a non-safe, non-hub place with no creature pool, it takes the linked ordinary templates:
    - a template that is already a member brings its family (not a `quest:` family);
    - the rest are grouped by rule and created at the region's base level;
    - every family is linked to the place and its pools are seeded.
- `familyOfOne(ctx, template, questLocationId, now)` (D-54):
  - It creates the family `quest:<templateId>`, with the template as its one member (role from `normalizeEnemyRole`), nouns from the template name, and temperament and icon by creatureType.
  - The pool place is the quest place when it is non-safe and not a hub. Otherwise it is the lowest-id such connected place, and when there is none, no pool is made.
  - At the pool place it links the template, runs `ensurePoolsForLocation`, then adds a home-1 pool (count 25).
  - It is find-or-create by key and returns the family row.

**`helpers/family_validate.ts`** (pure, no ctx)
- `validateFamilies(reply, { regionId, places, isTaken })` returns `{ families: ValidatedFamily[] | null }`:
  - It is null when there is no `families` array or no family survives.
  - At most 3 families (the first 3 plain objects), and at most 4 members each.
  - Names are run through `cleanItemName` with markup stripped first, then cut to 3 words.
  - Names are unique within the reply and against `isTaken`, without numerals. The fallbacks are tried in order: the family's fit place words, then `FAMILY_NAME_MARKS`, then two marks.
  - Fallback name: the plural noun in title case. Nouns are plain lowercase words; the singular falls back to the creatureType and the plural to `pluralize(singular)`.
  - Enum fallbacks: creatureType beast, temperament wary, iconKey ''.
  - Member roles: tank, damage, support or caster (healer is accepted too), mapped by `promptRoleToServer`. Unknown roles are dropped, and only the first member of a role is kept.
  - With no tank and no damage member, a filler damage member (`filler: true`) goes first.
  - ambushVerb is kept only when `ambushVerbForms` treats it as a plain regular verb; otherwise it is ''. ambushRest is plain words, at most 6.
  - fitLocationNames:
    - names are matched exactly, then case-insensitively;
    - a place is dropped when it is safe, a hub or uncharted (D-61);
    - the arrival point is kept when it is neither safe nor a hub;
    - with no fit left, the family gets the eligible places on its type's usual terrains, else every eligible place.
  - fitTerrains come from the fit places.
  - Relations resolve the reply's family names to kept family keys. Self-relations, unknown families, unknown kinds and repeats are dropped.
  - The key is `ai:<regionId>:<nameKey(name)>`.
- `validatePlaceWords(raw)` returns `{ shortName, placeNoun }`. shortName is at most 2 plain words, or ''. placeNoun matches `^the( [a-z][a-z'-]*){1,3}$`, or is ''.
- `FAMILY_NAME_MARKS` = Grey, Pale, Wild, Dark, Old, Red, Black, Lesser. This is PROPOSED copy for the D-58 review.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 2 - Correctness] familyOfOne seeds the pool place first.** Found during Task 2. A quest pool written to an unseeded place would make `ensurePoolsForLocation` see "creature pools exist" and never pool that place's ordinary families. So familyOfOne creates the family, then calls `ensurePoolsForLocation` on the pool place (which skips quest families), then adds its own pool. A test covers it.
2. **[Rule 2 - Correctness] ensurePoolsForLocation reuses existing families.** A template that is already a family member brings its family rather than being regrouped. A family's types linked at a second place therefore get a pool there with no duplicate family, and the quest template is never pulled into a creature-type family.
3. **[Rule 2] createFamily on an existing key adds new real members.** An existing template of the definition that is in no family yet becomes a member, so a later template of the same creature type is not left out. Fillers and new members are never added again.
4. **Extra exports beyond the plan's list:** `regionBaseLevel`, `resourceRarity`, `FamilyMemberDefinition`, `ValidateFamiliesInput`, `FamilyPlace` and `FAMILY_NAME_MARKS`. Later plans can use them, and the tests pin them.
5. **The validator returns null when no family survives** (for example, a families array of junk). It does not return an empty list, so the caller takes the rule path instead of building a region with no creatures.

No file outside files_modified was touched.
