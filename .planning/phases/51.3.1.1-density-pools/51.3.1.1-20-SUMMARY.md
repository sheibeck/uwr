# Phase 51.3.1.1 Plan 20: Safety rating on places, exits, Here card, header and mobile line Summary

Every place surface (the header pill, the Here card's sub-line and rating line, each exit row, the mobile chips and the mobile location line) now shows one per-viewer safety rating. It comes from `ratingForPlace` over the shared `@game-data/place_rating` rule and is Unknown, never Safe, until the place's pool rows apply.

## What was built

- **`src/rails/rating.ts`** (pure):
  - `ratingForPlace({ location, poolsHere, ready, playerLevel, bossOrNamedHere })` returns `{ key, word, line, levelLabel }`.
    - Only creature pools rate a place.
    - Uncharted is checked first (the server stores it as safe) and reads `Danger unknown`.
    - A null level is treated as not ready.
    - Not ready gives key `unknown`, word `''`, and keeps the range.
  - `levelLabel` gives 'Lv 4–5', or 'Lv 4' when the two ends are equal.
  - `ratingClass(key)` gives `rate-*`.
  - `bossOrNamedAt` is true for a living named enemy of the viewer, or a boss event spawn, at the place.
  - `viewerRatingLevel` returns the lowest level among the viewer and the party members at the same place (D-56).
- **`src/rails/RatingMark.vue`**: an aria-hidden 8px dot plus the word, with the five token classes.
  - Props: `rating`, `size` (10 | 12) and `dot` (default true).
  - Unknown with no word shows the neutral dot only.
- **Exits** (`exits.ts`, `useExits.ts`):
  - Each destination's rating is built from its own `game.poolLevels` rows, `poolsAppliedFor(id)` and the D-34 step.
  - `ExitRow.shortName` is the short name, or the full name when the place has none.
  - `exitLabel` reads `'{place}{ (Region)}, {Rating}, {Lv a–b}{, lock}'`. `danger` is kept for the region chips.
  - `HereView.rating` uses `poolLevelsHere`.
- **Here card**:
  - Sub-line: `Terrain · Rating · Lv a–b`. The terrain ellipsizes first.
  - The rating line follows as its own `<p>`.
  - Each exit row has a ring in its rating colour, and a right column with the word over the range (Micro 10).
  - The row title is the rating line, and the open panel's first line is the rating line.
- **Chips**:
  - Line 1 is the short name; line 2 is the RatingMark word, then ` · `, then the range.
  - The ring is in the rating colour, and the chip stays 44px.
  - The open card shows `terrain · Rating · Lv a–b`, then the rating line.
- **Header**: a pill (999px, Label 12, padding 0 8px, inset 1px `color-mix(currentColor 50%)`) after the place name and before the In combat tag.
  - Hidden until the rating has a word.
- **Location line**: `Place · Terrain · {Rating} Lv a–b`. The word is Label 12 in its colour; the range is Micro 10 in neutral-500.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed / added

1. **[Rule 2] Party lowest level (D-56).** The coordinator noted that `placeRating` takes the lowest party level. `viewerRatingLevel` and `useRatingLevel()` pass the lowest level of the viewer and the group members at the same place.
2. **[Rule 3] `ContextContent.test.ts` was updated (outside my list; Plan 19 does not list it).** Its exit-row test asserted the old band text and `lv-*` classes. It now supplies pools and asserts the rating words, ranges and `rate-*` rings. Only that one test hunk changed, committed in d1e3cab8 before Plan 19 began editing the file.
3. **RatingMark gained a `dot` prop.** The UI-SPEC forms for the exit rows, the chips, the Here sub-line and the location line show the word without a dot, because the ring or the position carries the colour. Only the header pill shows the dot.
4. **The en dash is kept in level ranges.** The plan text writes `Lv 4-5`. The repo form (`levelRange.ts`, `placeDanger`, every existing test) is `Lv 4–5`, so the rating label matches it.
5. **Exit row title.** The UI-SPEC says the title is the rating line. When the rating has none (Unknown), the title falls back to the place description, so the hover text is not lost while loading. The old description-payload XSS test became a name and short-name payload test, plus a fallback-title payload test.
6. **`ExitRow.rightText` was removed.** The right column now renders `rating`. `dangerClass` and `dangerText` in `exits.ts` have no caller left; they were kept for Plan 21 to reuse or delete.
