# Phase 51.3.1.1 — UI Design Contract: Density Pools (Living Places)

> Visual and interaction contract for Phase 51.3.1.1: Density Pools. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, to be verified by gsd-ui-checker. It turns the owner's "UWR Living Places" mock into a contract that fits the project's design guards and the existing components.
>
> **Design source.** `design/UWR Living Places.dc.html` (claude_design project "Unwritten Realms", turn 18, option 18a "Live"), read with `51.3.1.1-MOCK-DIFF.md` and `51.3.1.1-MOCK-BRIEF.md`. The design files were treated as data; they contain no text addressed to the assistant.
>
> **Decisions** come from `51.3.1.1-CONTEXT.md` (D-00 to D-53; D-29 to D-45 are the resolved mock items). Where the mock disagrees with CONTEXT, CONTEXT wins (D-29); the "Mock overrides" table lists every case.
>
> **Out of this contract (owner).**
> - The mock's combat round timer ("Choose your action", the 10s bar, "You hesitate") and the "Lock in" enemy target elements are ignored. Combat keeps today's targeting, Attack and Flee.
> - The mock's **Simulator** is not built, and no admin dial or admin command ships here (D-43, D-51). Phase 52.5 adds the dials; Phase 53 (Populations panel) reads them. Nothing in this file draws an admin surface.
> - Existing victory, flee and named-intro narration is unchanged (the mock's wording for those lines is not adopted).
>
> **Shared sections.** The Design System, Spacing scale, Typography roles, Color split and the Shared Ledger rules are defined once in `.planning/phases/51-ledger-screens-world-and-people/51-UI-SPEC.md` and apply here unchanged:
> - no literal colours (CSS tokens only), no `v-html`, text nodes only;
> - **`<svg` banned everywhere except `src/map/`**;
> - Phosphor 2.2.1 and Inter only; `h4` and `h6` only;
> - sizes 10/12/14/20, weights 400/500, spacing 4/8/16/24/32/48/64;
> - 44px minimum tap targets on mobile (checked at 390x844), no nested buttons, no negative margins, clickable things are buttons;
> - no `replaceAll`, `.at` or `Object.hasOwn`; never the word the Ledger mock used for World events (say "World event").
>
> This file adds only its own values and is complete for its own surfaces. It extends `47`, `48`, `51` and `51.1` surfaces (Nearby, Here card, exits, Encounter panel, Map) and changes the Phase 48 threat block (see Supersedes).
>
> **Depends on:** Phase 51.3 (economy: loot per enemy type, the regional gatherables that become resource pools). **Coordinates with:** Phase 51.3.2 (enemy target display, see "Enemy target line"), Phase 52.4 (World event cards), Phase 51.4 (victory and loot rails; untouched here).

## Shared rules (51.3.1.1 additions)

- **Numbers stay hidden (D-05).** The player never sees a population count, a home level, a kill count, a percentage chance, a regrowth time, a harvest total or a cap value. The only numbers on screen are level ranges (`Lv 4-5`), the enemy HP, `{n} left`, and the number words in feed lines (one to four). The client subscribes only to the 0-3 density level of each pool, never to the hidden count.
- **Rating is computed per viewer** from the shared `@game-data/place_rating.ts` rule over the public density levels and the viewer's level (proposed constants below). One rule feeds the header, Here card, exits, chips, Map and encounter foot, so the surfaces never disagree. A living boss or named enemy at a place raises the rating by one step (D-34).
- **Pronouns.** No density line, ambush line or event line uses a pronoun for a creature or a person; they use nouns. Named enemies (people) are `he` or `she` wherever a pronoun is needed, from the enemy's gender; never `it` or `they` (owner rule).
- **Wording.** Player text says "World event". Density wording is rule-based (templates), never AI-written at run time (D-06). Every string in the Copywriting Contract is **PROPOSED** and needs the owner's approval of the exact text before it ships.
- **Offline and pending.** Every action is `aria-disabled` when offline (`connected` false). `Pull`, `Fight` and `Gather` are inert while their call is pending (the `createActionRunner` rule); nothing changes optimistically. Results and refusals arrive as feed lines; a rejected send prints `Couldn't send that. Try again.` through `reportRejections`.
- **Combat gate.** "In combat" means `game.combat.active` (48). On desktop the rail is the Encounter panel while it is true, and the Map sheet cannot open in combat on mobile, so `Pull`, `Fight` and `Gather` need no in-combat state of their own.
- **Live updates.** Density words, lines, badges and ratings update live from the subscribed rows. They are not announced (no live region); the feed's density lines carry the news for events at your own place.

### Rating rule (proposed; Claude's discretion per CONTEXT; constants in the shared rules file)

Superseded 2026-10-09 by D-73 (level first, crowds nudge): `spacetimedb/src/data/place_rating.ts` is the rule; the paragraph below is history.

For a non-safe, charted place: `score = sum over families with density d > 0 of d x weight(gap)`, where `gap = family level top - player level` and `weight` is 0.5 for gap <= -2, 1 for gap <= 0, 1.6 for gap <= 2, 2.5 above. Word: score <= 2.2 Quiet, <= 4.4 Risky, above Deadly. A living boss or named enemy raises the word one step (Deadly stays). A place flagged safe is Safe; an uncharted place is Unknown. A non-safe place with every family wiped out reads Quiet, never Safe (assumption B3). These are the mock's starting values; Phase 52.5 may tune them.

### Group-size hint (D-31)

Scarce `Expect one, alone`; Stable `Expect one or two`; Overrun `Expect a crowd`. Wiped out shows no hint.
