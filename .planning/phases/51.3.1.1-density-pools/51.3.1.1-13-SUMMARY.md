# Phase 51.3.1.1 Plan 13: Travel Encounters Summary

Leaving or entering a non-safe place now rolls once per travelling party against that place's pools, seeded by the leader and sized by the lowest traveller's level. A leave hit starts the fight where the party stands and spends nothing. An enter hit prints the ambush line, then the place card, then the fight. A miss prints "Nothing follows you. This time." All three travel callers share one `travelDeps` builder.

## What was built

### Task 1: travelDeps (commit 0fc3184d)
- `helpers/travel.ts`: `export interface TravelDeps` (the old inline deps type plus `startCombat`) and `export function travelDeps(deps: any): TravelDeps`. It picks exactly `appendSystemMessage`, `appendPrivateEvent`, `appendLocationEvent`, `appendGroupEvent`, `areLocationsConnected`, `activeCombatIdForCharacter`, `ensurePoolsForLocation`, `isGroupLeaderOrSolo`, `effectiveGroupId` and `startCombat` from the module deps bag.
- `move_character` (movement.ts) and both typed travel paths in intent.ts call `performTravel(ctx, travelDeps(deps), ...)` inside the reducer. That matters because index.ts assigns `reducerDeps.startCombat` after the bag is built.

### Coordinator extra: typed loot ambush (commit 5495ffe4)
- intent.ts "loot <item>" passes `startCombat: deps.startCombat` to `pickUpQuestItem`, so the typed command draws the same quest-item ambush as `loot_quest_item`.
- quest_item_pickup.test.ts: the "KNOWN GAP" case is now "leave identical quest rows, item rows, combats and messages when the roll hits (one ambush each)". It expects 1 combat on both paths, origin `ambush_other`, and full snapshot equality. The header note about the gap is replaced.

### Task 2: leave and enter rolls (RED 532cf8bf, GREEN 83b239ae)
In `performTravel`:
1. **Leave roll.** This runs after every check (location, combat, gathering, connection, travellers, stamina, cross-region cooldown) and before the deduction loop. It happens only when the origin is charted and not safe (`travelRollsAt`).
   - `rollEncounter` uses phase 'leave', `rosterLevel(travelingCharacters)` and leaderId = the character.
   - On a hit with a non-empty `drawGroup`, it calls `startPoolFight` with originKind 'ambush_leave', candidates = the travellers and `groupId = effectiveGroupId(character)`. The line is `ambushLine({ phase: 'leave', party })` (kind 'ambush'). Then `return false`: no stamina spent, no cooldown, no move, no passage collapse.
2. **Deferred lines.** In `moveOne`, a rolling destination skips the 'You travel to' private line and the auto-look and records the character id. Everything else is unchanged: visited rows, depart/arrive location lines, `ensurePoolsForLocation`, passive search, World event registration and AUTO-JOIN.
3. **Enter roll.** This runs after the move loop and `collapsePassageIfEmpty`, and only when the destination rolls.
   - The travellers are re-read first.
   - If any traveller is in an active fight (AUTO-JOIN, Pitfall 10), there is no roll. Each traveller gets the plain 'You travel to {Place}.' (kind 'move') and the card.
   - Otherwise `rollEncounter` runs with phase 'enter' and the lowest level.
   - **Hit:** each traveller gets the ambush line (party wording when more than one), then the card. Then `startPoolFight` runs with 'ambush_enter', the re-read leader and travellers, and line null.
   - **Miss:** each traveller gets `travelQuiet(place, party)` (kind 'travel_quiet'), then the card.
4. The uncharted / world-gen block is unchanged, and uncharted places never roll.

Acceptance greps: `rollEncounter(` 2, `'travel_quiet'` 1, `'ambush_leave'|'ambush_enter'` 2, `travelDeps(` 2 in intent.ts and 1 in movement.ts.

## Tests

`reducers/travel_encounter.integration.test.ts` (14 tests) runs the real `move_character` handler on the strict pool world. The test scans T0 offsets with the real `rollEncounter` + `drawGroup` on a probe world to find hitting and missing timestamps. It covers:
- **Solo leave:** a hit gives the 'As you try to leave Glass Orchard, ...!' line and an `ambush_leave` fight at the orchard. Alice stays put with stamina 50 and no travel_cooldown. There is no move, quiet or look line. A direct `performTravel(ctx, travelDeps(bag), ...)` returns false. A miss spends stamina, sets the cross-region cooldown and moves.
- **Solo enter:** a hit gives the feed order ambush, look, combat ('Combat begins against Goblins.'), with no 'move' line. A miss gives exactly travel_quiet, look ('You travel to Mother Pan Flats. Nothing follows you. This time.').
- **Safe places:** a safe destination and a safe origin never roll over 25 timestamps each, even with an Overrun Goblins pool placed at the market.
- **AUTO-JOIN:** an arrival that joins an active group fight skips the enter roll on a timestamp that would otherwise hit. The traveller gets one fight, the plain move line and the card.
- **Party:** Alice (L3) and Bob (L6) travel; Cara is offline at the market.
  - Cara does not move and gets no lines.
  - Alice and Bob are the only participants and both get 'As your party crosses into ...'.
  - The Goblins read up to L8 at offset 5, and the group size equals `groupSizeFor(3, 5, seed)` = `groupSizeFor(3, 2, seed) - 1`, so the trim comes from the lowest level.
  - On a miss, both travellers get the party quiet line.
  - A party leave ambush keeps both at the origin with no stamina or cooldown spent.
- **Determinism:** the same timestamp gives identical outcomes 10 times. Leader 1 hits and leader 2 misses at the same timestamp and level.
