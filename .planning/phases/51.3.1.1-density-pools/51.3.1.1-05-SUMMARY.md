# Phase 51.3.1.1 Plan 05: Enemy targeting, shield absorb and single named spawns Summary

The round engine now drives Plan 04's enemy-side abilities. A support heals the most hurt ally of its own fight, and a tank shields an unshielded ally. Each skips the ability when nobody needs it and auto-attacks instead. A wind-up remembers its ally. Every enemy's current target and heal target are written on its row for the enemy card (D-40). Enemy shields absorb player auto-attacks, pet attacks and the bard pulse, and a source pin catches any new damage path. A named, boss or quest-boss fight now spawns one enemy.

## What was built

### Task 1: Ally targeting, skip rules, cast target, heal target and aggro target writes
- **`helpers/combat.ts`:** `executeAbilityAction`'s enemy args gain `targetEnemyId?: bigint`. `executeEnemyAbility(ctx, combatId, enemyId, abilityKey, targetCharacterId?, targetPetId?, targetEnemyId?)` passes it to `resolveAbility`'s 7th parameter.
- **`reducers/combat.ts`, new closure helpers in `registerCombatReducers`:**
  - `ENEMY_ALLY_KINDS = ['heal', 'shield']`, `isEnemyAllyKind(kind)`, `ENEMY_ALLY_RULES = ['self', 'all_allies', 'lowest_hp_ally', 'single_ally']`.
  - `pickAllyTarget(ctx, combatId, enemyId, rule, kind)` returns `{ allyEnemyId }` or undefined:
    - It picks from the living enemies of the fight, the caster included. The `self` rule means the caster only.
    - A heal picks the lowest HP fraction below full.
    - A shield picks an ally without a `damage_shield` (lowest HP fraction first).
    - Ties go to the lower id. If no one is eligible, it returns undefined.
  - `writeEnemyCard(ctx, enemyId, { healTargetEnemyId?, target? })`: one `combat_enemy` update, only when a value changes. A pet target sets `aggroTargetPetId` and clears `aggroTargetCharacterId`.
  - `pickEnemyTarget(rule, candidates, ctx, combatId, enemyId, kind?)` gains the 6th parameter. A heal or shield kind always takes the ally branch; player and pet targeting is unchanged for every other kind.
- **`tryEnemyAbilityForRound`:**
  - A heal or shield with no eligible ally is not a candidate, so the enemy falls through to its auto-attack (T-51.3.1.1-15).
  - After the AI roll, the card is written:
    - a heal or shield writes `healTargetEnemyId = ally`;
    - a player or pet ability writes `healTargetEnemyId 0n` plus the target;
    - an ally-rule ability (e.g. an `all_allies` buff) writes only `healTargetEnemyId 0n`.
  - A wind-up stores `combat_enemy_cast.targetEnemyId` (the ally, else `0n`). An immediate cast passes `targetEnemyId` to `executeAbilityAction`.
- **`landEnemyCasts`:**
  - A heal or shield ignores any player or pet target. It lands on the stored ally while that ally lives in this fight, and re-picks by the same rule only when the ally is gone.
  - If no ally is eligible, it fizzles (`{enemy}'s {ability} fizzles.`) and clears `healTargetEnemyId`.
  - Other kinds keep today's rules and write the landing target (no write when it is unchanged).
- **`processEnemyAutoAttackForRound`:** writes `healTargetEnemyId 0n` and the chosen character or pet, after the target is checked as valid. With no valid target it still clears the heal target.
- **`resolveRound` enemy turn:** a stunned turn clears the heal target, since an interrupted heal ends it. A winding-up enemy keeps its card until the cast lands.

### Task 2: Shield absorb at every player damage site, with a source pin
- `resolveAttack` gains `absorb?: (damage) => bigint`. It runs on any positive `finalDamage` before HP is lowered and before the hit line is built, and its result also carries `absorbed`.
- Sites now calling `absorbEnemyShield` before lowering `currentHp`:
  - the player auto-attack (`processPlayerAutoAttackForRound`);
  - the pet auto-attack (`processPetCombat`; pet threat counts `finalDamage + absorbed`);
  - the bard Discordant Note pulse (`tick_bard_songs`).
- Each site logs `A ward on {enemy} absorbs {n} damage.` privately to the attacker, the pet owner or the bard. It goes through the new closure helper `wardAbsorbLine` and is the same PROPOSED wording as Plan 04's ability path.
- `reducers/enemy_damage_sites.test.ts` scans every non-test `.ts` file under `spacetimedb/src`. It finds each `combat_enemy.id.update(...)` whose argument sets `currentHp` and requires one of two things:
  - an `absorbEnemyShield(` call before it in the same function (the nearest definition at indent 0 or 2), or
  - an allow-list entry `{ file, contains, reason }` that matches exactly one write.
- Allow list:
  - enemy heal and enemy drain self-heal (`helpers/combat.ts`);
  - the enemy DoT tick (`reducers/combat.ts`, RESEARCH: DoT may bypass);
  - four perk writes in `helpers/combat_perks.ts`, marked KNOWN GAP (see below).
- The pin also states that the auto-attack, pet, bard and `applyDamageToEnemy` writes are protected, not allow-listed.

### Task 3: Named, boss and quest-boss fights spawn exactly one enemy
- `spawnEnemyWithTemplate` now inserts one `enemy_spawn` with `groupCount 1n` and one member, using the same role pick seed as the old first member.
- The `groupMin..groupMax` loop and the danger bias are gone from this function only; `spawnEnemy` is unchanged.
- The place-scaled level (`placeSpawnLevel`) is kept, with no stat bonus (D-52). A comment cites D-07 and D-53.
- The tests went into `combat_actors.integration.test.ts` (new describe block), as the plan named first:
  - the direct helper call (groupMin 3..5 gives one spawn and one member);
  - `pull_named_enemy` with groupMin 4 gives one spawn (engaged) and one `combat_enemy`;
  - level 5 in a danger-500 place, with `maxHp`, attack and armour equal to `computeEnemyStats(templateAtLevel(template, 5), role, [character])`.

## Known Gaps and follow-ups (not fixed; outside this plan's scope or files)

- **Perk damage bypasses enemy shields** in `helpers/combat_perks.ts`: the bonus strike, flat bonus, on-kill AoE and the active `damagePercent` perk. They are allow-listed as KNOWN GAP in the pin. A later plan that owns `combat_perks.ts` should call `absorbEnemyShield` and drop those entries.
- **A pet killed by an enemy auto-attack keeps its `aggro_entry` (pre-existing).** `pickEnemyTarget`'s aggro branch accepts pet entries without checking that the pet exists. An enemy can therefore keep choosing the dead pet and idle until a player out-aggroes it. The card is not affected: `writeEnemyCard` runs only after the pet is checked.
- **Still on the wrong side for AI-written enemy abilities (Plan 04's note):** enemy `fear`, `hot` and non-debuff `buff`. `hot` is not an ally kind in `pickEnemyTarget`. `memberAbilities` never uses these kinds.
- **`spawnEnemyWithTemplate` still throws on `isBoss` templates** ('Named enemies cannot be tracked'), a refusal meant for `start_tracked_combat`. No production code sets `isBoss` today. When Plan 27 retires `start_tracked_combat`, it can move or drop the refusal.
- `helpers/loot.test.ts`'s WR-01 comment ("pull_named_enemy spawns a group") is now historical. The test still passes.
