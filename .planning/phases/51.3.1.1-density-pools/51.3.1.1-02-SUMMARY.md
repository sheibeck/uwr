# Phase 51.3.1.1 Plan 02: Shared rating rule, density copy and family rules Summary

Three pure shared modules are now in place. `place_rating.ts` is the one rating rule. It gives Safe, Danger unknown, Unknown, Quiet, Risky or Deadly, scored in integer tenths from the `DENSITY_RULES` rating constants. `density_lines.ts` holds every density, rating, feed, ambush, encounter, World event and rumour string as PROPOSED templates and builders. `family_rules.ts` holds the one role vocabulary (D-53), the rule-built member abilities, filler names, nouns, and the temperament and icon defaults.

## What was built

**Task 1: `data/place_rating.ts`** (15 tests)
- `RATING_KEYS`, `RatingKey`, `RATING_WORDS` (Safe, Quiet, Risky, Deadly, Unknown).
- `placeRating({ isSafe, isUncharted?, ready, families: {level, lvHi}[], playerLevel, bossOrNamedHere? })` returns `{ key, word }`.
  - safe -> Safe; uncharted -> 'Danger unknown'; not ready -> Unknown with word ''.
  - Otherwise the score is sum(level x weight(lvHi - playerLevel)) over families with level > 0.
  - Score up to RATING_QUIET_MAX_X10 is Quiet, up to RATING_RISKY_MAX_X10 is Risky, above that Deadly.
  - A boss or named enemy raises the rating one step; Deadly stays Deadly.
- `ratingLevelRange(families: {lvLo, lvHi}[])` returns `{ lo, hi }` or null. Wiped-out families are included.
- Imports only `./density_rules`, with no inline rating numbers.

**Task 2: `data/density_lines.ts`** (39 tests)
- Words:
  - `CREATURE_DENSITY_WORDS`, `RESOURCE_DENSITY_WORDS`, `DENSITY_SR_PREFIX`

  - `NUMBER_WORDS`, `numberWord`, `capitalNumberWord`
- Templates: `CREATURE_LINES` (3 temperaments x 4 levels), `RESOURCE_LINES`, `RATING_LINES`, `GROUP_HINTS`.
- Place nouns: `PLACE_NOUN_BY_TERRAIN`, `DEFAULT_PLACE_NOUN`.
- Builders:
  - `placeNounFor`, `creatureLine`, `resourceLine`, `ratingLine`, `groupHint`, `sentenceCase`
  - `ambushVerbForms`, `ambushLine`, `pullLeadIn`, `travelQuiet`
  - `densityDownLine`, `densityGoneLine`, `overrunSettleLine`, `vacuumLine`
  - `gatherResult`, `lastGatherLine`, `harvestCapRefusal`, `pullRefusal`, `outOfTimeRefusal`, `exhaustedRefusal`, `allExhaustedLine`
  - `encounterHeading`, `encounterSource`, `worldEventName`, `rumorItem`
- Constants: `NOTHING_HUNTS`, `SLAIN_NAMED_LINE`.
- Types: `AmbushLinePhase`, `DensityShift`, `DensityShiftKind`.
- The test builds more than 200 outputs from every builder, with one and many. None uses they, them, their, its or it, and none contains the banned World-event word.

**Task 3: `data/family_rules.ts`** (22 tests)
- Roles:
  - `normalizeEnemyRole` (melee, ranged, dps -> damage; support -> healer; unknown -> damage)

  - healer: Strike and Mend (heal on the lowest-HP ally, 2 s cast)
  - caster: Bolt (2 s cast) and Siphon (drain, 2 s cast)
  - Cooldowns are 0 / 12 / 12 / 10 / 12, as planned. No taunt or cc.
- Filler names: `ROLE_NAME_WORD`, `fillerMemberName`.
- Nouns: `pluralize`, `nounsFromTemplateName`.
