## Fixed Issues

### WR-01: The client copies the server's place target-level formula

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/data/enemy_rules.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/data/enemy_rules.test.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/location.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/location_spawn_level.test.ts`, `src/rails/levelRange.ts`, `src/rails/levelRange.test.ts`
**Commit:** ceef98e9
**Applied fix:**
- New pure rule `placeTargetLevelFor(dangerMultiplier, levelOffset, baseLevel = 1n)` in `data/enemy_rules.ts`. It computes floor(base * multiplier / 100) + offset, clamped to at least 1. A missing multiplier reads as 100 and a missing offset as 0.
- `computeLocationTargetLevel` now calls it.
- The client `placeTargetLevel` imports it via `@game-data/enemy_rules`, and `routeLevel` takes its band from `placeLevelBand`. No copy of the math is left on the client.
- Parity tests:
  - The server helper equals the shared rule over a grid of multipliers, offsets and base levels, and also when the region row is missing.
  - The client `placeTargetLevel` and `routeLevel` equal the shared rule and band over a grid.
  - A source guard checks that `levelRange.ts` imports the rule and holds no `/ 100n` or `level ± 1` math.
- The existing `levelRange.test.ts` cases are kept and now test the shared rule.

### WR-02: `boss_kill` resolved and linked its target at the player's place

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/llm_apply.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/llm_apply.test.ts`
**Commit:** 1867624a
**Applied fix:**
- `questPlaceId = npc.locationId` is hoisted above the questType branches.
- The "giver's place is gone" skip now applies to kill, kill_loot and boss_kill. delivery, explore and the other types are unchanged.
- boss_kill now works from `questPlaceId` in three places: it searches that place and its connections, links an invented boss there, and runs its no-name fallback there.
- New tests in the moved-away block:

  - A boss template linked only at the player's new place 200n is not bound, and the no-name fallback does not pick it.
  - A gone giver place creates no boss quest and no template.

### IN-01: `settlePoolKills` unlinked every kill before any pool was written

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/pools.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/pool_kills.integration.test.ts`
**Commit:** 761619c6
**Applied fix:**
- Counted kills are grouped by pool.
- Each pool's rows are unlinked (`poolId: 0n`) right before that pool is written, inside the loop.
- Each pool's lookup and `applyDepletion` run in their own try. A failure is logged with `redactSecrets` and that pool is skipped.
- New test: a fight with kills from two pools, where the first pool (in id order) throws on lookup:
  - the call does not throw;
  - the error is logged;
  - the second pool is depleted;
  - the failing pool is unchanged;
  - every kill is unlinked, so a second settle depletes nothing (no double count).
- The review B WR-03 test still passes.

### IN-02: A renamed invented target could pass 40 characters

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/data/economy_design_rules.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/family_validate.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/family_validate.test.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/llm_apply.test.ts`
**Commit:** 951b0463
**Applied fix:**
- `MAX_NAME_CHARS` (40, the `cleanItemName` cap) is now exported. Only the `export` keyword was added.
- `NameBook.take` gains an optional `maxChars` argument. `freeCreatureName` passes `MAX_NAME_CHARS`, so every candidate is fitted before the uniqueness check:
  - the words between the mark and the creature noun are dropped first;
  - then a single over-long word loses its end.
- Family naming, which calls `take` without the argument, is unchanged.
- New tests:
  - a taken 40-character one-word name;
  - a taken long two-word name, which keeps the noun behind the mark;
  - fitted names that are already taken are skipped;
  - short names are unchanged;
  - an end-to-end kill quest with a taken 40-character name.

**Known limitations (left as noted, per the coordinator):**
- A renamed target ("Old Ash Wolf") still differs from the name in the NPC's dialogue and stored `questDescription` ("Ash Wolf").
- The model's casing is kept. Title-casing was not applied.
- The derived family name of a family of one, which is the plural from `nounsFromTemplateName`, can still be one character over 40 when the template name is at the cap. That rule is outside this finding.

These limitations are also documented on `freeCreatureName`.
