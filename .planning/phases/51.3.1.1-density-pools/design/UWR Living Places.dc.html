18Living places: populations, pulls and ambushes

Places no longer hold individual enemies or gathering nodes. Each place has creature families and resources, and each has a density the player only sees as a word: Overrun, Stable, Scarce or Wiped out for creatures; Abundant, Plentiful, Sparse or Exhausted for resources. The Nearby rail describes each one in the Keeper's voice. Pull draws a mixed-role group whose size depends on the density, and named enemies are still listed one by one. Every place and exit shows a safety rating (Safe, Quiet, Risky or Deadly). Arriving at or leaving a dangerous place can start an ambush. Once a fight starts, each round gives you a 10-second countdown to choose an action. Your choice resolves when the timer ends, and if you choose nothing you hesitate. In a fight, each enemy card ends with who it's targeting, which also shows taunt, so there's no threat list. The simulator on the right is the server view, so you can change densities, move time on and send rival hunters through. Players never see those numbers. The party bar and hotbar are placeholders.

18aLive. Start on Mother Pan Flats. Pull the skitterers, or travel to the Glass Orchard (Deadly) to risk an ambush. Win fights to thin a family until it's wiped out, then move time on in the simulator to see a rival family move in.
