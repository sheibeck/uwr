# Phase 51.3.1.1 Plan 14: The scheduled pool tick Summary

A guarded `tick_pools` reducer reschedules one row each minute, then settles only the dirty pools (via `place_pool.by_dirty`). Every 10 minutes it runs a seeded, Overrun-weighted background hunter through `applyDepletion(..., 'hunter')`, and every 30 minutes it reports region trends through `onPoolShift` region_trend. `init`, `clientConnected` and `sweep_passages` arm the tick, so it starts on a republished database.

## What was built

### helpers/pool_tick.ts
- `poolState(ctx): PoolStateRow`: find-or-create the singleton id 1n (version 0n, zero times).
- `settleDirtyPools(ctx, now): number`: `[...place_pool.by_dirty.filter(true)]` in id order, `settlePool` each, per-pool try/catch. Clean pools are never read or written. Regrowth is silent. An Overrun pool settling below 67 prints `overrunSettleLine` to the online characters at the place, through `settlePool` and `announceFall`.
- `runHunters(ctx, now, state)`: runs once `now - lastHunterMicros >= HUNTER_INTERVAL_MICROS`. For each region (id order, isolated):
  - seed `poolSeed(region.id, now / HUNTER_INTERVAL_MICROS)`;
  - `hunterActive(seed)`;
  - the region's settled creature pools above level 0;
  - `pickHunterTargets(seed, ..., HUNTER_PLACES_PER_REGION)`;
  - `applyDepletion(ctx, pool, HUNTER_THIN_POINTS, now, 'hunter', seed)`.
  It then sets `lastHunterMicros = now`, active or not.
- `runRegionTrends(ctx, now, state)`: runs once `now - lastTrendMicros >= TREND_CHECK_MICROS`.
  - It sums the creature `pool_level.level` per region (`pool_level.by_region`).
  - The first check inserts `pool_region { regionId, trendSum }` with no shift.
  - Later checks call `regionTrend(then, now)`; a non-null result calls `onPoolShift({ kind: 'region_trend', regionId, trend })`.
  - The sum is stored on every check, and `lastTrendMicros` becomes now.
- `runPoolTick(ctx, arg, now)` runs settleDirtyPools, then runHunters, then runRegionTrends. Each step has its own try/catch, so a step can be added in front (Plan 15).
- `ensurePoolTickScheduled(ctx)` inserts a row due now with `afterRegionId 0n` when `pool_tick` is empty.

### index.ts
- `tick_pools`: the guard (first statement), then the unchanged reschedule insert, then `runPoolTick(ctx, arg, ctx.timestamp.microsSinceUnixEpoch)` in a try/catch with a console line.
- `ensurePoolTickScheduled(ctx)` in `init` and `clientConnected` (after `ensurePassageSweepScheduled`), and in `sweep_passages` after its own reschedule and before `sweepPassages`.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Test design] Hunter activity share measured over ten regions**
- **Found during:** Task 1 GREEN
- **Issue:** Region 1's first 100 intervals after T0 roll 39 active, against a long-run rate of 25.05% over 10 000 intervals (regions 2, 3 and 7 give 28, 25 and 25 in their first 100). A single-region band test would pin an unlucky stretch of the seed.
- **Fix:** The 100-interval runHunters loop now asserts that the tick acts exactly on the seeded-active checks, that Overrun is picked more often than Stable, and that activity stays below half. The share is checked across ten regions × 100 intervals, within 5 points of HUNTER_ACTIVITY_PCT.
- **Commit:** 42ae88c3

**2. [Rule 2 - Correctness] Orphan creature pools are skipped by the tick**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (behavior "a pool whose family row is missing logs once")
- **Fix:** settleDirtyPools treats a creature pool with no family row as a bad row: it logs once per tick and does not settle it, so no nameless pool_level mirror is written.
- **Commit:** 42ae88c3
