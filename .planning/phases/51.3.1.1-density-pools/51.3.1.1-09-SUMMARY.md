# Phase 51.3.1.1 Plan 09: Region fill and quest targets feed pools; hubs, stations and bind stones by rule Summary

The region fill now builds creature families and pools by rule from the enemy types the current prompt returns. Quest-invented kill and kill_loot targets become families of one with their own Scarce pool. The region economy leaves out filler members and turns applied gatherables into resource pools. Hubs, their vendor and banker, crafting stations and bind stones are placed by the server's seeded rules (D-59 to D-64). None of this changes prompt wording or adds an enqueue site.

## What was built

### Task 1: region fill families (commits 7d0c664e RED, 997a69e2 GREEN)
- `families.ts`:
  - `ruleRelations(familyIds)`: every ordered pair is a rival, deduplicated.
  - `familyFitPlaces(fitTerrains, places)`: host places (charted, neither safe nor a hub) whose terrain fits, else every host place, in id order.
  - `linkFamilyToPlaces(ctx, familyId, fitPlaces, familiesByPlace)`: links the family and records it per place.
  - `seedRegionPools(ctx, places, familiesByPlace, now)`: creature pools per host place (family ids ascending) and resource pools at every charted place.
  - `buildRegionFamilies(ctx, region, templates, places, now)`: runs familiesFromTemplates, then createFamily at regionBaseLevel, then linking, rule relations and seeding. It returns the family rows. Rerun-safe.
- `writeRegionFill` step 6 now calls `buildRegionFamilies` with the arrival point and the new places, re-read after the hub step. The old "link every enemy to every non-safe place" step is gone.

### Task 2: quest targets, economy (commits 0cca48b7 RED, fa620c24 GREEN)
- `llm_apply.ts`: after an invented quest target is inserted, `kill` and `kill_loot` call `familyOfOne(ctx, newEt, character.locationId, now)` inside a try/catch with a console line. `boss_kill` does nothing more (D-07). `startEnemyLoot` is unchanged.
- `region_economy.ts`:
  - `regionEnemyTemplates` skips templates whose `family_member` row has `filler: true`, so the region job input and the late-enemy follow-up stay at today's size and cost (T-51.3.1.1-27).
  - After status complete and before `startLateEnemies`, it calls `addResourcePoolsForRegion(ctx, regionId, now)` inside a try/catch (D-48).

### Task 3: hubs (commits 9d37a9ea RED, 55005848 GREEN)
- `regionHubCount(region, isStarter)` = `hubCountFor(danger, isStarter, hubSeed(region.id))`.
- `readHubMarks(fill, arrival, rowsByLowerName)`: the arrival point first when `fill.arrival.isHub`, then marked `fill.locations` by lowercase name. It returns `[]` for today's reply shape.
- `placeRegionHubs(tx, { region, arrival, isStarter, markedIds })` calls `chooseHubs` on the region's charted places. A newly chosen hub becomes safe, gets a bind stone, and gets a station when it already had one or `hubHasStation(danger, isStarter, stationSeed(region.id, id))` hits. An existing hub is kept as is. It returns the hub rows.
- `settleRegionServices(tx, regionId, hubs)` runs `ensureRegionServices` per hub. Every vendor or banker at a non-hub place of the region becomes `lore`, keeping its name, description and greeting.
- `writeRegionStart`:
  - starter arrival: safe, hub, station and bind stone;
  - other arrivals: safe, but no hub, no station and no bind stone.
- `writeRegionFill`: step 4b places the hubs (with reply marks) before the families step; step 8 is `settleRegionServices`; the boundary anchors on places still non-safe after 4b.
- `failWorldFill`: `placeRegionHubs` with no marks, then `settleRegionServices`. A missing region or start location skips this.
- `reuseStarterRegion`: home is the region's lowest-id charted hub, else today's rule.
