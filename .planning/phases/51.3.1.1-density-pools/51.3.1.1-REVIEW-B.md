## Summary

**Scope.** The hunks from b00ed36c..HEAD in the 55 listed files. To check behaviour across files, these call chains were followed into files outside the list:
- `helpers/pools.ts`: `settlePoolKills`, `createPool`;

- `helpers/llm_budget.ts`: `reserveLlmBudget`, `settleLlmCost`;
- `data/density_rules.ts`: the hub, family, feud and seed rules.

**Tests.** The 31 test files in the list pass (2398 tests, `npx vitest run`). No test file makes a paid call. The only `fetch` and `process.env` mentions in `scripts/llm/*.test.mjs` are guard assertions.

**Prompt text.** A script compared the shipped `WORLD_GEN_START_BLOCK`, `WORLD_GEN_BLOCK` (A1 with the three Revision 2 edits applied) and `REGION_ECONOMY_BLOCK` with `51.3.1.1-PROMPT-DRAFT.md`. All three match byte for byte. These volatile lines also match the approved wording:
- the Hubs, Families and Feud lines, including `Feud: none.`;
- the B2a and B2b family lines;
- the C rumour line and the R2-C history line;
- the D-75 line.

What holds up:
- **Enemy-side abilities (D-53).**
  - A heal or shield always picks an ally enemy (`pickAllyTarget`), and a wind-up stores it in `combat_enemy_cast.targetEnemyId`.
  - Drain and execute hit the player, and drain heals only the caster's own row, re-read before the write.
  - Taunt and cc are skipped for enemies.
  - The enemy `ownerCharacterId` hazard is avoided.
- **Enemy shields.** `absorbEnemyShield` sits in front of every player-sourced HP write in the two combat files:
  - ability damage through `applyDamageToEnemy`;
  - the bard pulse;
  - pet auto-attacks and player auto-attacks through `resolveAttack.absorb`.
  - Threat still counts the whole hit.
  - The enemy DoT tick and `combat_perks.ts` are the documented exceptions (deferred row 1).
- **Fight start.**
  - `startCombat` is the single insert path.
  - Pool enemies (spawnId 0n) never touch `enemy_spawn`.
  - The origin columns are written once.
  - The role-template seed is `poolSeed(ts, combatId, index)`, so it is deterministic.
- **Kill depletion.** Kills are settled before any `combat_enemy` row is deleted, and `resetSpawnAfterCombat` skips pool enemies.
- **World generation.**
  - Hub, station, family and feud seeds use no timestamp. The fill request and the write compute the same `hubCount` and `feudCount`: `feudCountFor` clamps 2-3 by `min(families, …)`, and the kept count is at least 3.
  - Families are validated after the hub step, so a hub never hosts creatures.
  - Arrival safety follows D-61.
  - Vendors and bankers end up at hubs only.
- **Family validator.**
  - It reads no number from the reply.
  - Names are cleaned to 1-3 plain words and kept unique against the existing names.
  - Enums are clamped.
  - Only own properties are read, so `__proto__` cannot be followed.
  - Histories reject digits, markup, first person, the Keeper and instruction words.
- **Truncation retry.**
  - The retry happens only once, because the stored `stopReason` blocks a second one.
  - The truncated attempt is settled and charged.
  - The retry takes a fresh reservation through every budget gate, and a refused reservation ends the job.
- **Economy migration.** `migrateEconomyToFamilies` is idempotent.

The defects:
- **Raw quest creature name (CR-01, BLOCKER).** The D-74 invented-target path writes the model's quest creature name raw into a public family name and into later prompts.
- **Gear left out of a family reply (WR-01).** It is reported but never filled.
- **Region of a kill quest (WR-02).** Kill quests are resolved from where the player stands when the reply applies, not where the NPC stands.
- **Pool depletion (WR-03).** It can be applied twice when cleanup fails part way.
- **Wind-up heals (WR-04).** A wind-up heal on an ally that is back to full health silently does nothing.

## Critical Issues

### CR-01: A model-invented kill target's name is stored raw and becomes a public family name and prompt text

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/llm_apply.ts:792-850` (also `:804`, `:824`, `:830`)

**Issue:** For a kill or kill_loot offer whose target is not pooled, the apply does the following with `effect.targetEnemyName` exactly as the NPC reply wrote it:
- inserts it as `enemy_template.name`;
- links that template at `character.locationId`;
- calls `familyOfOne`, which builds the family name and nouns from it with `nounsFromTemplateName`. That function only splits on whitespace and pluralizes the last word; it does no cleaning.

The NPC reply is not schema-checked. `targetEnemyName` sits next to player-authored text in the same model turn, so a player can steer it. Nothing caps its length, strips digits or symbols, or even checks that it is a string. From there it reaches:
- **Public tables:** `creature_family.name` and the `pool_level.name` mirror, which every client reads in Nearby and the density lines (D-03).
- **Other players' NPC prompts:** the "Enemies in the area" line (`getNearbyEnemyContext` reads `pool_level.name`), escaped only for `<`/`>` by `w()`.
- **The late-family `region_economy` prompt** (`startFamilyLoot`), as world data.

This breaks the phase rule that names from hostile input are never accepted (D-46, "AI names, server stats"). Every other AI name in the phase goes through `cleanName` / `cleanItemName` and a name book. Player text is also laundered into world data, which other routes trust and other players receive.

A non-string value (a number or an object) gets past `resolveKillQuestTarget` (it calls `String()`) and then makes `enemy_template.insert` throw. The whole reply apply fails: `applyStored` retries it and ends in `apply_error`, so the NPC's dialogue is lost. The same raw-name pattern remains in the `boss_kill` branch (`:967`), but this phase rewrote the kill path and widened its reach.

**Fix:** Clean the name before resolving it, and skip the quest when it is unusable:
```ts
const rawTarget = typeof effect.targetEnemyName === 'string' ? effect.targetEnemyName : '';
const targetName = cleanItemName(rawTarget); // 1-3 plain words, letters only, '' when unusable
if ((questType === 'kill' || questType === 'kill_loot') && rawTarget !== '' && targetName === '') {
  console.log(`offer_quest "${questName}": unusable target name; quest skipped`);
  continue;
}
const resolved = resolveKillQuestTarget(ctx, questPlaceId, targetName);
// ... invent path: name: targetName, and make it unique (as the family_validate NameBook does)
```
Apply the same cleaning to the `boss_kill` insert at `:967`. Add tests for a numeric, a 200-character, a `<tag>` and an "Ignore previous instructions" target name.

## Warnings

### WR-01: Gear the family reply left out is reported as "rule fallback" but never written, so members lose their gear for good

**Files:**
- `spacetimedb/src/helpers/region_economy_validate.ts:113-117, 494-497`
- `spacetimedb/src/helpers/region_economy.ts:698-737`

**Issue:** `validateFamilyEntry` collects `missingGearFor`, which its doc comment describes as "the apply writes rule gear for them". The apply never reads that field: no production code does (checked with grep). `writeFamily` gives such a member `aiLootTable(..., gearId: 0n)`, so it gets a loot table without gear. Because the member now has `enemy_loot_entry` rows, `lacksLoot` is false from then on. No late-family job, and no rule pass, will ever give it gear.

This contradicts:
- D-47: one piece of gear per role member;
- PROMPT-DRAFT B3: the server "fills anything missing from the rule-based fallbacks".

A region reply that skips the support and caster gear (a likely way to save tokens with 7 families) leaves those members with no gear permanently. `region_economy.test.ts:1500` pins the current behaviour, so the test encodes the gap instead of catching it.

**Fix:** Inside `writeFamily`, before the loot loop, write rule gear for each member in `entry.missingGearFor` that still lacks loot. Do it the way `writeRuleFamily` does: validate a stub entry `{ member: ref }` so the validator's rule name and `repairGear` apply. Then put its id in `gearIds`. Update the test at `:1500` to expect a gear entry.

### WR-02: D-74 resolves the quest from where the player stands when the reply lands, not from the NPC's place

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/llm_apply.ts:792, 824, 830`

**Issue:** Three things use `character.locationId` at apply time:
- `resolveKillQuestTarget`, which walks `placesByHops` and stays inside the start place's region;
- the invented template's link;
- `familyOfOne`.

The `talk_to_npc` reducer only checks the player is at the NPC's place when the job is enqueued. The apply runs after the paid call, seconds later, and the player may have moved meanwhile, possibly into another region. The kill quest then gets:
- a target pool, and a family of one, near the player's new place;
- possibly a pool in a different region from the quest giver, which breaks D-74 ("in the region");
- a `targetLocationId` that does not match what the NPC said.

**Fix:** Resolve from the quest giver's place: `const questPlaceId = npc.locationId;`. Use it in `resolveKillQuestTarget`, in the `location_enemy_template` link and in `familyOfOne`. If the place is gone, skip the quest.

### WR-03: Pool kills can be settled twice when cleanup fails after settling

**Files:**
- `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat.ts:484-491` (settle)
- `:555-557` (`combat_enemy` deletes)
- `:2864-2872` (`closeFightAfterFailure`)
- `:3026-3029` and `:3037-3040`

**Issue:** `clearCombatArtifacts` settles the kills first. It deletes the `combat_enemy` rows near its end, after:
- the round tick cancel;
- the participant, pet and character updates;
- `endCombatCooldowns`;
- the aggro deletes.

When anything in between throws inside `handleVictory` or `handleDefeat`, `isolateRoundStep` swallows the error and keeps the writes made so far, including the pool depletion. `closeFightAfterFailure` then calls `clearCombatArtifacts` again. The killed `combat_enemy` rows still exist, so `settlePoolKills` depletes the same pools a second time.

Pools are shared world state (D-17). A double depletion can push a family to Wiped out, which starts a vacuum surge (D-20) that never really happened.

**Fix:** Make settlement one-shot per enemy row. In `settlePoolKills`, after the points are counted, zero each row's pool link:
```ts
// pools.ts settlePoolKills, inside the loop over killed pool enemies
ctx.db.combat_enemy.id.update({ ...enemy, poolId: 0n });
```
Alternatively, move the `combat_enemy` delete loop directly after the settle call. Add a fault test that throws once in `endCombatCooldowns` and checks the pool drops only once.

### WR-04: A wind-up heal whose stored ally is back to full health does nothing, with no fizzle line, while the card still says "Healing X"

**Files:**
- `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat.ts:2291-2300` (`landEnemyCasts` keeps the stored ally while it lives)
- `spacetimedb/src/helpers/combat.ts:581-582`

**Issue:** `landEnemyCasts` keeps the stored ally for as long as it is alive, whatever its HP. It writes `healTargetEnemyId` for the card and calls `executeAbilityAction`.

In `resolveAbility`, `namedAlly()` returns that ally. Because it is non-null, the `?? mostHurtAlly()` fallback never runs. The ally is now at full health, so the function returns at `if (!ally || ally.currentHp >= ally.maxHp) return;` and logs nothing.

The cast was announced ("begins to cast X"), the enemy's turn and cooldown are spent, and nothing visible happens. Two other paths show "fizzles" and clear the card: a lost target, and a re-pick with nobody hurt. This path does neither.

**Fix:** In `landEnemyCasts`, for a heal, keep the stored ally only while `storedAlly.currentHp < storedAlly.maxHp`; otherwise re-pick (or fizzle) through `pickEnemyTarget`. Also make `resolveAbility` fall back when the named ally is full:
```ts
const named = namedAlly();
const ally = named && named.currentHp < named.maxHp ? named : mostHurtAlly();
```

## Info

### IN-01: A skipped invented quest leaves orphan rows, and its link can block later quests

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/llm_apply.ts:801-842`

**Issue:** The template and its `location_enemy_template` link at `character.locationId` (usually a safe hub) are inserted before the pool check. On `!pooledThere`, or when `familyOfOne` throws, the quest is skipped but the template, the link and possibly a pool-less family remain.

Through that link, `resolveKillQuestTarget` step 3 then returns null for every later quest naming the same creature.

**Fix:** Insert nothing until `resolveKillQuestTarget` has returned `invent`. Drop the link at the quest place, since `familyOfOne` already links at the pool place. Delete the template and family if the pool check fails.

### IN-02: The D-75 line is tied to a cap that only happens to be 1

**Files:**
- `spacetimedb/src/data/llm_layers.ts:927-937`
- `spacetimedb/src/reducers/npc_interaction.ts:73`

**Issue:** "has no unfinished task with this player" is printed when `activeQuestFromThisNpc === false`. That value is `getActiveQuestCountForNpc >= MAX_QUESTS_PER_NPC`. It means "this NPC has no unfinished task" only while `MAX_QUESTS_PER_NPC === 1`. Raising the cap would make the line false.

**Fix:** Pass a separate `hasOpenTaskFromThisNpc: count > 0n` field and key the D-75 line on it.

### IN-03: The Feud line disappears when the Families line prints but the stored feudCount is missing or invalid

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/data/llm_layers.ts:785-791`

**Issue:** The approved block says "the Feud line of the user message says how many families...". In that state the model gets a Families line but no Feud line. Only a malformed or partially stored input can reach it.

**Fix:** When `familiesLine` prints, fall back to `Feud: none.`.

### IN-04: `region_economy` budget headroom is thin at the design cap

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/data/llm_tuning.ts:384` (`region_economy` 4096) with `spacetimedb/src/data/economy_rules.ts` `ECONOMY_DESIGN_FAMILIES_MAX = 7`

**Issue:** At the design cap, one reply holds:
- 7 families, each with a drop, a trophy and up to 4 gear pieces with 1-2 sentence descriptions;
- 3 gatherables;
- 3 recipes.

That is roughly 3.5k output tokens. `region_economy` is not in `LLM_TRUNCATION_RETRY_ROUTES`, so a truncated reply fails the whole region design after a paid call. The route is marked `insufficient_data`.

**Fix:** Measure it at the next paid run. Until then, consider 6144 tokens or a design cap of 5.

### IN-05: The model can give contradictory relations

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/world_gen.ts:514-522`

**Issue:** Each AI relation is stored together with its inverse. "A: B prey" plus "B: A prey" stores both prey and predator in each direction. `vacuumCandidates` tolerates this, but the relation graph becomes ambiguous for Phase 52.4.

**Fix:** In `validateFamilies`, drop a relation whose inverse was already recorded for the pair.

### IN-06: `startCombat` accepts an empty draw

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat.ts:202-230`

**Issue:** The exported `startCombat` inserts an encounter and participants even when `drawn` is empty. The next round then resolves at once as a victory. Today's callers guard against this (`startPoolFight`, `startCombatForSpawn`), but the function itself does not.

**Fix:** Return null early when `drawn.length === 0`.

### IN-07: Unused imports after the careful pull was removed

**File:** `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat.ts:7-8`

**Issue:** `statOffset` and `WIS_PULL_BONUS_PER_POINT` are imported but no longer used. Only a comment mentions the second one.

**Fix:** Remove both from the import list.
