Facts (verified 2026-10-08):
- `helpers/world_gen.ts` ~L790-866 shows how a region-fill enemy is written:
  - `enemy_template.insert` with role, roleDetail, abilityProfile, terrainTypes, creatureType, timeOfDay 'any', socialGroup, socialRadius 0n, awareness 'normal', groupMin, groupMax, armorClass, level, maxHp, baseDamage, xpReward;
  - one `enemy_role_template` (roleKey, displayName, role, roleDetail, abilityProfile);
  - one `enemy_ability`;
  - links via `location_enemy_template` to each non-safe new location.
  - The base level is `dangerMultiplier / 100n`, clamped to at least 1. Stats come from `enemyStatsForLevel(level)`.
- Resources:
  - `helpers/location.ts` `getGatherableResourceTemplates(ctx, terrainType, timePref, zoneTier, regionId)` (~L67) returns `{ template, weight, timeOfDay }[]`, including the region's AI gatherables (`regionalGatherEntries`).
  - `zoneTier` from `dangerMultiplier`: under 130n is 1, under 190n is 2, else 3 (`spawnResourceNode` ~L175).
  - Item pins: `loadItemPins`, `pinPct`, `scaleWeights` (used in `spawnResourceNode`). Pin 0 removes an entry.
  - Modifier reagents come from `CRAFTING_MODIFIER_DEFS`. Regional gatherable rarity is `economy_item.rarity`; the static terrain list is common.
- Exclusions:
  - Named enemies: `named_enemy.enemyTemplateId`.
  - Quest targets: `quest_template.targetEnemyTemplateId` with `questType` 'boss_kill'. kill and kill_loot stay ordinary or become a family of one (D-54).
  - Boss: `enemy_template.isBoss`.
  - World event enemies: `data/world_event_data.ts` `enemyTemplateKey` entries (resolve by name).
- Name cleaners to reuse: `data/economy_design_rules.ts` `cleanItemName`, `nameKey`, `uniqueItemName`. Validator pattern: `helpers/region_economy_validate.ts` (header "never reads a number from the reply", the `isTaken` callback).
- The AI reply shape is PROMPT-DRAFT section A3: `families[]` with name, singularNoun, pluralNoun, creatureType, iconKey, temperament, ambushVerb, ambushRest, members[{ role, name }], fitLocations[], relations[{ family, kind }]; plus `arrival { shortName, placeNoun }` and per location `shortName`, `placeNoun`. The validator is written against this shape now and wired in Plan 23 after approval.
