## 2. Creature density lines (level x temperament, 12 lines)

Source: `spacetimedb/src/data/density_lines.ts` `CREATURE_LINES`, built by `creatureLine`. Shown on the Nearby family card, the look text and the family examine. `{Plural}` is the sentence-case plural, `{plural}` lowercase, `{singular}` lowercase, `{place}` the place noun (section 3).

| Level | aggressive | wary | skittish |
|-------|------------|------|----------|
| 3 Overrun | `{Plural} swarm {place}, and every last {singular} has noticed you.` | `{Plural} crowd {place}, watching every way in and out.` | `{Plural} boil out of every crack in {place}, jumpy and everywhere.` |
| 2 Stable | `{Plural} patrol {place}, in no hurry to be anywhere else.` | `{Plural} keep to {place}, watching you from a distance.` | `{Plural} dart about {place}, never still for long.` |
| 1 Scarce | `A few {plural} still prowl {place}, thinned out and short-tempered.` | `A few {plural} linger in {place}, watching you nervously.` | `You catch sight of one {singular} in {place}. Then you don't.` |
| 0 Wiped out | `No {plural} are left in {place}. The quiet feels borrowed.` | `The {plural} are gone from {place}.` | `Not one {singular} stirs in {place}.` |

**Deliberate change from the UI-SPEC (Plan 02):** the UI-SPEC's Overrun aggressive line read `{Plural} swarm {place}, and every one of them has noticed you.` It ships as `... and every last {singular} has noticed you.` so the line keeps the no-pronoun rule for creatures. Example: `Goblins swarm the orchard, and every last goblin has noticed you.`

An unknown temperament reads the wary row.
