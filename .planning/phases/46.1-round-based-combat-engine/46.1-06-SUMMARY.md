# Phase 46.1 Plan 06: Round choices, early resolution and flee Summary

Players now choose their action for the open round (ability, ready attack or flee) through validated reducers, the round resolves the moment everyone waited on has chosen, and flee runs under the old quick-123 odds as a real round choice.

## What changed

- `reducers/combat.ts`
  - `submitCombatChoice(ctx, character, choice)` after `resolveRound`, exposed as `deps.submitCombatChoice`. Checks in order: active combat, active living participant ('You cannot act right now.'), known action type, then for an ability: exists, owned, level, not utility (at-peace line), round cooldown ('<name> is on cooldown for N more round(s).'), mana or stamina (stamina_free honoured). Targets: an ally must be an active, living participant of the same fight ('That target is not in this fight.'); an enemy must be a living enemy of the same fight ('That enemy is not in this fight.'), and a valid enemy becomes `combatTargetEnemyId`. Every refusal is a `failCombat` line and writes nothing. A valid choice goes through `ensureRound` and `upsertChoice` (one row per player per round, replaced in place), posts 'You ready <ability>.' / 'You ready an attack.' / 'You attempt to flee...' (+ group line), and when `allWaitingChosen` it calls `resolveRound`, which cancels the pending tick and opens the next round.
  - `submit_combat_action({ characterId, abilityTemplateId?, targetEnemyId?, targetCharacterId? })`: `requireCharacterOwnedBy`, `lastActivityAt`, then the shared path (ability when an id is given, else auto_attack).
  - `flee_combat` now only stores a flee choice. `resolveFleeChoice` runs in the player step of `resolveRound`: chance = `calculateFleeChance(region.dangerMultiplier, default 100)`, roll = `roundSeed(now + id * 13, N) % 100`. Success deletes the character's aggro rows (pet entries included), its pets in the fight, clears the enemy aggro pointer and the target, sets `lastCombatEndAt`, carries round cooldowns back to wall clock (`endCombatCooldowns`), deletes its choices and its participant row, and posts 'You successfully flee.' (+ group). Failure posts 'You fail to flee!' (+ group) and the player does nothing else that round.
  - The enemy phase of `resolveRound` is skipped when no participant row is left; step (g) then ends the fight as a defeat with an empty participant list.
  - `tick_casts`: a finished cast of a character in an active fight is deleted with 'Your casting is interrupted by combat.' and nothing runs; out-of-combat casts are unchanged.
- `reducers/items.ts` `use_ability`: in combat, utility keeps its refusal, then `deps.submitCombatChoice(ctx, character, { actionType: 'ability', ... })` and return. The wall-clock cooldown check moved below that branch (out of combat only); the unreachable in-combat participant check and enemy-name block are gone. The out-of-combat path is otherwise unchanged.
- `helpers/travel.ts`: group auto-join writes `nextAutoAttackAt: 0n` and calls `beginCombatCooldowns`.
- `index.ts`: `submitCombatChoice: null as any` in `reducerDeps`.
- `combat_choices.integration.test.ts` (new, 39 tests, strict mock): solo and two-player early resolution, replacement, late choice with a stale old tick, orphan fight, every rejection, ownership, cooldown source by place, flee success and failure (solo and group, group lines, no 'fleeing' status), joiner through `move_character`, cast cancellation and out-of-combat cast.

## Deviations from Plan

None that change scope. Notes:

- The out-of-combat "executes instantly" test uses a stamina ability, because any mana ability gets a minimum cast time (existing `abilityCastMicros` rule) and takes the cast path.
- The real-scheduler race statement (a tick racing a choice transaction) is a `backstop` item: it cannot run under the mock. The mock tests cover the sequential case (old tick after early resolution changes nothing, and tick cancellation); on the platform the transaction ordering plus `isStaleTick` and the open-round state check in `resolveRound` make a second resolution of the same round a no-op.

## Notes for later plans

- Phase 48 choice contract: `combat_action` holds at most one row per (combatId, characterId, roundNumber), only for the open round. `actionType` is `ability` (with `abilityTemplateId`), `auto_attack` or `flee` (no ability id). `targetEnemyId` is also the player's current target; `targetCharacterId` is the ally. No row for an active participant means "will auto-attack". A successful flee removes that player's `combat_participant` row.
- The generated client bindings do not yet list `submit_combat_action`; 46.1-09 regenerates them.
- `scheduleCombatTick` is still destructured in `reducers/combat.ts` and `index.ts` (46.1-08 removes it).
