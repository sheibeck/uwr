## Artifacts this phase produces (consolidated, for Phase 48)

**Round and choice contract for Phase 48** (all tables below are public unless marked private; subscribe by `combatId`):

- `combat_round` (index `by_combat`): `id` (autoInc, not ordered; never sort by it), `combatId`, `roundNumber` (u64, starts at 1, +1 per resolution), `state` (`action_select` while choices are open; `resolved` once done; `resolving` exists only inside the resolving transaction and is never seen by a client), `timerExpiresAtMicros` (u64 µs since the Unix epoch; the deadline, `startedAtMicros + 10 s`), `narrationCount` (big moments narrated so far in the fight, 0 to 3), `startedAtMicros` (u64 µs; when the round opened). Lifecycle: round 1 is inserted in the transaction that starts the fight; when a round resolves (deadline tick, or the last waited-on player's choice) it becomes `resolved` and round N+1 is inserted in the same transaction; exactly one row per active fight is `action_select`; at the fight's end every row of the fight is deleted, so a client that groups the feed by round must keep the boundaries it has seen. Feed mapping: an event with `createdAt` t belongs to the round N with `startedAtMicros(N) < t <= startedAtMicros(N+1)`; events at exactly round 1's `startedAtMicros` are the fight's opening lines.
- `combat_action` (indexes `by_combat`, `by_character`): the open round's choices only. `combatId`, `characterId`, `roundNumber` (= the open round), `actionType` (`ability` | `auto_attack` | `flee`), `abilityTemplateId` (only for `ability`), `targetEnemyId` (the enemy picked with the choice; also the character's current target), `targetCharacterId` (ally for heals and buffs, always an active participant of the same fight), `submittedAt`. At most one row per (combatId, characterId, roundNumber); a new choice updates it in place; rows of a resolved round are deleted in the same transaction that opens the next round. No row for an active participant means "will auto-attack the current target, else the lowest-id living enemy". Party members can read each other's rows.
- Waiting rule: a round waits only for participants with `status = 'active'` whose character HP is above 0, and never beyond the deadline. A successful flee deletes that player's `combat_participant` row.
- Resolution order: players in ascending character id (choice, else auto-attack; a failed ability choice falls back to auto-attack), pets in ascending pet id, enemies in ascending enemy id (a stunned or winding-up enemy takes no action), wind-up landings in ascending enemy id, then effects and DoT/HoT tick once, round cooldowns drop by 1, adds arrive, deaths are marked, targets of dead enemies move to the lowest-id living enemy, then victory or defeat (end narration with the final round number) or one big-moment check and the next round.
- `ability_cooldown.roundsRemaining` (u64, default 0): while in combat, rounds still blocked including the current one (0 or no row = choosable now); an ability used in round N with cooldown C rounds is choosable again from round N + C; `startedAtMicros`/`durationMicros` are an estimate (`roundsRemaining x 10 s`) so the Phase 47 hotbar sweep stays roughly truthful. Out of combat `roundsRemaining` is 0 and the wall-clock fields rule.
- `character_effect.roundsRemaining` and `combat_enemy_effect.roundsRemaining`: rounds left; they tick at the end of each round; an enemy stun is `effectType 'stun'`, `magnitude 1`, `roundsRemaining` = rounds left.
- `combat_enemy_cast` (wind-up warning, CMB-02): a row exists from the round an enemy announces a cast (`announcedRound`) until the end of the round it lands (`landsAtRound`), or until it is interrupted (stun) or the enemy dies; `abilityKey` (join `enemy_ability` by the enemy's template for the name), `targetCharacterId` / `targetPetId`, `endsAtMicros` (estimate).
- `combat_enemy_cooldown.readyAtRound`: first round the enemy may use that ability again. `combat_pending_add.arriveAtRound`: the round at whose end the add joins.
- `combat_narrative` (`roundNumber`, `narrativeType` in `kill` | `near_death` | `phase` | `victory` | `defeat`) and `event_private` rows of kind `combat_narration` (speaker segments) arrive asynchronously, possibly after the round resolved, and are dropped by the executor when older than 20 s.
- Reducers: `use_ability({ characterId, abilityTemplateId, targetCharacterId? })` in combat records an ability choice; `submit_combat_action({ characterId, abilityTemplateId?, targetEnemyId?, targetCharacterId? })` records a ready (auto-attack) choice or an ability choice; `flee_combat({ characterId })` records a flee; `set_combat_target` unchanged; the realtime ability reducer is removed.
- Private, never in bindings: `combat_moment` (once-per-fight moment flags and the 3-per-fight budget), `round_timer_tick` (one scheduled tick per active fight), `combat_loop_tick` (kept defined, drained).
- Still not exposed (Phase 48 scoping, unchanged here): `aggro_entry` is private, so the threat order for CMB-02 needs its own small addition in Phase 48.
