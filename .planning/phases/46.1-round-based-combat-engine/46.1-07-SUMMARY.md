# Phase 46.1 Plan 07: Enemy wind-ups, pets and adds in rounds Summary

Every non-player actor now counts rounds: enemy abilities wind up across rounds and cool down in rounds, pets act once per round, pulled adds arrive after a number of rounds, and `resolve_pull` ignores any caller other than the module.

## What changed

- `reducers/combat.ts`
  - `tryEnemyAbilityForRound(..., nowMicros, roundNumber)`: no internal stun check (resolveRound skips stunned enemies first); a candidate is skipped while `!enemyAbilityReady(row.readyAtRound, roundNumber)`, ready rows are deleted; the AI roll is `roundSeed(nowMicros + enemy.id + combat.id, roundNumber) % 100`. A chosen ability with `cooldownSeconds > 0` writes `{ readyAtRound: N + cooldownRounds(cooldownSeconds), readyAtMicros: now + rounds x 10 s (estimate) }`. When `windupRounds(castSeconds) > 0` it inserts a `combat_enemy_cast` row (`announcedRound N`, `landsAtRound N + W`, `endsAtMicros` estimate, target) and posts '<enemy> begins to cast <ability>.' to every active participant; otherwise it executes at once as before.
  - `resolveRound` enemy step: a stunned enemy deletes its cast row(s) with the 'is interrupted.' line and skips; an enemy with a pending cast takes no other action. New landing step `landEnemyCasts` right after the enemy step: casts with `landsAtRound <= roundNumber`, ascending enemy id; a dead or missing enemy (or an ability row that no longer exists) drops the row silently; the stored target must be an active, living participant, or a living pet of this fight, else `pickEnemyTarget(ability.targetRule, ...)` retargets, else '<enemy>'s <ability> fizzles.' is posted; each landing is in try/catch and the cast row is always deleted (finally). Helpers `postToActiveParticipants` and `castAbilityName` added.
  - `processPetCombat(ctx, combat, livingEnemies, roundNumber, nowMicros)`: pets sorted by id, owner dead or missing removes the pet, target = own living target, else the owner's current living target, else lowest-id living enemy (no target: skip with no write); ability via `petAbilityDue` in try/catch, one auto-attack per round through `resolveAttack` seeded by `roundSeed`, aggro gain as before, and only `targetEnemyId` is written back. All pet timer reads and writes and the retry interval are gone from this function.
  - `processPendingAdds(..., roundNumber)`: an add joins when `arriveAtRound <= roundNumber`; the loop iterates a copy of the rows.
  - `resolve_pull`: module-identity guard as the first statement; a partial outcome stores `arriveAtRound: N + PULL_ADD_DELAY_ROUNDS - 1n` (N = the fight current round, default 1) and an `arriveAtMicros` estimate; both messages now read '... will arrive in 2 rounds.' (add/adds wording and reason suffix kept).
- `combat_actors.integration.test.ts` (new, 27 tests, strict mock, real captured reducers): 1- and 2-round wind-ups, instant ability, announcement to every participant, stun interrupt, death before landing, retarget (character and dead pet target), fizzle, pre-phase cast row, enemy cooldown (round 1 use, not ready round 2, ready round 3, far-future clock does not free it, wind-up starts cooldown, cooldown 0 writes nothing, no-ability enemy), pet cadence (rounds 1 and 4), no pet timer writes, ordering players then pets (ascending id) then enemies, dead-owner pet removal, four target rules, pending add by round and pre-phase row, resolve_pull forged call and partial pull in rounds, row shapes checked with `rowColumnProblems`.
- `combat_round_clock.invariant.test.ts`: the scripted fight now also runs a caster (Bolt, 2 s cast, 8 s cooldown), a pet with a 10 s ability and a pending add under the two clocks (identical state, round by round, with the clocks hours apart), plus the static guard: for `processPendingAdds`, `processPetCombat`, `tryEnemyAbilityForRound`, `processEnemyAutoAttackForRound`, `processPlayerAutoAttackForRound`, `tickEffectsForRound`, `resolveRound` and `submitCombatChoice` the comment-stripped body has no comparison operator next to `readyAtMicros`, `endsAtMicros`, `arriveAtMicros`, `nextAutoAttackAt`, `nextAbilityAt` or `durationMicros`; every body slice is checked non-empty, and the detector itself is tested on six violating and four clean sample lines.

## Deviations from Plan

None that change scope. Notes:

- `helpers/combat_fight_fixture.ts` was listed in `files_modified` but did not need a change: the fixture's existing `extra` option carried the `enemy_ability`, `active_pet`, `combat_pending_add`, `combat_enemy_cast` and `enemy_spawn` rows.
- The two-clock invariant cannot run the enemy AI on literally identical timestamps: the 50% ability roll is seeded by the timestamp (a random seed, not a deadline). Each clock nudges its per-round timestamps by a few microseconds so the roll passes in every round; the clocks stay hours apart. The guard test and the nudge are documented in the test file.
- In the pet test, the enemy goes for the pets once they hold aggro (the pet's first hit creates its aggro entry), so the ordering assertion accepts a strike on a pet as the enemy turn.
- `RETRY_ATTACK_INTERVAL` in `reducers/combat.ts` is now unused (it was only the pet retry delay); it was left in place to keep the change minimal.

## Contracts for later plans

- Wind-up (Phase 48 CMB-02): a `combat_enemy_cast` row exists from the round the enemy announces a cast (`announcedRound`) until the end of the round it lands (`landsAtRound`), or until a stun interrupts it or the enemy dies. `abilityKey` joins `enemy_ability` by the enemy's template for the name; `targetCharacterId` / `targetPetId` are the intended target; `endsAtMicros` is an estimate.
- Enemy cooldown: `combat_enemy_cooldown.readyAtRound` is the first round the enemy may use that ability again.
- Add: `combat_pending_add.arriveAtRound` is the round at whose end the add joins.
- A cast's stored target is only a statement of intent: it is re-validated at landing and may retarget or fizzle.
