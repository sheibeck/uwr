Moment per-call text (data/llm_layers.ts, new private buildCombatMomentVolatile; N = String(events.roundNumber); S = render(events.momentSubject)), in this line order:
1. first line by kind:
   - kill (M1): `A moment in the fight, round {N}: {S} has just fallen. Narrate this one beat; the fight is not over.`
   - near_death (M4): `A moment in the fight, round {N}: {S} has been driven below a fifth of full health. Narrate this one beat; the fight is not over.`
   - phase (M5): `A moment in the fight, round {N}: {S} has been wounded past the halfway mark and the fight turns. Narrate this one beat; the fight is not over.`
2. kill and momentFirst (M2): `It is the first death of the fight.`
3. kill and momentBossOrNamed (M3): `{S} is a named foe, the most dangerous one here.`
4. context lines (M8, same text and rules as buildCombatOutroVolatile): `Setting: ...` when locationName; `Enemies faced: ...` when enemyNames; `Your character (address as you, never by name): ...` for one player or `Your party (address together as you): ...` for several; `Survivors: ...` listing living player names only (no HP numbers)
5. kill with a playerActions entry (M6a then M6): `The killing blow:` then `- {WHO} used {ABILITY} on {S}, dealing {D} damage` for an ability or `- {WHO} auto-attacked {S} for {D} damage` for an auto-attack; drop the damage clause when D is missing or 0
6. when an ability name is present: an empty line, then the existing allowlist line `IMPORTANT: Use ONLY these exact ability names in your narration: {names}. Do NOT invent or rename abilities.`
7. an empty line, then (M7): `Reply with the segments JSON object: the Keeper's narration of this one moment only, in the second person, two sentences at most.`

RESEARCH's M4 said "below a fifth of their health". It is reworded to "below a fifth of full health" because the owner's pronoun rule forbids a singular they for the player.
