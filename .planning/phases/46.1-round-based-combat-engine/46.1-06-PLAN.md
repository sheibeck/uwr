<objective>
Give players the round-choice input: `use_ability` in combat records an ability choice, the new `submit_combat_action` records a plain attack ("ready") or an ability, `flee_combat` records a flee, and the round resolves the moment every waited-on player has chosen. Validate ownership and targets, resolve flee under the existing rules, make mid-fight joiners wait-able, and stop pre-fight casts from firing outside the round order.

Purpose: RND-01 (early resolution; the solo player never waits), RND-02 (no choice means auto-attack, already in 46.1-05; an explicit ready choice ends the round early), RND-04 (each player's chosen action in the public `combat_action` row). Also closes the pre-existing target hole and the unresolved `fleeing` status (CONTEXT fixes in scope).

Output: the shared choice path and reducers, the use_ability branch, the joiner and cast changes, and their integration tests.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| client to choice reducers | use_ability, submit_combat_action and flee_combat take a characterId and optional ability and target ids from the client |
| one player's transaction to the shared fight | A choice can resolve the whole party's round inside the choosing player's transaction |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-46.1-06-02 | Elevation | targetCharacterId | high | mitigate | Ally target must be an active living participant of the same fight (choice time here, re-checked at resolution in 46.1-05); cross-fight and no-fight tests |
