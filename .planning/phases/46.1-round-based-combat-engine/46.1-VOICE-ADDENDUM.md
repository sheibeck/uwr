# Phase 46.1 Voice Addendum: big-moment per-call text

Status: auto-approved overnight (owner instruction 2026-10-05), owner to review

## Scope

This addendum covers per-call user text only: the lines that `buildCombatMomentVolatile` in `spacetimedb/src/data/llm_layers.ts` sends to the Keeper when the round engine narrates one big moment (a kill, a player driven near death, or an enemy wounded past the halfway mark).

Not changed in Phase 46.1, and verified identical to commit 487b3068 by the voice gate: the Keeper Bible (`keeper_bible.ts`), the `combat_narration` route block (`llm_layers.ts` ROUTE_BLOCKS and `llm_routes.ts`), `COMBAT_NARRATION_SCHEMA` (`llm_schemas.ts`), and both snapshot directories. The victory, defeat and round per-call text is byte-identical to the base.

Voice rules the strings follow: the narration is second person; the Keeper never speaks in the first person; a lone player is addressed as "you" (the existing "address as you, never by name" line); no singular "they" or "it" for a player; no HP numbers; no pronoun for the subject, so a person or a beast both read correctly.

## Strings as shipped

`{N}` is the real round number. `{S}` is the moment subject: the fallen enemy, the player near death, or the enemy past half. Player names are wrapped by `wrapPlayerName`; enemy and ability names go through `w()`. `{WHO}` is the killer's tagged name, `{ABILITY}` the ability name, `{D}` the damage of the killing action.

| Id | Used where | String |
|----|------------|--------|
| M1 | First line of a kill moment | `A moment in the fight, round {N}: {S} has just fallen. Narrate this one beat; the fight is not over.` |
| M2 | Kill, added when it is the first death of the fight | `It is the first death of the fight.` |
| M3 | Kill, added when the fallen enemy is a boss or a named foe | `{S} is a named foe, the most dangerous one here.` |
| M4 | First line of a near-death moment | `A moment in the fight, round {N}: {S} has been driven below a fifth of full health. Narrate this one beat; the fight is not over.` |
| M5 | First line of a phase-change moment | `A moment in the fight, round {N}: {S} has been wounded past the halfway mark and the fight turns. Narrate this one beat; the fight is not over.` |
| M6a | Kill with a known killer, header of the action list | `The killing blow:` |
| M6 | Kill, the action line (ability, then auto-attack); the damage clause is dropped when the damage is missing or 0 | `- {WHO} used {ABILITY} on {S}, dealing {D} damage` and `- {WHO} auto-attacked {S} for {D} damage` |
| M6b | Kill with an ability (reused unchanged from the round text), after an empty line | `IMPORTANT: Use ONLY these exact ability names in your narration: {names}. Do NOT invent or rename abilities.` |
| M7 | Closing line of every moment, after an empty line | `Reply with the segments JSON object: the Keeper's narration of this one moment only, in the second person, two sentences at most.` |
| M8 | Context lines of every moment (reused unchanged from the outro; `Survivors` lists living player names only) | `Setting: ...`, `Enemies faced: ...`, `Your character (address as you, never by name): ...` or `Your party (address together as you): ...`, `Survivors: ...` |

Line order of a kill: M1, M2 when first, M3 when named, M8, M6a with M6, an empty line and M6b when an ability was used, an empty line, M7. Near death and phase changes use M4 or M5 in place of M1 and have no M2, M3, M6a, M6 or M6b.

## One wording change from research

RESEARCH proposed M4 as "driven below a fifth of their health". It ships as "below a fifth of full health", because the owner's pronoun rule forbids a singular "they" for the player (a lone player is always "you").

## Budget

At most 3 big moments plus the end of the fight are narrated per encounter (`MAX_COMBAT_NARRATIONS`, enforced by the round resolver in 46.1-01 and 46.1-08). Every narration goes through the cap-exempt `combat_narration` route under the global refusals (halted, daily calls, daily cost, ceiling); a refusal is silent, an error is logged redacted and swallowed, and a round never waits for narration.
