### WR-01: A throw inside `resolveRound` strands the fight

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat_round_faults.integration.test.ts` (new)
**Commit:** f935744c
**Applied fix:** New `isolateRoundStep(combatId, label, step)` logs a throw with `console.error` (through `redactSecrets`) and returns false. It wraps each independent step: each player's turn (flee, ability, auto-attack, kill record), pets, each enemy's whole turn (the stun and cast reads were previously outside the existing try), wind-up landing, effect ticks, round cooldowns, pending adds, marking the fallen, retarget. `processPendingAdds` also isolates each add: a pending add whose template is gone no longer throws every round; its pending row is always consumed and its spawn released (only logged and skipped, no "social add arrives" line). `handleVictory` and `handleDefeat` run in an isolated step; on a throw `closeFightAfterFailure` still runs `clearCombatArtifacts` and marks the encounter `resolved`, each in its own isolated step. The round then always reaches the next-round decision and re-arms its tick. Writes a failed step made before it threw are kept (no savepoints), the same trade-off as the existing per-enemy and per-cast isolation. Tests (12): a missing-template pending add, a throwing whole pending-adds step (injected, also checks the secret is redacted from the log), a poisoned effect row (bigint/number mix in `tickEffectsForRound`, checks a later step still runs) all advance the round on the timer path and the early path (the player's choice is not rolled back); a flee whose resolution throws is not rolled back; a throwing victory and a throwing defeat path still close the fight (rows, ticks and encounter state); a control victory still pays out.
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (control-flow and state-consistency change; partial writes of a failed step are kept by design).
**Not done:** the review's optional suggestion to re-arm a tick in `ensureRound` for an open round whose deadline passed more than a round ago.

### WR-02: A player killed mid-round stays `active` and keeps soaking later enemy actions

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat_round_deaths.integration.test.ts` (new)
**Commit:** 9f425dba
**Applied fix:** New `livingActiveParticipants` (status `active` and character hp above 0). `pickEnemyTarget` filters through it first, which covers the enemy ability scoring, the wind-up landing retarget and the auto-attack in one place (the `lowest_hp`, `random`, `all_allies` and `aggro` rules all included). The enemy-turn `activeNow`, the `landEnemyCasts` active list and the adds' aggro target use it too. Death is still marked at the end of the round. Note that WR-04 removed the immediate-DoT-tick source of this window; the remaining real source is an enemy ability whose damage writes hp 0 without marking the participant, which is what the tests use. Tests: with two enemies, the first kills a 1-HP player with a `lowest_hp` Bolt and the second now hits the living player (before: it picked the corpse and lost its action); an aggro-targeted corpse is skipped for the living player; with every player at 0 HP enemies have no target.
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (targeting logic).

### WR-03: `markParticipantDead` can resurrect a dying player with an `hp_bonus` buff

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat_stale_death.integration.test.ts` (new)
**Commit:** 8bdcc4a2
**Applied fix:** `clearCharacterEffectsOnDeath` re-reads the character row per `hp_bonus` effect (`ctx.db.character.id.find(character.id) ?? character`) and writes from that row, so a stale pre-damage row cannot restore HP; `markParticipantDead` goes through it. Tests: an enemy strike that kills a player with an `hp_bonus` buff leaves hp 0 and maxHp reduced by the bonus (before: hp restored to 5); a lethal DoT tick does the same.
**Related fix in the same commit (not in the review):** in `tickEffectsForRound` the lethal-DoT branch called `markParticipantDead`, which deletes all of the character's effect rows, and the loop then went on to `character_effect.id.update` the deleted rows (the strict mock reproduced "update matched no row"; a real update of a missing row throws and would roll the round back). The loop now `break`s for that character after `markParticipantDead`. WR-01's isolation would have swallowed the throw, but at the cost of the rest of the effect ticks.
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (state logic).

### WR-04: Character DoTs tick twice in the round they are applied; enemy DoTs do not

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/combat.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/combat.test.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat_dot_symmetry.integration.test.ts` (new)
**Commit:** 1f0666f2
**Applied fix:** Dropped the immediate first tick for DoTs in `addCharacterEffect` (the leftover of the real-time loop). A DoT of D rounds now ticks exactly D times at the end of each round (`tickEffectsForRound`), counting the landing round as the first, on a player exactly as on an enemy. The enemy ability's own damage line already says "applies a burning effect", so no extra application message was added. Updated four existing `helpers/combat.test.ts` expectations that asserted the immediate tick (hp unchanged on application, no "You suffer" line on application). Tests pin the count both ways: identical seeded DoT rows (3 rounds) tick 3 times on the player and 3 on the enemy; an enemy `dot` ability landing in round 1 ticks D times in total (D = 1 for its 3 s duration; before the fix it ticked 2); a player `dot` ability on an enemy ticks D times (D = 3 for 9 s).
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (rule change; per-round tick values for players are now lower than before).
