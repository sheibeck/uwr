<objective>
Move every non-player actor onto rounds: enemy abilities get round cooldowns and real wind-ups (announced one round, landing at the end of a later round, cancelled by stun or death), pets act once per round, pulled adds arrive after a number of rounds, and the pull scheduler gets the module-identity guard. Close the companion invariant with a static guard that no round function compares a wall-clock field.

Purpose: RND-03 ("enemy abilities count in rounds", and ROADMAP success criterion 3: "an enemy wind-up announced in one round lands in a later round"). The wind-up rows are what Phase 48 reads for the wind-up warning (CMB-02).

Output: enemy, pet and add logic in reducers/combat.ts with an integration test file, and the extended invariant test.
</objective>
