# Phase 46.1 Plan 08: Big moments in rounds and removal of the real-time swing code Summary

resolveRound now picks at most one big moment per round (first kill, boss or named kill, near death, boss or named phase), records it as a private combat_moment row and enqueues its narration without ever being able to hold up, delay or roll back the round; the per-second swing scheduling and every constant, table and getter it left behind are gone.

## What changed

### Task 1: big moments (reducers/combat.ts)

- Helpers above `resolveRound`, all non-throwing (errors are logged with `console.error` only): `snapshotForMoments` (every enemy row of the fight including dead ones, plus the roster's characters, at round start), `livingHpMap` and `recordKillsBy` (killing-blow attribution), `momentCountOf`, and `recordBigMoment` (builds `EnemySnapshot` and `PlayerSnapshot` lists, runs `detectMoment`, inserts the `combat_moment` row, then calls `enqueueCombatMomentNarration`).
- `resolveRound`: snapshot after the roster is read; in the player step the living-enemy HP map is read before each action and `recordKillsBy` runs after it (ability name only when the ability path succeeded); `recordBigMoment` runs after the victory and defeat checks (both return first), before the round is marked resolved; the next round starts with `narrationCount` = the number of moment rows of the fight.
- Cap, one-per-round, once-per-subject and the priority order come from `detectMoment` (46.1-01); the budget comes from counting the fight's `combat_moment` rows, so a refused enqueue still counts and is never retried.
- `helpers/combat_fight_fixture.ts`: `isBoss` option (sets `enemy_template.isBoss` on the shared template).
- `combat_moments.integration.test.ts` (new, 18 tests, strict mock, real captured `resolve_round_timer`): first kill with killer line and `narrationCount` 1 in round 2, ability kill with ability name, DoT kill without killer line, boss kill, named foe (named_enemy row), plain kill not flagged, near death once (a second drop in round 2 fires nothing), named phase change, non-named enemy fires no phase, near death beats first kill in the same round, cap (3 rows: no row, no job, round resolves), 2 rows leave room for exactly one, final round (no moment row, one outro job with the round number), refused enqueue (`llm_admin_state: []`: row written, no job, nothing posted, round 2 open with its tick), a throwing helper (vi.mock with a hoisted switch: row written, round 2 open), and two source rules (call counts 1/1/2, row insert before the enqueue).
- Near death and phase tests use a character DoT and an enemy DoT so the HP movement does not depend on a hit roll; the plan wording "an enemy hit" is covered by the same crossing logic (snapshot at round start versus HP after the round).

## Contracts for later plans

- A moment lands later (asynchronously) as an `event_private` row of kind `combat_narration` with speaker segments plus a `combat_narrative` row whose `roundNumber` is the round it happened in; it may arrive after that round has resolved and is dropped by the executor if older than 20 s.
- `combat_round.narrationCount` of an open round equals the number of `combat_moment` rows of the fight (0 to 3).
- Boss and phase moments fire rarely until something sets `enemy_template.isBoss` or a player holds a `named_enemy` row for the template (accepted per CONTEXT); first kill and near death carry most fights.
