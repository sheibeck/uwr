<objective>
Turn the combat engine into rounds: open round 1 when a fight starts, resolve each round in a fixed order inside one transaction (players, pets, enemies, effects, cooldowns, adds, deaths, retarget, victory or defeat, next round), drive it from one guarded scheduled tick per fight, and retire the per-second loop.

Purpose: RND-01 (10-second rounds driven by the tick), RND-02 (auto-attack when no choice), RND-03 (deterministic order; effects, DoT/HoT, stun and cooldowns in rounds), RND-04 (rows written at start and on resolution, removed at the end). The early-resolution input path (use_ability, submit_combat_action, flee) is 46.1-06; enemy wind-ups, enemy cooldowns, pets and adds in rounds are 46.1-07; big-moment wiring and dead-code removal are 46.1-08.

Output: the round orchestrator and tick reducer in reducers/combat.ts, a shared test fixture, the lifecycle integration test and the rounds-not-wall-clock invariant test.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
Primary noun: the combat round (`combat_round.roundNumber`) is the unit of time inside a fight.
Decision: PROMOTE. While a fight is active, rounds are primary: ability cooldowns (`ability_cooldown.roundsRemaining`), effects and DoT/HoT (`roundsRemaining`), enemy stun, enemy ability cooldowns (`combat_enemy_cooldown.readyAtRound`), enemy wind-ups (`combat_enemy_cast.landsAtRound`) and add arrivals (`combat_pending_add.arriveAtRound`) are decided by round counts only. Wall-clock values are kept only as client estimates (`startedAtMicros`/`durationMicros`, `endsAtMicros`, `readyAtMicros`, `arriveAtMicros`, `timerExpiresAtMicros` for the countdown) and for out-of-combat cooldowns.
Where it binds: 46.1-04 stores rounds; this plan makes resolveRound the only clock in a fight and adds the companion invariant test (cooldowns, effects, DoT/HoT, stun); 46.1-07 extends the same test file to enemy cooldowns, wind-ups, pets and adds and adds a static guard over the round functions.
Companion invariant test (accepted): `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat_round_clock.invariant.test.ts`: for every in-combat timing, the behavior is driven by round counts and never by a wall-clock comparison while a fight is active (same scripted fight under two clocks gives identical round-domain state).
</assumption_delta_decision>
