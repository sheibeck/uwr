<objective>
Move world generation, last in the locked order, onto the executor: every trigger starts generation in its own transaction, failures go to ERROR instead of the old client-driven PENDING retry, explore is the player's retry (including the first region), and the prepare reducer and its client watch are deleted.

Purpose: PIPE-01 for world gen, PIPE-04 ("world gen never auto-retries without player action") and PIPE-05 (lock released with an in-voice message). Implements CONTEXT "prepare_world_gen_llm ... deleted as each domain moves", the research-driven decision "World-gen failure goes to ERROR, and exploring retries ... the first region gets a small retry path from the player's own ERROR starter state, for example by extending the explore intent. Update the pinned retryWorldGen tests deliberately", and RESEARCH section 4.5.
Output: `startWorldGeneration`, `failWorldGen`, three trigger call sites, the explore first-region path, prepare deletion, client cleanup, allowlist to zero for index.ts, publish, bindings and build.
</objective>
