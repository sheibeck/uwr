**Shared mechanisms introduced (so later work reuses them):**
- `reportRejections(runner, feed)` in `src/ledger/actionRunner.ts` and `usePartyActions(game, consoleApi)` in `src/social/usePartyActions.ts`. Every party control and Nearby report a rejected call the same way: one `SEND_ERROR_TEXT` local system line in the feed. `NoticeLine` has an opt-in `sendErrors` prop that shows that line as the rejection; the mobile Party sheet (`SocialScreen.vue`) passes it.
- `keepFocus({ source, area, capture, restore })` and `focusLost()` in `src/ledger/keepFocus.ts`. This is the one focus-after-removal rule. Before a re-render it notes where focus sits in an area. After the re-render, if that control went away, focus moves to the next equivalent control or the area's `tabindex="-1"` heading. It is used by InviteCard, OutgoingInvites, PartyBlock, VitalsRail, VitalsStrip and NearbyList. `PlayerMenu`'s `fallbackFocus` now runs after the DOM settles and only while focus is still lost, so a host's `keepFocus` rule wins.
