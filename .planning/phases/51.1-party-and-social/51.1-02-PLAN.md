## Decisions
- **Owner, after research (51.1-CONTEXT):** only online members with Travel with leader on, standing at the leader's place, travel with the leader and count in the stamina check; offline members are also skipped when a fight starts (`getGroupOrSoloParticipants`). Server, `travelChecks.ts` and `follow.ts` agree through the one predicate `comesAlongWithLeader` (Plan 01) and a parity test (Plan 07).
- **"Online" on the server** means `character.online === true` (Plan 01's stored flag). A row without the field reads offline.
- **Planner, scope of the fight rule:** only `getGroupOrSoloParticipants` changes (the owner named it). The pet heal loops over group members in `reducers/combat.ts` and `getGroupParticipants` / `partyMembersInLocation` in `helpers/character.ts` are not fight pulls and stay unchanged.
- **Planner, refusal copy:** `{name} is offline.` (the UI-SPEC gating reason). The intent whisper path uses `fail(ctx, character, message)`; the reducer path keeps its existing kind `'whisper'` like its other refusals.
- **Planner, the existing stamina refusal** `${traveler.name} does not have enough stamina to travel` stays byte-identical (the client's warning copy matches it).
