# Phase 51.1 Plan 12: Nearby players and their menu Summary

Nearby lists only online players, marks your party members, and gives each player row Whisper, Examine and the role-aware menu (right-click too); NPC Talk to moves to the plain chat circle so no two actions share an icon.

## What was built

- `nearby.ts`: `nearbyRows` skips every player whose `online !== true`; new optional `partyIds?: ReadonlySet<bigint>` gives a listed party member the hint `In your party`, everyone else `Lv {n}`. Every other kind and the sort order are unchanged. The header comment records CONTEXT Area 2.
- `NearbyList.vue`: passes `partyIds` (from `game.groupMembers` character ids) to `nearbyRows`; the player cluster is exactly `Whisper {name}` (`PhChatCircleDots`), `Examine {name}`, then `<PlayerMenu :target-id side="left" :size :fallback-focus>` (its opener is labelled `Actions for {name}`). The Invite button, its `invite(row)` function and the `PhUserPlus` import are gone. `@contextmenu` on the `li` calls `preventDefault()` and the row's `PlayerMenu.open('first')` for player rows on desktop only (mobile leaves the event alone). The NPC Talk button uses `PhChatCircle`. The Nearby `h6` has `tabindex="-1"` and a ref that serves as the menu's focus fallback.
- Tests: `nearby.test.ts` (online filter, party hint, order kept), `NearbyList.test.ts` (cluster order, icons, menu open, right-click open and defaultPrevented with first item focused, NPC right-click untouched, mobile left alone with the 44px opener, offline player absent, party hint, markup as text, focus fallback to the heading, source pins), `ContextContent.test.ts` (fixtures online, cluster rewritten, offline loop skips the opener), `AppFrame.populated.test.ts` (Marisol online with race/class, Map sheet case now opens `Actions for Marisol` and chooses `Invite to party`, you lead the party in that case).
