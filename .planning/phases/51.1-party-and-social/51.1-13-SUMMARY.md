# Phase 51.1 Plan 13: Vitals rail party area out of combat Summary

The desktop vitals rail now shows, out of combat, who is online, who travels with the leader and who is short of stamina, every pet under its owner, a menu on every member and on yourself, the gated Invite, Loot: personal, Invited · waiting and the incoming invite card, plus your Travel with leader switch under your own block.

## What was built

- `src/social/MemberCard.vue` (new, 252 lines): props `{ member: PartyMemberView; state: FollowState | null; variant?: 'rail' | 'sheet'; fallbackFocus?: () => HTMLElement | null }`. Root `div.member-card` (surface, `--radius-md`) is `[div.content][PlayerMenu.member-menu]`. Content: padding 8, column, gap 4; the name row is `StatusDot`, `CharacterName.member-name` (12/500), the crown, an `.sr-only.status-word` (`online` / `offline`), the class (`flex: 1 1 0`, ellipsis; it gives way first), then `span.right` (`FollowIcon` 12, `Lv {n} · {s} st` with the 51 low mark moved unchanged from PartyBlock) and `.sr-only.stamina-sr`; the 4px health bar and 3px resource bar. The ⋯ is the last child with `margin: 4px 4px 0 0`. Right-click on the card calls the menu's `open('first')` and prevents the browser menu (rail always; the sheet variant only when `frame.isDesktop`). Offline: `.muted.offline` (name neutral-500, bars 45%). Unknown (`known === false`): `.muted.unknown`, `Member`, no dot, status word, follow icon, stamina or ⋯. An open menu draws the inset 1px neutral-600 on the card.
- `src/rails/PartyBlock.vue`: injects `SOCIAL_KEY`; the `h6` has `tabindex="-1"` and a ref. Out of the party fight it renders, in order: header with Invite (enabled solo or for the leader with room; otherwise `aria-disabled`, `title` and an `.sr-only` twin `Only the leader can invite.` / `Your party is full.`, and the click does nothing), the follow summary (`PhFootprints` 12 accent-300, Label 12 neutral-400), the stamina warning (`PhWarningCircle` 12 and text in con-red), `div.cards` of `div.member-entry` (MemberCard + PetRow with the owner name), `Not in a party.`, `Loot: personal` (title and `.sr-only` `Each fighter rolls their own loot.`), `OutgoingInvites variant=rail` and `InviteCard variant=rail` (both focus the heading through their events). The Phase 48 combat branch is unchanged.
- `src/frame/VitalsRail.vue`: `div.self-block` (relative, column, gap 16, `--radius-md`; `::before` at `inset: -8px` with the radius and `pointer-events: none`) wraps identity, bars, XP and chips, then the self `PlayerMenu.self-menu` (absolute top 0 right 0) in a party, then your `PetRow` (owner null, solo too). The identity row gets `.reserve` (padding-right 32) and a contextmenu handler that opens the self menu. `<TravelSwitch variant="rail" />` sits between the block and the `.hr`. The self menu's focus fallback is the party block's `h6`.
