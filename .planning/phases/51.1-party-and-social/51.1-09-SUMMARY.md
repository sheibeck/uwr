## What was built
- `StatusDot.vue`: 8 x 8 aria-hidden circle; online `--color-con-light-green` filled, offline hollow with inset 1px `--color-neutral-600`, busy `--color-con-yellow` (provisional, not fed in 51.1).
- `CharacterName.vue`: name as a text node in an ellipsizing inline-flex span, `title` = full name, optional ` (you)` in neutral-500, trailing default slot (empty in 51.1; 52.2 puts the guild tag there).
- `FollowIcon.vue`: PhFlagBanner (accent), PhFootprints (accent-300 or neutral-500), PhPersonSimple (neutral-500) at 12px, title and `.sr-only` twin from `FOLLOW_TEXT`; `decorative` gives aria-hidden and no twin.
- `PetRow.vue`: a `div` (no button, link or tabindex) with the elbow `::before` at `left: -12px; top: -4px`, paw, name (title, ellipsis), `Lv {n}`, `{hp}/{max}`, a 4px progressbar labelled `{pet} health {hp} of {max}` (min-width 24), the hourglass and `{m:ss}` in the stamina colour only with an expiry, and `.sr-only` `Your pet` / `{owner}'s pet` and `fades in about {n} minutes`.
- `PetTag.vue`: `span.tag.tag-neutral`, paw, `{pet} {pct}%` (name ellipsizes, `max-width: 50%`), `{m:ss}` only with an expiry, and the `.sr-only` sentence `Your pet {pet}, health {pct} percent[, fades in about {n} minutes]`.
- `TravelSwitch.vue`: a whole-row `button role="switch"`; visible only for a non-leader member, out of combat, once your member row and the leader's character row are known; `aria-checked` from your member row's `followLeader` (no optimistic flip); `aria-describedby` on the sub-line (`On · you move when {leader} travels` / `Off · you stay put when {leader} travels`); `aria-disabled` while offline or pending; calls `setTravelWithLeader(!on)` from `createPartyActions`; 32 x 16 track with a 12px knob on a 120ms `left` transition, none under `prefers-reduced-motion`; `sheet` variant has min-height 44 and padding `8px 16px`; Nocturne focus ring kept.
- `socialGuards.test.ts`: walks every non-test `.ts` and `.vue` under `src/social` (12 files now): no v-html, no `<svg`, no literal colours (hex, rgb, hsl, oklch), no replaceAll / `.at(` / Object.hasOwn, no banned word, sizes 10/12/14/20, weights 400/500, `appendLocal(` only on lines naming `SEND_ERROR_TEXT`, every `Ph*` name in a file exists in `@phosphor-icons/vue`; plus an existence check for the 18 icons the phase names (PhHourglassMedium among them).
- `effectChipsGuards.test.ts`: FILES gained `src/rails/PartyBlock.vue`, `src/frame/VitalsRail.vue`, `src/frame/VitalsStrip.vue`; all three already passed the checks, so no unrelated code was touched.

## Deviations from Plan
None of the rule 1-4 kind. Small choices inside the plan: the pet timer needs both seconds and an expiry (so a pet without one never shows a clock); `CharacterName` has no `aria-label` (its text already is the name; a label on a generic span is not valid ARIA); `PetRow`/`PetTag` hold their visible numbers aria-hidden where the plan said the timer only, so screen readers get one sentence.
