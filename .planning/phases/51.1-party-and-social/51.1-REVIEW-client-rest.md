## Summary

I reviewed the client changes outside `src/social` and `src/module_bindings`:
- the vitals rail self block (the self target in combat, the self `⋯`, your pet row, the XP line) and the party block (rail, combat and sheet recipes);
- the mobile strip self row, row 3 and the party grid;
- the mobile Party sheet (`SocialScreen.vue`) and the `NoticeLine` `kinds` prop;
- the Nearby player cluster and its menu, and online-only Nearby;
- `travelChecks` with the online follow rule, the Map players count, and the `online` field passed through `useExits` and `useDestination`;
- the social hub wiring in `useSession` and `App.vue`.

I traced calls into `src/social` (`PlayerMenu`, `ActionMenu`, `InviteCard`, `OutgoingInvites`, `TravelSwitch`, `PetRow`, `CharacterName`, `partyActions`), `src/ledger/actionRunner.ts`, `src/console/feedStore.ts`, `src/combat/useCombatController.ts`, `src/net/bindTable.ts`, and the server's `groups.ts` (`leave_group`, accept, reject, the invite cap).

**What checks out:**
- **Combat gate.** Every surface reads `game.combat.active`, never `game.inCombat`.
- **XP line.** It is hidden in combat in both the rail and the strip, and it returns when the fight ends.
- **Ally target.** The self target and the member cards share `controller.allyTargetId`. A click is client state only. The controller resets a target who left the party (`allyResetNeeded`).
- **No nested buttons.** The `⋯` and pet rows are siblings of the target buttons. Every element inside a target button is a span, and `EffectChips` uses `inline`. A DOM test asserts `button button` is absent in both the rail and the strip.
- **Online rule.** `travelChecks` calls the shared `comesAlongWithLeader` with `online === true`. Both mappers now pass `online` through. The Map players count and Nearby keep only rows whose `online` is exactly true. The client invite cap matches the server's (members plus live invites).
- **useSession.** The social hub's lifecycle mirrors the map hub: it is built after the map hub, reset on logout and disposed on dispose. Its keys are id-list strings, so a recomputed array does not resubscribe.
- **Stacking.** On desktop, the self block's tints sit on `::before` with no z-index, so no stacking context traps the `position: fixed` menu panel. The only `isolation: isolate` is on the mobile combat self row, which has no menu.
- **Design guard.** Sizes are 10/12/14/20 and weights 400/500. There are no negative margins (the outsets use `inset` on pseudo-elements), and only tokens are used.
- **Tests.** The Phase 48 rewrites replace each removed case: the `You` card, the `You` chip, the chip row and `selfCardView` (deleted with the function, as the UI-SPEC Supersedes table allows). The one exception is in WR-04.
- **Suites.** The in-scope suites pass: 48 files, 1082 tests.

No blockers. The defects are:
- client rejections are invisible on the Party sheet, and partly invisible everywhere;
- focus is lost after Leave party or after answering an invite in a fight;
- focus is lost when a fight starts or ends, or when a row disappears;
- the player menu is live while disconnected, and an offline assertion was narrowed with no replacement;
- focus after Remove from party probably lands on the heading instead of the next card's `⋯`.

## Warnings

### WR-01: Client rejections never reach the Party sheet's NoticeLine, and three party controls never report them anywhere

**File:** `src/screens/SocialScreen.vue:23`, `src/ledger/NoticeLine.vue:50`, `src/rails/PartyBlock.vue:159,187,251,266`
**Issue:** The UI-SPEC error row (Q2, Q5, Q6, Q7, Q11, Q12, Q13) requires the client rejection line `Couldn't send that. Try again.`, shown on mobile in the sheet's `NoticeLine`. Two things break this:
- **No path to the sheet.**
  - `SocialScreen` mounts `<NoticeLine :kinds="SHEET_KINDS" />` with no `rejection` counter.
  - `PlayerMenu` reports a rejection with `game.feed.appendLocal('system', SEND_ERROR_TEXT)` (`src/social/PlayerMenu.vue:51-56`). `appendLocal` writes `source: 'local'` (`src/console/feedStore.ts:336`), and `NoticeLine` skips every entry whose source is not `'private'`. So the menu's rejection line never shows in the sheet.
  - The feed itself is hidden while a sheet is open (`AppFrame.vue:208`, `v-show="!sheetOpen"`).
- **No report at all.** `InviteCard`, `OutgoingInvites` and `TravelSwitch`, which `PartyBlock` hosts in both the rail and the sheet, count rejections in their own `createActionRunner` and never read them:
  - `InviteCard.vue:95-96` only emits on success;
  - `partyActions.ts:9` says no feed line is written there.

  A rejected Accept, Decline, Cancel invite or Travel with leader is therefore silent on desktop too. The 51.1-16 summary says "menu and card rejections print to the feed themselves", but that is true only for the menu.

**Failure scenario:** On a phone with the Party sheet open, the transport drops mid-call or `requireCharacterOwnedBy` throws on `accept_group_invite`. The button becomes enabled again, and the player sees and hears nothing.
**Fix:** Give the three components the same feed line as `PlayerMenu`, and let the sheet mirror it. For example:
```ts
// InviteCard / OutgoingInvites / TravelSwitch
watch(() => runner.rejection.value, (next, prev) => {
  if (next > prev) game.feed.appendLocal('system', SEND_ERROR_TEXT);
});
```
Then either:
- teach `NoticeLine` to accept a local `system` line equal to `SEND_ERROR_TEXT` when the host opts in (for example a `mirrorLocalErrors` prop set by `SocialScreen`); or
- lift a shared rejection counter through `PartyBlock` (an emitted `rejected` event) into `<NoticeLine :rejection>`.

Add a sheet test that rejects `acceptGroupInvite`, then rejects a menu action, and expects the line both times.

### WR-02: In a fight, Leave party and answering an invite drop focus to `body`, because solo combat has no Party heading

**File:** `src/rails/PartyBlock.vue:176`, `src/rails/PartyBlock.vue:131-133`, `src/rails/PartyBlock.vue:266`, `src/frame/VitalsRail.vue:107-109`
**Issue:**
- **No heading when solo.** In combat the heading is `<h6 v-else-if="inParty" class="sr-only">`, so a solo character in a fight has no `h6` at all.
- **Two fallbacks point at it.** The self menu's `fallbackFocus` is `partyHeading()`, which queries `.party h6`. The invite card's `@answered` calls `focusHeading()`, which reads `headingEl`.
- **Both cases happen in play.**
  - `leave_group` has no combat check (`spacetimedb/src/reducers/groups.ts:116`), so Leave party works in a fight. The group goes to null, the self `⋯` unmounts with focus, and `partyHeading()` returns null.
  - An incoming invite only reaches a solo character, and the card stays in combat. Decline leaves you solo in a fight, and `headingEl` is null.

  Both drop focus to `body`, against the Accessibility table ("Leave party … → the Party heading", "Invite answered → the Party heading").
- **Accept may fail too.** After Accept, `group` and `group_member` arrive by a re-keyed subscription after the reducer promise. The sr-only heading may not exist yet when `answered` fires.

**Fix:** Always render the screen-reader heading in combat. It is invisible, so it does not conflict with "shows nothing but the incoming invite card":
```vue
<h6 v-else ref="headingEl" class="sr-only" tabindex="-1">{{ heading }}</h6>
```
For Accept, defer the focus until the heading exists (`await nextTick()`, or focus in a watcher on `inParty`). Add tests for: Leave from the self menu in a fight (in a party), then focus is on `.party h6`; and Decline in a fight while solo, then focus is on `.party h6`.

### WR-03: Starting or ending a fight, or a row disappearing, drops focus to `body`; only the `⋯` opener has a fallback

**File:** `src/frame/VitalsRail.vue:80-81`, `src/frame/VitalsStrip.vue:165-193`, `src/frame/VitalsStrip.vue:260-300`, `src/rails/PartyBlock.vue:138-145`, `src/rails/PartyBlock.vue:254-264`, `src/rails/NearbyList.vue:168-186`
**Issue:** The focus area asks that focus moves when rows disappear. Only `PlayerMenu` handles its own opener going away. Every other focusable control that 51.1 makes conditional is removed with focus inside it:
- **Self target (certain).**
  - Desktop: when `combat.active` turns false, `targetWrapper` swaps `button.self-target` for the `Unwrapped` fragment.
  - Mobile: the self row swaps the button for divs.

  A keyboard user who targeted himself and kept focus there loses it at the end of every fight.
- **Member targets and grid cards (certain).** A member leaving in a fight (allowed, see WR-02) removes his `button.member-target` or `button.ally-card`. `menuFallback` only runs for the `⋯`.
- **Member `⋯` at the fight boundary (likely).** The combat and out-of-combat card lists are separate `v-if` branches. When the old branch unmounts, `menuFallback` searches `cardsEl`, which still holds the old openers. With two or more members it returns `rest[index]`, an opener that is about to be removed.
- **Nearby (certain).** Whisper and Examine on a player row go away when the player logs out (new in 51.1: offline players drop out) or walks away. `headingFocus` is wired only into `PlayerMenu`.

**Fix:**
- **Rail and strip.** Remember whether the target held focus before the swap (`watch(selfTarget, …, { flush: 'pre' })`). After the swap (`flush: 'post'`), focus the self `⋯` if it is shown, else the Party heading.
- **`menuFallback`.** Resolve the next opener after the DOM update (`nextTick`) rather than from the leaving list.
- **NearbyList.** Before the update, record the key of the row that holds focus. After the update, if `document.activeElement === document.body`, focus the next row's first button, else the Nearby heading.

Add tests for the fight-end case and for a player row removed while its Whisper button has focus.

### WR-04: While disconnected, the Nearby `⋯` stays live and its menu entries look enabled but send nothing, and the offline test was narrowed with no replacement

**File:** `src/rails/NearbyList.vue:383-390`, `src/rails/ContextContent.test.ts:571`
**Issue:**
- **What the UI-SPEC asks.**
  - The Interaction States table gives the `⋯` icon button a disabled state (45%, `aria-disabled`).
  - The error row says "offline disables actions while reading stays".
- **What the code does.**
  - The opener has no `aria-disabled`.
  - `ActionMenu` disables an entry only on `entry.disabled || isPending` (`src/social/ActionMenu.vue:79-81`). Nothing in `playerMenuEntries` reads `game.connected`.
  - `createActionRunner.run` returns `false` offline without counting a rejection (`src/ledger/actionRunner.ts:36`).

  So a disconnected player can open the menu, choose `Invite to party`, and get no effect and no message.
- **The test change.** `ContextContent.test.ts` "offline" previously required every button to be `aria-disabled`. It now filters out `.menu-opener`, and no test asserts that the menu's entries are inert while disconnected. The `PlayerMenu` offline tests cover only an offline target.

**Fix:** Treat a disconnected client like an offline target: pass `connected` into the entry builder and disable every action entry with a hint such as `Not connected`. Alternatively, `aria-disable` the opener with that reason, as the spec table says. Restore the assertion: in the offline case, open the menu and expect every `menuitem` to be `aria-disabled="true"`.

### WR-05: Focus after Remove from party probably lands on the heading, not on the next card's `⋯`

**File:** `src/rails/PartyBlock.vue:138-145`
**Issue:** `menuFallback` finds the leaving card by comparing `document.activeElement` with each `.menu-opener`.
- **The usual Remove flow.** Focus is on the `Remove` confirm button inside the leaving card's open menu panel, not on its opener.
- **Likely order of events.**
  1. `bindTable` refreshes rows synchronously in the SDK's row callbacks (`src/net/bindTable.ts:101-103`).
  2. That queues Vue's flush before the reducer promise's `await` chain in `PlayerMenu.select` resumes.
  3. The card therefore unmounts while the menu is still open.
  4. `index` is `-1`, so the function returns the heading.
- **Result.** The Accessibility table asks for "the next card's `⋯`, else the Party heading". The existing test only covers focus that sits on the opener itself.

**Fix:** Identify the leaving card from wherever focus is inside it:
```ts
const leaving = (document.activeElement as Element | null)?.closest('.member-entry');
const entries = Array.from(cardsEl.value?.querySelectorAll<HTMLElement>('.member-entry') ?? []);
const index = leaving ? entries.indexOf(leaving as HTMLElement) : -1;
const next = entries.slice(index + 1).find((e) => e.querySelector('.menu-opener'));
return next?.querySelector<HTMLElement>('.menu-opener') ?? headingEl.value;
```
Add a test that removes the member while focus is on the menu's `Remove` button.

### IN-02: The self pet row's elbow does not reach the self block

**File:** `src/frame/VitalsRail.vue:225`, `src/frame/VitalsRail.vue:256`
**Issue:**
- **The gap.** `PetRow` is a direct child of `.self-block`, which has `gap: 16px`. With the row's own `margin-top: 4px`, it starts 20px below your chips, but the elbow (`top: -4px`, 16px tall) only reaches 4px up. The connector floats with a 16px gap above it.
- **The contrast.** `PartyBlock` avoids this by putting the card and its pet row in a `gap: 0` `.member-entry`.

**Fix:** Keep the 16px gap only between identity, bars and chips:
- give the self block `gap: 0`;
- render the content column as a gap-16 element in both modes (a `div.self-content` out of combat, the button in combat);
- let the pet row's 4px margin and elbow meet it.

### IN-03: Offline players still feed the console keyword vocabulary and the menu's target lookup

**File:** `src/console/FeedView.vue:38` (out of the diff; the consumer of the changed rule)
**Issue:** 51.1 drops offline characters from Nearby, the Map count, `look` and `who`, but `game.playersHere` is still unfiltered. An offline player's name stays a clickable keyword in feed lines (`buildVocabulary({ players: game.playersHere.value })`), so clicking it leads to a server refusal. `PlayerMenu` also resolves targets through `playersHere`.
**Fix:** Filter `online === true` once, in `gameData.playersHere` or in a derived `onlinePlayersHere`. Then FeedView, Nearby and the menu agree.

### IN-04: The grid card's paw `title` is set on an SVG element and shows no tooltip

**File:** `src/frame/VitalsStrip.vue:274-280`
**Issue:** `PhPawPrint` renders an `<svg>`, and browsers do not show a tooltip for a `title` attribute on SVG elements (only for a `<title>` child). On a touch screen there is no hover anyway. The pet name and HP are in the card's `aria-label`, so nothing is lost for assistive technology, but the UI-SPEC's `title="{pet} {hp}/{max}"` has no effect.
**Fix:** Either wrap the paw in a `span` that carries the `title`, or drop the attribute and note that the accessible name carries the pet.
