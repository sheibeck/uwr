# Phase 51.1 Plan 07: Follow Rule on the Client Summary

The client now mirrors the server's follow rule exactly. A pure `follow.ts` gives each party member's follow state, the "{n} of {m} travel with you" summary and the stamina warning. The Map's `travelChecks` counts only followers who are online, following and at your place. The Map's Players count counts only online characters. One parity test runs the same eight cases through the real server `move_character`, `travelChecks` and `followState`.
