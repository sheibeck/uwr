## Decisions
- **UI-SPEC "Incoming Invite Card" and "Outgoing Invites"** are the source of every value and string; "Mock override 27": the card at the bottom of the rail's party block, and on mobile above the composer and at the top of the Party sheet.
- **Planner, the live region** stays mounted (the component root is always rendered; only the card body depends on the invite) so the announce is heard; its text changes only when a new invite id arrives.
- **Planner, the inviter's name gate:** the card body renders only once the inviter's name resolves (the game hub subscribes inviter characters); Accept needs the name for `fromName`.
- **Planner, unresolved outgoing names:** the row shows 'Player' and its Cancel invite is aria-disabled until the name resolves (the reducer needs `targetName`).
- **Planner, mobile focus after answering:** focus moves to the composer's input (there is no Party heading on the main mobile screen).
- **Planner, the leader name** in the follow line: the inviting group's leader, else the inviter.
