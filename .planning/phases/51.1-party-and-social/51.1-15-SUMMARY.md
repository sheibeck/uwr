# Phase 51.1 Plan 15: Mobile combat header (self target, pet tag, party grid) Summary

On a phone in a fight, your own row is the self target, your effect chips and your pet tag share row 3, and the party is a 3-column grid of 44px target cards with a paw for members who have a pet. The Phase 48 `Party n` / `You` / `Name %` chip row is gone in combat.

## What was built

All in `src/frame/VitalsStrip.vue` (the non-compact branch; the compact keyboard-open branch is unchanged).

- **Self row** (`combatActive` and the character row exists): `button.self-target` (`type="button"`) holds the avatar, `CharacterName` (class `self-name`, 14/500), the crown (aria-hidden) and the class line, built from spans. When selected (`allyTargetId === character.id`, true by default) it gets the `selected` class and `PhCrosshairSimple.self-marker` (fill 16, accent) at its right end. `aria-pressed`, `aria-label` `Target yourself with your next ability. Health {hp} of {max}, mana {m} of {max}, stamina {s} of {max}.`, click `controller.selectAlly(character.id)`. The tags (`InCombatTag`, `Level up`, `New skill`) are the button's siblings in `.identity-row`.
  - CSS: `min-height: 44px`, `padding: 4px 0` (the 36px avatar plus 4 and 4), `--radius-md`, transparent; the ring (`inset 0 0 0 1px var(--color-accent)` plus the 12px glow) and the 7% / 14% tints sit on `::before` at `inset: 0 -4px`, `z-index: -1`, under `isolation: isolate`. No negative margin.
- **XP line**: `v-if="!combatActive"`.
- **Row 3** (`.row3`, `min-height: 28px`, gap 8; in combat when you have effects or a pet): `.row3-chips` (sideways-scrolling, scrollbar hidden) holds `EffectChips nowrap compact`; `PetTag` (a span, `margin-left: auto`, `max-width: 50%`) is the last child, fed `social.petOf(characterId)` and `social.petSecondsLeft`. Not a button, not a target.
- **Party grid** (`ul.party-grid`, `aria-label="Party"`, `grid-template-columns: repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr))`, gap 8; in combat when there are other members): one `li` per member in `partyMembers` order.
  - Known member: `button.ally-card` (`min-height: 44px`, `padding: 4px 8px`, `--radius-md`, `var(--color-surface)`, column, gap 4). Top row: `CharacterName` (12/500) then `PhPawPrint` 10 `accent-300` (`title="{pet} {hp}/{max}"`, aria-hidden) when `social.petOf(member.id)` exists, and `{pct}%` (Micro 10, neutral-400, tabular). Below: a 3px HP track (aria-hidden) and `EffectChips compact nowrap inline :limit="STRIP_EFFECT_LIMIT"` (one chip plus `+N`). `aria-pressed`, the ring when selected (no crosshair), click `controller.selectAlly(member.id)`; it never opens the Social sheet.
  - `aria-label`: `Target {name} with your next ability. Health {pct} percent{, pet {pet} health {hp} of {max}}{, offline}{. Effects: {texts}}.`
  - Offline: `.muted` (name neutral-500, bar at 45%), still a button. Unknown member (no character row): `div.ally-card.unknown.muted` reading `Member`.
  - No `⋯`, no menu, no `You` chip, no `Party {n}` text in combat.
- Removed from the file: `allyMode`, `allyLabel`, `isSelected`, the combat branch of the chip row, `.ally-row` (the padding plus negative-margin hit-slop), `.ally-chip` and its `::after` slop, `.party-count`.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Test file type error left by the Task 1 GREEN commit**
- **Found during:** Task 2 `vue-tsc -b`
- **Issue:** `row.get('.pet-tag').exists()` (a `get` result has no `exists`).
- **Fix:** `row.find(...)`. Landed in the Task 2 GREEN commit `09dc63cc`.

### Interpretation notes (not deviations)

- **Ring layering without a negative margin:** the UI-SPEC's `padding: 4px; margin: -4px` became `padding: 4px 0` on the button with the ring on `::before` at `inset: 0 -4px` (the plan's own instruction). The `::before` sits at `z-index: -1` inside `isolation: isolate` so the tint paints under the text. This differs from the desktop rail (Plan 14 avoided any stacking context because of the fixed menu panel); the strip has no menu on mobile in combat, so nothing is trapped.
- **Task 1 / Task 2 split:** the old combat chip tests were pinned to the old chip row, so Task 1 rewrote all of them (self-row halves) and Task 2 added the grid halves. The AppFrame.combat mobile strip case moved to the self row in Task 1 and gained the grid assertions in Task 2.
- **No grid when alone in the group:** `partyMembers` returns no other rows, so no empty `ul` is rendered.
- **Out of combat the pet tag does not show** (the plan scopes it to combat); the Phase 47 chip row is untouched.
