# Phase 51.1 Plan 02: Offline members left behind, online gates Summary

Offline party members no longer travel with the leader, count in the all-or-nothing stamina or region-timer checks, or get pulled into a fight, and offline characters drop out of who, look and look at while whispers to them are refused by name.

## What changed

- `helpers/travel.ts`: the leader's follower loop now looks up each group member and calls `comesAlongWithLeader({ followLeader, online, atLeaderPlace })` (shorthand `online` local, as the Plan 01 single-writer guard requires) and keeps the "not already at the destination" test. The stamina and cross-region timer loops iterate the same list, so they needed no change. `travelStaminaCost`, the solo path and the stamina refusal text are untouched.
- `helpers/group.ts` `getGroupOrSoloParticipants`: after the same-place test, skips a member whose `online !== true`. The initiator is always first and always in.
- `helpers/look.ts`, `helpers/examine.ts`: other players are kept only when `online === true`.
- `reducers/intent.ts`: `who` / `players` filters to online; typed whisper refuses with `fail(ctx, character, "{name} is offline.")`.
- `reducers/commands.ts` `whisper`: refuses with `fail(..., "{name} is offline.", 'whisper')` after the target is found.

## Tests

- `travel_followers.integration.test.ts` (11, real `move_character`, strict mock): online follower moves and pays; offline stays with stamina kept; missing `online` field reads offline; follow off stays; neighbouring-place member stays [E6]; stamina equal to cost travels and ends at 0, one below blocks the whole trip with the exact text [E5]; offline follower at stamina 0 neither moves nor blocks [E5]; all others offline, leader alone pays only its own cost [E7]; mixed online/offline; a non-leader member moves only itself.
- `group_participants.test.ts` (7): solo, online included, offline excluded, missing flag excluded, other place excluded, offline initiator still included, order.
- `online_gates.integration.test.ts` (12, real `submit_intent` and `whisper`): who, look, look at, reducer whisper, typed whisper / w / tell.
- RED runs: travel_followers 5 of 11 failed and group_participants 2 of 7 failed before the implementation (the offline-member cases); online_gates 9 of 12 failed before the implementation (the online-path cases already passed).
- Seeds changed to say online: `travel_visited.integration.test.ts` (Follower, Stayer), `travel_passage.integration.test.ts` (Follower, Stayer), `examine.test.ts` (Mira, Woodrow, plus a new offline case), `chat_text.test.ts` (Tamsin, the whisper target; not in the plan's file list, found by the server suite).
