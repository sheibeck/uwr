## Decisions
- **CONTEXT Area 4** entries by role; **owner (51.1-CONTEXT)** icons: Whisper `PhChatCircleDots`, Add friend `PhHeart`, Invite to party `PhUserPlus`, no icon shared; **UI-SPEC** "Entries by target and role" and "Entry rules" are the source for every label, hint and confirmation.
- **Coordinator default:** the "Answering another invite" hint is dropped (the server refusal covers it).
- **Planner, icons as ids:** the pure module returns icon ids (`MenuIcon`), and the renderer (Plan 10) maps them to Phosphor components, so this module imports no Vue.
- **Planner, Invite hint precedence** (one hint): 'In another party', 'Leader invites', 'Party full', 'Offline'. Party full counts members plus live invites, the server's cap (Plan 03).
- **Planner, Cancel invite** appears for a target with a live invite from your group when you lead it or sent it (Plan 03's cancel rule), with the hint 'Pending'.
- **Planner, actions use the reducers, not the console's invite**: menu actions call `game.reducers` through a runner (no echo line, the open sheet stays open so its NoticeLine shows the result); Whisper and the rail Invite use the console pre-fill as the UI-SPEC says; Examine uses `consoleApi.examine`.
- **Planner, next leader** for the Leave prompt: the remaining member with the earliest joinedAt, then the lowest member row id (Plan 03's `nextLeaderAfter`).
