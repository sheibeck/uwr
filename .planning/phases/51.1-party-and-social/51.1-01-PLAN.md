## Decisions (planner, Claude's discretion: "storage shapes for online status")
- **Column shape.** `online: t.bool().default(false)` then `lastOnlineAtMicros: t.u64().default(0n)` as the last two `character` columns. The UI-SPEC calls the second one `lastOnlineAt`; it is stored as u64 microseconds (0 = never recorded) because a defaulted u64 column has a published precedent here (`startedAtMicros`, Phase 46.1) and a defaulted timestamp column has none. No UI reads it in 51.1 (52.2 does).
- **The rule.** Online means "some player row has this character as activeCharacterId". `syncCharacterOnline` applies it, so the two-session guard is built in.
- **Backfill without a new reducer.** After the publish every row reads offline. `clientConnected` syncs the connecting player's active character (the `--break-clients` publish reconnects every client), and the module-guarded `sweep_inactivity` tick (every 5 minutes) ends with `reconcileOnline`, which repairs any drift. No admin `reconcile_online` reducer is added (research A4 suggested one; fewer client-callable surfaces, as in 51-03).
- **Grace.** The flag does not flip in `clientDisconnected`; it flips in `disconnect_logout` 30 s later, so a page refresh does not flap (research A7).
- **Out of scope.** The online-count view and the singleton counter are Phase 52.2; nothing here counts. The typed `camp` intent only schedules `character_logout` and never clears `player.activeCharacterId` (pre-existing); this plan does not change it, so a typed camp leaves the character online until the inactivity sweep. Record it in the SUMMARY as a follow-up.
- **Group rules home.** `MAX_GROUP_SIZE` moves out of `reducers/groups.ts` into `data/group_config.ts` in Plan 03 (this plan only creates the module; `groups.ts` keeps its local constant until Plan 03 switches the import).
