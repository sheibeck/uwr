## Decisions
- **CONTEXT Area 4:** invites expire after 5 minutes and the inviter can cancel; fix the solo inviter auto-creating a group when the invite fails. **CONTEXT Area 2:** `join_group` must require a pending invite.
- **Coordinator default (51.1-CONTEXT):** a one-member group dissolves when its last pending invite ends (expiry, cancel or decline only; never on leave or kick).
- **Planner, who may cancel:** the invite's group leader or the invite's inviter (UI-SPEC "Invited · waiting": the leader, and the inviter if someone else).
- **Planner, the size cap counts pending invites:** members plus live invites of the group must stay below `MAX_GROUP_SIZE` (UI-SPEC Q6: "one row per pending invite, up to the free party slots"); this also bounds invite spam per group. The refusal keeps the existing text 'Your group is full.'.
- **Planner, the expiry tick is not deleted on early ends:** a tick whose invite is gone is a no-op (research Pattern 2), so cancel, accept and decline do not touch tick rows.
- **Planner, deterministic successor:** `leave_group` uses `nextLeaderAfter` (earliest joinedAt, then lowest member id) so the client's Leave prompt can name the new leader truthfully. `campCharacter` and `delete_character` keep their own successor logic (out of scope).
- **Planner, copy:** new lines avoid "group"/"party" so they read well next to the existing "group" lines: 'You invited {name}.', 'Your invite to {name} expired.', 'The invite from {name} expired.', '{name} cancelled the invite.', 'You cancelled the invite to {name}.', 'No pending invite to {name}.', 'You need an invite to join.', 'That invite has expired.', '{name} is offline.'. Existing lines keep their bytes.
- **Planner, `MAX_GROUP_SIZE`:** `groups.ts` imports it from `../data/group_config` (Plan 01) and drops its local constant.
