# Phase 51.1 Plan 14: Desktop combat rail (self target and COMBAT3 member cards) Summary

In a fight on desktop, you target yourself by clicking your whole self block, and each party member is a COMBAT3 card. A card shows the member's class, follow icon, level, HP, mana (when they have any), stamina and effect chips, with the ⋯ beside it, never inside it. Travel-only items and the XP line hide until the fight ends.

## What was built

- `src/social/CombatMemberCard.vue` (new, 313 lines). Props `{ member, state, fallbackFocus? }`; it injects GAME_KEY and COMBAT_KEY.
  - **Structure.** The root `div.member-card` holds `[button.member-target][PlayerMenu.member-menu]`. The button contains spans only:
    - the header: the crosshair (fill 12, accent, only when selected), `CharacterName`, the crown (aria-hidden), the class (or `Offline`; `flex: 1 1 0`, ellipsis), and the right group (`margin-left: auto; flex: none`) with the decorative `FollowIcon` and `Lv {n}`;
    - the bars: HP 4px (`title="Health {hp}/{max}"`), mana 3px only for mana users (`Mana {m}/{max}`), and stamina 3px always (`Stamina {s}/{max}`);
    - `EffectChips` from `effectViews(game.effects, member.id, true)`, `inline` and `dense`.
  - **Accessible name.** `Target {name} with your next ability. {class}, level {n}, health {hp} of {max}, {follow phrase}{, offline}{. Effects: …}.`
  - **Interaction.** `aria-pressed` follows `controller.allyTargetId`. A click calls `selectAlly(member.id)`. A right-click opens the menu and never changes the target.
  - **Styling.** The 48 ring shows when selected; hovering or pressing the button tints the card 7% / 14%; an open menu adds an inset of 1px neutral-600. An offline card is muted (bars at 45%). An unknown member is a plain `Member` div with no bars, no chips and no ⋯.
- `src/rails/PartyBlock.vue`: the Phase 48 combat branch is replaced.
  - **In combat:** an `.sr-only` `h6` `Party · {n}` (`tabindex="-1"`, in a party only), then `CombatMemberCard` + `PetRow` per member, then the invite card.
  - **Hidden in combat:** the header and Invite, the hint, the summary, the warning, the Loot line and Invited · waiting.
  - **Removed:** the COMBAT_KEY injection, the self-card code and the Phase 48 card CSS.
- `src/rails/party.ts`: the Phase 48 self-card view function is deleted (it had no other consumer).
- `src/frame/VitalsRail.vue`: in a fight, once your row exists, the identity row, the Health/Mana/Stamina bars (the damage flash still runs on Health) and your chips sit inside `button.self-target`.
  - **Button attributes:** `title="Click to target yourself"`, `aria-pressed`, and the label `Target yourself with your next ability. Health … mana … stamina ….`. A click calls `selectAlly(character.id)`.
  - **Selected state:** the crosshair (fill 16) at the end of the identity row, and the ring on `.self-block::before`.
  - **Siblings and menus:** the ⋯ and your pet row are siblings after the button. Right-click on the block opens the self menu.
  - **XP and time limits:** the XP row hides in combat and returns afterwards.
  - **Out of combat:** the markup is Plan 13's.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The AppFrame.combat Mara fixture lacked `race` and `online`**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The in-combat member card now mounts Mara's ⋯ (PlayerMenu reads `race`). Without `online`, she would also read as an offline, muted card.
- **Fix:** The fixture now has `race: 'Elf'` and `online: true` (the same fixture rule as Plan 13).
- **Files modified:** src/frame/AppFrame.combat.test.ts
- **Commit:** ff58b865

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug, layering] The self tints are painted under the content without a stacking context**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** A background on the positioned `::before` paints over in-flow content. The usual fix (`z-index: -1` plus `isolation: isolate`) would trap the self menu's fixed panel under the positioned pet rows later in the rail.
- **Fix:** `.self-target { position: relative }`, which paints it above the earlier pseudo-element in tree order. No z-index is set.
- **Files modified:** src/frame/VitalsRail.vue
- **Commit:** 86dd292e

### Interpretation notes (not deviations)

- **Solo in a fight:** PartyBlock renders no `h6` at all, because the spec says the block "shows nothing but the incoming invite card". In a party the `.sr-only` `Party · {n}` heading exists, and it is also the focus target for the self menu and the invite card.
- **Commit order:** Task 1's GREEN commit (956cf4d3) left AppFrame.combat's old desktop You-card case failing until Task 2. That file belongs to Task 2 in the plan, and both tasks landed back to back.
