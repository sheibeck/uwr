# Phase 51.1 Plan 06: Social Data Layer Summary

A session-owned social hub now holds every row the party surfaces need beyond the game hub: pets of you and your party, the invites your group sent, the invite addressed to you with its group and members, and the character rows behind the names. Invite and pet countdowns run on the server clock. `GameReducers` can send `set_follow_leader` and `cancel_group_invite`. No component renders any of it yet; later plans do.

## What was built
- `src/social/queries.ts`: `SocialQueries`, `socialQueries()`. Every query has a WHERE on an indexed column; id lists must be non-empty (throws). The `user` table is never named.
- `src/social/socialContext.ts`: `SocialData`, `SOCIAL_KEY`, `createInertSocial()`.
- `src/social/socialData.ts`: `SocialConn`, `SocialInput`, `SocialDeps`, `createSocialData()`. Keys: pets = you plus party ids; outgoing = your `character.groupId`; inviting group and members = the incoming invite's `groupId`; extra characters = outgoing targets plus inviting members. The tick (`createSecondsTick`) runs only while an incoming or outgoing invite or a pet with `expiresAtMicros` exists, and samples the clock at once when those rows change.
- `src/game/context.ts`: `setFollowLeader({ characterId, follow })` and `cancelGroupInvite({ characterId, targetName })` on `GameReducers`.
- `src/session/useSession.ts`: `SessionDeps.social`, `Session.social`, `createDefaultSession` factory (`SocialConn` added to the generic list), built after the map hub, `social.reset()` on logout, `social.dispose()` on dispose. `src/App.vue`: `provide(SOCIAL_KEY, session.social ?? createInertSocial())`.
