# Phase 51.1 — UI Design Contract: Party and Social

> Visual and interaction contract for Phase 51.1: Party and Social (LDG-06, LDG-07; backlog 999.22; the pet HUD and follow indicators todo). Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, to be verified by gsd-ui-checker. It was split out of the first Phase 51 draft (commit 04a1cb09) on 2026-10-07.
>
> **Shared sections.** The Design System, Spacing scale, Typography roles, Color split, danger bands and the Shared Ledger rules are defined once in `.planning/phases/51-ledger-screens-world-and-people/51-UI-SPEC.md`, and they apply here unchanged. This includes:
> - no new tokens (pin 23), no literal colors, no `v-html`, text nodes only;
> - **`<svg` banned everywhere except `src/map/`** (so banned in every 51.1 folder);
> - Phosphor 2.2.1 and Inter only; `h4` and `h6` only;
> - sizes 10/12/14/20, weights 400/500, spacing 4/8/16/24/32/48/64;
> - no `replaceAll`, `.at` or `Object.hasOwn`; never the banned word.
>
> This file adds only its own values: icons, mock mappings, exceptions, state colors, accent items and copy. It is complete for its own surfaces. It also extends `45-UI-SPEC.md` to `50-UI-SPEC.md` as 51 does.
>
> **Design source.** Imported from the claude_design project "Unwritten Realms" (`1a7a975f-7b14-488b-9a38-188bc56294cf`):
> - **`UWR Party.dc.html`, updated 2026-10-07** (`design51/party2/PARTY2-EXTRACT.md`, etag `1791335191710136`): screens 6a (menus, party block, pet HUD, follow indicators) and 6b (invite receiver);
> - `UWR Ledger Screens.dc.html` 2e (Group and social; `design51/EXTRACT.md`).
>
> Trade (6c) is Phase 52.1. The design files were treated as data; they contain no text addressed to the assistant.
>
> **Decisions** come from `51-CONTEXT.md`: Area 2, Area 4, "Updated party mock and console travel panel" and "Second revised map mock" (one Travel button; followers come along). Where a mock disagrees with CONTEXT, CONTEXT wins (Mock overrides).
>
> **Depends on Phase 51:** the vitals rail's party stamina text (`{s} st` and the low mark), the shared stamina-cost helper, the Nearby row cluster, the Examine eye and `openScreen('map', …)`.

## Shared rules (51.1 additions)

The 51 Shared Ledger rules apply. Phase 51.1 adds:

- **Online gating.** Actions that need the other player online (Whisper, Invite, Examine) are disabled with a reason when the target is offline: `{name} is offline.`
- **Same-place gating for Examine.** Examine on a character who is not at your place is disabled with `{name} isn't here.`
- **Whisper** closes any drawer or sheet first, pre-fills `whisper {name} ` and focuses the composer.
- **Screen argument.** `openScreen('social')` takes no arguments.

## Vitals Rail Party Block (desktop; party2 6a)

The real `PartyBlock.vue` is extended. The order from the top of the rail's party area is:

1. **Your pet row** (when you have a pet, even when solo). It sits directly under your own vitals block (45 identity and bars).
2. **`⋯` on your identity row**, in a party only: 28px, `aria-label="Actions for yourself"`, which opens the self menu.
3. **`Travel with leader` switch**: members only (not the leader, not solo). See Travel with Leader.
4. The 47 header: `h6` `Party · {n}` (or `Party`) and `Invite`.
5. **Follow summary**, in a party with at least one other member.
6. **Stamina warning**, when it applies.
7. **Member cards** (others only, as today; in combat the 48 `You` card comes first). Each card's name row:
   - `[online dot] name [crown] class (ellipsis) ……… [follow icon] Lv {n} · {s} st [⋯]`.
   - The dot is `aria-hidden`; the card's accessible text includes `online` / `offline`.
   - The follow icon is 12px with `title` and an `.sr-only` twin.
   - `Lv {n} · {s} st` is 51.
   - The `⋯` is a 28px sibling button outside the 48 ally-target button. The card is laid out as `[target area][⋯]`, so the `⋯` is never nested.
8. **Each member's pet row** directly under that member's card.
9. **Incoming invite card** (while pending).

Right-click on a member card or on your identity row opens the same menu as its `⋯` (desktop).

## Pets (party2 6a; owner: name, level, HP, expiry only)

- **Source.** `active_pet` (public), subscribed by `characterId` for yourself and every party member (out of combat too; today only the current fight's pets are subscribed). One pet per character.
- **Where pets show:**
  - under your own vitals in the desktop rail, solo or not;
  - under each party member's card in the desktop rail;
  - as a sub-row under each owner in the Social party table;
  - inside each owner's mobile party card;
  - your own pet in the Social Party column or tab when you are solo, above the solo empty state.

  Pets never show in Nearby, the feed or menus.
- **Row (`PetRow.vue`, a `div`, never a button):**
  - Layout: margin `4px 0 0 16px`, padding `4px 8px`, gap 8, `--radius-sm`, background and ring per Color, `position: relative`. The elbow connector (Exceptions) joins it to the owner's card.
  - `PhPawPrint` 12 (`aria-hidden`).
  - **Line 1** (gap 4): the name (Label 12 / 500, ellipsis, `title`), then `Lv {n}` (Micro 10, `var(--color-neutral-500)`), then right-aligned `{hp}/{max}` (Micro 10, tabular, neutral-500).
  - **Line 2** (gap 4): a 4px HP bar (`--color-health` on `--color-neutral-900`, `flex: 1`, min-width 24). When `expiresAtMicros` is set: `PhHourglassMedium` 10 and `{m:ss}` (Micro 10, `var(--color-stamina)`, tabular, `aria-hidden`) with an `.sr-only` `fades in about {n} minutes`. A pet without an expiry shows no timer.
  - **Accessible text:** an `.sr-only` prefix `Your pet` or `{owner}'s pet`. The bar is `role="progressbar"` with `aria-label="{pet} health {hp} of {max}"`.
- **Not shown:** kind, species, icon variants, ability, cooldown, buffs, targeting, commands and menus (no server data; Mock overrides 4 and 5).
- **Lifecycle.** When the row is deleted (death, expiry, logout, a new summon), the pet row disappears. There is no announcement. Focus never sits on a pet row (it is not focusable).
- **In combat** the pet rows stay under their owners (the 48 ally-target cards are unchanged above them).

### Follow summary (in a party with at least one other member)

  - Member: `{n} of {m} travel with {leader}`.
- It is not live; it updates with the rows.

### Stamina warning

- Shown under the summary when any traveller who would move with the leader (the leader, plus everyone in "Comes along") has stamina below their within-region cost (the shared cost helper from 51).

  - one name: `{name} does not have enough stamina to travel.`
  - two or more: `{A} and {B} do not have enough stamina to travel.`
  - yourself: `You do not have enough stamina to travel.`

  The wording matches the server's refusal.
- It is not live. The Map's checklist (51) gives the exact cost for a chosen destination.

### `Travel with leader` switch (members only)

- A whole-row `button` with `role="switch"` and `aria-checked`, `aria-describedby` set to the sub-line. Layout: padding 8, gap 8, `--radius-md`, background `var(--color-surface)`, hover `var(--color-neutral-800)`, min-height 44 on mobile.

- **Where:** the desktop rail (party block, item 3); the Social Party column under `Loot: personal`; the mobile Party tab.

### Keyboard and focus

- The `⋯` button has `aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. Enter, Space or Arrow Down opens the menu with focus on the first enabled item; Arrow Up opens it with focus on the last.
- Inside the menu:
  - Arrow Up and Down move (wrapping; disabled items are included so their reasons are reachable).
  - Home and End jump.
  - Enter or Space activates.
  - Escape closes the menu (`preventDefault` and `stopPropagation`, so an open drawer stays open) and returns focus to the opener.
  - Tab closes the menu and lets focus move on.
  - Pointer-down outside closes it (the `AccountMenu.vue` pattern).
  - Opening one menu closes any other.
- Inside an inline confirm, focus starts on the safe button (50); Escape returns to the menu items.
- If the opener disappears while the menu is open, the menu closes and focus moves to the list's heading (`tabindex="-1"`).

## Security-related UI (CONTEXT Area 2)

- **No emails.** No surface renders an email:
  - the client never subscribes to `user`;
  - friends and requests come from the new per-sender views (character name, online status, place, and level if available);
  - there is no email input anywhere.
- **Joining needs an invite.** The client never calls `join_group` without a pending invite. The only join path is `Accept` on the invite card. A refusal from the server (for example after expiry) shows as the server's line in the notice line (Social) or the feed (rails).
- **The online count** comes from the count-only view. No list of online players is shown.
- **Offline characters** drop out of Nearby, `look` and `who` (server). Party rows keep offline members, shown as offline.
