## Shared Components (SOCIAL-DIFF section C; built in 51.1 so 52.2 does not churn shared code)

### `StatusDot.vue`

- Props: `status: 'online' | 'busy' | 'offline'`. An 8 x 8 circle, `aria-hidden="true"`. The host always carries the status word in its accessible text (`online`, `in combat`, `offline`); the dot is never the only signal.
- Visuals: online filled `var(--color-con-light-green)`; offline hollow, inset 1px `var(--color-neutral-600)`; busy filled `var(--color-con-yellow)` (provisional; 52.2 confirms the busy colour).
- **51.1 feeds `online` and `offline` only** (from `character.online`). `busy` is supported and tested, not fed (assumption B15).

### `CharacterName.vue`

- One component for every member and player name in 51.1: the rail member cards (in and out of combat), the self identity rows (rail and mobile), the mobile grid cards, the mobile Party sheet cards, the menu header, Nearby player rows, the invite card's inviter and chips, and outgoing invite rows.
- Renders the name as a text node in a `span` (single line, ellipsis, `title` = full name), with optional `you` (appends ` (you)` in `var(--color-neutral-500)` where the surface asks for it) and a **trailing default slot**. 51.1 passes nothing to the slot; 52.2 puts the guild tag there, in one place.
- Size and weight come from the host (each surface sets its own role below).
