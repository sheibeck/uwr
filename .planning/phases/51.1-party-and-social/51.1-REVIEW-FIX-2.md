### WR-01: invite → leave → invite skipped the re-invite wait

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/group_invites.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/groups.ts` (comment only), `spacetimedb/src/reducers/groups.integration.test.ts`
**Commit:** bd61c963
**Applied fix:**
- `endInvite` starts the wait for a decline, a cancel, or a `withdrawn` end that the inviter caused himself. That means the `actor` is the inviter.
- When nobody is left, `settleGroupAfterLeave` passes the leaving character as the actor. That covers `leave_group` and camping, including the AFK camp.
- An expiry starts no wait. Neither does a withdrawal someone else caused: a deletion (the deleted character's waits are removed anyway), or another member leaving last.
- Tests:
  - invite → leave → re-invite at T+2 is refused with `Wait a moment before inviting Bram again.`, and goes through once the wait ends;
  - a ten-round leave/re-invite loop sends Bram one invite line;
  - camping out of the lone group writes the wait row;
  - a withdrawal the inviter did not cause writes none.

### WR-02: A 2-member party's Leave prompt named a successor, but the server dissolves the group

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/data/group_config.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/data/group_config.test.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/helpers/group_invites.ts`, `src/social/playerMenu.ts`, `src/social/playerMenu.test.ts`, `src/social/PlayerMenu.component.test.ts`
**Commit:** edf0be89
**Applied fix:**
- New pure shared rule `leaveOutcome({ remaining, liveInvites })` → `'empty' | 'disbands' | 'stays'` in `group_config.ts`. The client reads it through `@game-data/group_config`.
- `settleGroupAfterLeave` now branches on it (same behaviour as before), and so does the client prompt. The two cannot drift.
- Prompt copy: `Leave the party? The party disbands.` when the outcome is `disbands`. That is one member left and no live invite out, for the leader or a member. Otherwise the leader still sees ` Leadership passes to {name}.`
- The server's own wording (`The group has disbanded.`) is a past-tense notice, so it does not fit a question asked before the action. The prompt uses plain copy in line with the UI-SPEC's "party" wording instead. The string is exported as `LEAVE_DISBANDS`.
- Tests:
  - `leaveOutcome` cases;
  - the four 2-member cases (leader or member, with or without a live invite);
  - a component test that the leader of a 2-member party sees "The party disbands." and no successor.
- An existing test used `memberCount: 2` for "no known successor". It now uses 3.

### WR-05: A group kept alive by a live invite later dissolved without telling its last member

**Files modified:** `spacetimedb/src/helpers/group_invites.ts`, `spacetimedb/src/reducers/groups.integration.test.ts`
**Commit:** 69e11e30
**Applied fix:**
- `endInvite` dissolves first, then writes its lines. If the dissolve leaves a member solo who is not the inviter, that member is told with the same `{line} The group has disbanded.` pattern:
  - If he already gets a reason line (a canceller), that line gets the suffix: `You cancelled the invite to Cole. The group has disbanded.`
  - Otherwise he gets an onlooker line: `Cole declined the invite. The group has disbanded.`, `The invite to Cole expired. The group has disbanded.`, `Bram cancelled the invite to Cole. …`, or `The invite to Cole is no longer open. …`.
- A solo inviter's own auto-group still dissolves silently, as before.
- `settleGroupAfterLeave` now ends expired invites quietly (internal `finishInvite(..., announceDisband = false)`). Its own `{departure line} The group has disbanded.` stays the only notice, so there is no double line.
- Tests (leader Ann invites Cole, then leaves, so Bram leads alone):
  - Cole declines;
  - the invite expires;
  - Bram cancels;
  - a solo inviter's decline gets no extra line.
