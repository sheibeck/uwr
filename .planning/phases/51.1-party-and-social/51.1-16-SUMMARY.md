# Phase 51.1 Plan 16: Mobile Party sheet Summary

On a phone the Party tab opens a Social sheet whose body is the whole party: the invite card, the header with a 44px Invite, the follow summary and stamina warning, the Travel with leader switch, your self card, member cards with places and menus, pets, Loot, outgoing invites and a NoticeLine that shows party refusals. Every party action is reachable at 44px or more, and no email reaches any party surface.

## What was built

- **`NoticeLine.vue`**: `kinds?: ReadonlySet<string>`, default `() => MIRRORED_KINDS` (still system, reward and heal). The mirror filter reads `props.kinds`. A `group` line gets the info icon. Header comment updated.
- **`MemberCard.vue`**:
  - `sheet` variant: card `var(--color-bg)`, `--radius-md`, padding `8px 16px`, gap 8. Row 1 is `[StatusDot][CharacterName][crown][FollowIcon]`. Line 2 is `.member-line` = `Lv {n} · {Here | place | Offline} · {s} st` (12px neutral-500, low mark kept). The `Lv` and stamina parts are `flex: none; white-space: pre` and only `.member-place` ellipsizes. Then the 4px health bar (no resource bar, no class text) and the PlayerMenu with `size="sheet"` (44 x 44).
  - `self` prop: your name with ` (you)`, your follow state, `Lv {n} · {s} st`, no bars, ⋯ labelled "Actions for yourself".
  - Place: Offline for an offline member, Here when `member.locationId` equals your `game.character.locationId`, else the name from `game.locations`, else no text.
- **`PartyBlock.vue`**: `variant` prop; the rail and combat recipes are byte-for-byte the same output. The sheet order is exactly UI-SPEC "Mobile Party Sheet" items 1 to 9. `ul.cards` holds the `li.member-entry` sheet cards; `div.self-entry` holds your card and pet row; `div.solo-pet` holds the solo pet row; solo shows `EmptyState` (PhUsersThree, "You're travelling alone.", "Invite someone by name, or use the menu on a player in Nearby.").
- **`SocialScreen.vue`**: mobile branch is `PartyBlock variant="sheet"` then `NoticeLine :kinds="SHEET_KINDS"` (`system`, `group`) as the last child. No rejection counter is passed (no runner lives at this level; menu and card rejections print to the feed themselves). Desktop placeholder untouched.
- **`mobileTargets.test.ts`**: 11 listed controls judged at 390px, completeness over every `<button` in src/social (plus the one in PartyBlock), a stale-entry check, a failing fixture (40px, desktop-only, missing rule, one axis short), and source pins that the sheet really passes the 44px props.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical functionality] The sheet variant never renders the combat recipe**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (reading what the completeness list had to say about `CombatMemberCard`)
- **Issue:** The plan has the sheet reuse PartyBlock, whose out-of-combat/combat switch is `game.combat.active`. If a fight starts while the sheet is open, the sheet would flip to the desktop `CombatMemberCard` (28px ⋯, no self card), breaking the 44px promise.
- **Fix:** `outOfCombat = !inCombat || isSheet`; the summary, warning and Loot line still hide in a fight; the switch and Invited list already hide themselves in combat. Test: "the sheet variant keeps its own recipe if a fight starts under it".
- **Files modified:** src/rails/PartyBlock.vue, src/rails/PartyBlock.test.ts
- **Commit:** b073f4bf

**2. [Plan wording] Task 2 RED**
- The plan expected the RED run to find a defect or fail until the list was complete. None of the controls was under its size, so the only RED evidence is the mutation described above and the fixture cases.

No other deviations. No STATE.md or ROADMAP.md edits (as instructed).
