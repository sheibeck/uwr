## Planner decisions
- **Order helpers** live in `src/map/order.ts` (`compareBigint`, `compareNames` with `localeCompare(b, undefined, { sensitivity: 'base' })`), because `src/rails/levelRange.ts` keeps its own private copy and is not edited here.
- **"About n minutes"** reads `about 1 minute` for one and `about {n} minutes` otherwise, where n is the remaining seconds divided by 60, rounded up, at least 1. The UI-SPEC examples use the plural; the singular is plain English (recorded for the owner).
- **Unknown terrain word:** the server value with its first letter upper-cased, or `Place` when the value is empty. The icon is PhMapPin (UI-SPEC).
- **Region level range** uses the known places only and skips safe places (research A10). Uncharted places have no level and are skipped too.
