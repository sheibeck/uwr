## Planner decisions (gaps the UI-SPEC leaves open)
- **Several followers on a timer:** the WAIT row label joins their names (', ' and ' and ') with "can't cross yet", and the time is the longest. Several short of stamina: label '{A and B} are short on stamina', detail 'Party travel is all or nothing.'; one short follower keeps the UI-SPEC detail 'Needs {n}, has {s}. Party travel is all or nothing.'
- **Uncharted note with followers:** 'Travelling here opens a new region.' then ' {n} following.' for a leader with followers, or ' Only you travel.' for a member (the UI-SPEC says "plus the follower part" without a format).
- **Passage tag:** a passage's terrain tag already reads 'Passage' with PhDoorOpen, so it is one tag, not two.
- **Several roles on one quest:** one card per quest; its role line joins the roles in the order Giver here, Goal here, Pick up here with ' · '.
- **Unknown party rows:** a following member whose character row is not readable is left out of the prediction; the server's refusal line is still the truth (shown in the notice line or the feed).
- **No teleport (research O3):** no ability moves a character to a non-adjacent place today, so the far-place detail offers only 'Select first stop'.

## Findings (planner, code read 2026-10-07)
- performTravel order on the server: combat, gathering, connection, stamina (all travellers), cooldown (cross-region only). The client order is the UI-SPEC blocking order; whatever the server refuses first prints as its own line.
- Party data on the client (`src/game/context.ts` GameData): `group` (leaderCharacterId), `groupMembers` (characterId, followLeader), `knownCharacters` (party and inviters, with stamina, locationId, racialTravelCostIncrease and racialTravelCostDiscount), `effects` (own and party character_effect rows), `gathers` (the active character's gather rows), `quests`, `questTemplates` (npcId, targetLocationId, sourceLocationId, name, requiredCount). travel_cooldown rows for you and the party come from the map hub (plan 51-07); here they are a plain input.
- `src/rails/quests.ts` trackedQuests gives each active quest's `countText` (the progress) and order; reuse it for the quest cards.
