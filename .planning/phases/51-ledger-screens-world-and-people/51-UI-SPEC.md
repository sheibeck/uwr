## Owner requests during the UI-SPEC run (2026-10-06, relayed by the coordinator; these override anything below)

1. **Map layout follows the revised mock `UWR Map.dc.html` 11a** (imported 2026-10-06, `MAP-EXTRACT.md`). The map drawer covers the center feed and the right rail (x 252 to 1280 at 1280x800); the left 252px vitals rail stays visible, and the build keeps the **real** console vitals rail, not the mock's reduced copy (no Keeper quote, Stamina kept). A title row holds `Map`, the region chips and the close button; below it a canvas and a 300px detail column; the legend is a pill floating on the canvas. Mobile is unchanged from 2d (this contract keeps it as a sheet above the tab bar, Mock overrides 32).
   - **More map changes are coming** (owner, 2026-10-06): the map and the rail's routes panel will show where regions meet. The Map Contract and the rail's "Here card and routes" subsection are self-contained so both can be revised in one pass when that mock arrives.
2. **Regions lock only by the crossing timer** (owner correction, supersedes "no locked regions"). After crossing into another region the server sets the caller's `travel_cooldown` (5 minutes base, shortened by the renown perk `travelCooldownReduction`, travel songs and other travel-speed effects). While it runs, **every other region's chip** shows `PhLockSimple`, dimmed, with the remaining time (`Bellspire Reach · 3:12`), and cross-region Travel buttons are unavailable with `You can cross into another region in 3:12.` Travel within the region is unaffected. The client shows the remaining time from the caller's own `travel_cooldown` row (`readyAtMicros` minus now) and **never computes reductions itself**. The countdown is announced politely, not every second (see Map > Region chips). With no timer running, no region is locked.
3. **Bind stone row in Nearby.** When `location.bindStone` is true at the current place, Nearby shows a bind stone row with a `Bind` action (the existing `bind_location({ characterId })` reducer), the bound state `Bound here` when `character.boundLocationId` equals the current place, and the Examine eye. Phosphor 2.2.1 has no standing-stone glyph; the closest fitting icon is **`PhCastleTurret`** (an upright stone structure). The same icon marks the bind point and bind stones on the Map, so the game has one bind symbol. The mock's `ph-bed` is replaced (Mock overrides row 4). See Context rail > Nearby.
4. **"Hail" becomes a Talk icon.** NPC rows in Nearby lose the word "hail": the hint becomes `NPC`, and a `PhChatCircleDots` icon button labelled `Talk to {name}` performs the same hail (`consoleApi.hail`). The feed keyword label becomes `Talk to {name}` too (decided: yes, for consistency). Row action icons are laid out as one cluster with the Examine eye and the `⋯` menu (Context rail > Nearby > Row anatomy).

## Shared rules for the three screens

The Phase 50 "Shared Ledger Screen Rules" apply unchanged: screen root and scroll, the notice line (`NoticeLine`, newest private line after the screen opened, polite status), pending (inert until the promise settles, no optimistic changes), offline (actions disabled, reading stays), combat (these screens cannot open in combat and close when it starts), and disabled with a reason (`aria-disabled`, focusable, reason line with `aria-describedby`).

Phase 51 adds:

- **Online gating.** Actions that need another player online (Whisper, Invite, Examine) are disabled with a reason when the target is offline: `{name} is offline.`
- **Same-place gating for Examine.** `look` covers the current place and, after the server change, its neighbouring places only. Examine on a character, NPC or enemy that is not at your place is disabled with `{name} isn't here.`
- **Actions whose result lands in the feed.** Whisper pre-fills the composer, and Talk and Examine print in the feed. On desktop the composer and feed sit under the drawer, so **Whisper closes the drawer first**, then pre-fills `whisper {name} ` and focuses the composer. Talk and Examine from inside a drawer close it too. On mobile, any of these from inside a sheet closes the sheet and returns to Story. From the context rail (desktop) nothing closes: the feed is visible.
- **Timers.** Visible timers re-render on a shared 1-second tick only where they show seconds (invite expiry `m:ss`, travel cooldown `m:ss`); minute timers (`2h 14m`) re-render every 60 seconds (47 rail card rule). The visible timer text is `aria-hidden`. Screen readers get a static sentence that changes at most once a minute (for example `Expires in about 3 minutes`) and is **not** a live region. Only arrival events (a new invite) are announced, politely, once.
- **Screen arguments.** `openScreen('map', { locationId })` opens the map with that place selected (its region shown). `openScreen('events', { eventId })` opens with that event selected. `openScreen('social')` takes no arguments.

## Map Contract (LDG-04, LDG-05)

### What is known (data rule, CONTEXT Area 1)

- **Visited** places come from the new per-character visited table (written on arrival and on creation or first spawn), exposed through a `my_*` view.
- **Heard of** = a place connected (`location_connection`) to a visited place and not itself visited. Derived on the client.
- Places neither visited nor heard of are **not drawn**, not listed and not counted.
- **Regions known** = regions with at least one visited or heard-of place.
- The current place is always visited.

### Route graph (one coordinate space, no `<svg>`)

- **Plane.** One absolutely positioned `div` (`.graph-plane`) of explicit pixel size holds the nodes, labels and edges. Every position is in plane pixels, so dots and lines never drift. The plane sits inside the canvas (`overflow: auto`, dark scrollbar rule) and is scaled for zoom with `transform: scale(z)` from its top-left; the canvas scroll size follows `width * z` and `height * z` (a sizer element).
- **Layout (deterministic, pure, tested; `src/map/graphLayout.ts`).**
  1. Start node = the region's start place (research names the rule; recommended: the region's place with `bindStone`, lowest id; fallback lowest id in the region).
  2. Breadth-first search over the region's known places from the start node, using connections between known places. Depth = steps from the start.
  3. Column = depth (start on the left). Pitch 176px; x = 48 + depth * 176.
  4. Inside a column, order by the parent's row index, then name (`localeCompare`, base sensitivity), then id. y = 48 + row * 64, columns centered vertically on the tallest column.
  5. Known places of this region unreachable from the start (a gap in what is known) form extra columns after the last, ordered by name then id.
  6. **Border nodes.** A known place in another region connected to a place in this region is drawn one column to the right of its neighbour's column (or the last column), with a second label line `{Region}` (Micro 10, `var(--color-neutral-500)`).
  7. Plane size = last column x + 176 by tallest column y + 64.
  - The same inputs always give the same layout (no randomness, no time).
- **Edges.** One `div` per undirected connection between two drawn nodes (the server writes two directed rows; dedupe by the unordered pair). Each edge is `position: absolute` at the first node's center, `width` = the distance between centers, `height` 1px, `transform-origin: 0 50%`, `transform: rotate(θ)` with θ = `atan2(dy, dx)`. Edges render before nodes so dots sit on top. Edges are `aria-hidden`.
- **Path highlight.** The shortest path (breadth-first, over drawn nodes, ties by id) from the current place to the selected node is redrawn as 2px accent edges on top. When the current place is not in the shown region, no path is drawn and the detail says how far it is not known.
- **Nodes.** Each node is a `button` (the hit box, 32px desktop and 44px mobile, transparent, centered on the dot) containing the dot (`aria-hidden`); the label is a sibling text element to the right (x + 16, vertically centered), one line, ellipsis at 144px, full text in `title`.
- **Zoom stack** (bottom-right of the canvas, `position: absolute`, offsets 8): `Zoom in` (`PhPlus`), `Zoom out` (`PhMinus`), `Center on you` (`PhCrosshair`); `.btn .btn-secondary .btn-icon`, 32px (44 mobile). Zoom steps 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5; default 1. Zoom in and Zoom out are `aria-disabled` at the ends. Center scrolls the current place (or the start node if you are elsewhere) into the middle of the canvas.
- **On open**, the canvas scrolls so the current place is visible, and nothing moves on its own afterwards.
- **Canvas label** (top-left, Micro 10 uppercase 0.1em, `var(--color-neutral-500)`, not in the plane): `{Region} · known routes`.

- **Roving tabindex.** The graph is one tab stop (`role="group"`, `aria-label="{Region} route graph"`). - **Node `aria-label`:** `{name}, {state}{, bind point}{, bind stone}{, uncharted}, {level label}, {n} steps from here` (or `, you are here`; `, distance unknown` when you are elsewhere). `aria-pressed` on the selected node.
- **List view.** A view switch at the canvas top-right on desktop (`position: absolute`, offsets 8) and above the canvas on mobile (`SegTabs`, tablist label `Map view`, tabs `Graph` with `PhGraph` and `List` with `PhListBullets`). Selecting a row selects the node exactly as in the graph. The List view is the full equivalent for screen-reader and keyboard users, and the graph's focus order matches it.

- **A11. Remove friend is in the friend menu** (the reducer exists and the old client had it), with a confirmation.
