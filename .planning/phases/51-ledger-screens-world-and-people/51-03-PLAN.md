## Decisions
- **Coordinator decision 1 (A1):** the sweep sends an offline character in a passage to the place recorded as `fromLocationId` on its visited row for the passage when that place is an own-region neighbour of the passage (same regionId as the passage), else to the lowest-id own-region neighbour. A character who crossed in from the far side therefore lands on the passage's own region side, never across.
- **Coordinator decision 2:** no collapse inside applyWorldStartResult; the departure trigger and the sweep cover it (a freshly renamed, unoccupied passage lasts at most one sweep interval).
- **Planner, sweep interval:** 5 minutes (`PASSAGE_SWEEP_INTERVAL_MICROS = 300_000_000n`, the sweep_inactivity value; research A3). "Offline" means no player row has the character as activeCharacterId (`onlineCharacterIds`); Phase 51.1 replaces that body with its stored online flag.
- **Planner, the moved offline character:** the move is silent (no feed line; the player sees the place on return through the normal arrival look) and marks the new place visited with no origin. No new player-facing copy.
- **Planner, no admin reducer:** the first sweep tick is the one-time cleanup (research O6). Fewer client-callable surfaces.
- **Planner, re-home target:** the lowest-id own-side neighbour (`passageSides(...).own[0]`).
- **Planner, event_spawn_item is re-homed, not deleted:** the rows are a live world event's collectibles. `collect_event_item` (`reducers/world_events.ts` about line 51) requires the character to stand at the item's locationId and counts each pickup as event contribution, and `helpers/world_events.ts` (about line 252) deletes them by event when the event ends. Deleting them at collapse would silently take contribution away from an active event and drop collected history; re-homing keeps them reachable on the own side, the same treatment as event_objective. event_spawn_enemy is deleted because its enemy_spawn row is deleted (an enemy group cannot stand in a deleted place, and passages are isSafe, where spawns are already cleared).
- **Planner, enemy_spawn children:** deleting an enemy_spawn row also deletes its enemy_spawn_member rows through `enemy_spawn_member.by_spawn`, the pattern of `helpers/location.ts` ensureLocationRuntimeBootstrap (about lines 325-330), so no member row is orphaned.
- **Planner, kept columns:** visited_location.fromLocationId on rows at other places keeps the deleted id as history; it is read only from a passage's own visited row, and a deleted id matches no place in any later own-side list. event_location (`event: true`) rows are transient and never persist past the transaction, so they are ignored.
