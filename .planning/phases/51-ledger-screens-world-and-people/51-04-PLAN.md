## Planner decisions (UI-SPEC open items and gaps)
- **O2 start node:** lowest-id known region place with bindStone, else lowest-id known region place (research recommendation; positions may shift when places are discovered, still deterministic).
- **Unreachable places (step 5):** each unreachable group is its own breadth-first layout rooted at its first place by name then id, appended as further columns, repeated until every region place is placed.
- **Parent of a node:** the neighbour at depth minus 1 that comes first in the previous column's row order.
- **Free row for a border node:** work in plane y: a y is free when no node already in that outer column lies within 72px; try the neighbour's y, then y − 72, y + 72, y − 144, y + 144 and so on (ties go up). Border nodes are placed in order of (neighbour column, neighbour y, name, id).
- **Label boxes:** 144 x 36; right of the node starting at centre x + 20, or for left-outer nodes ending at centre x − 20; top at centre y − 10. Gate pill boxes are 176 x 24 centred on the gate point (the region name inside ellipsizes at 144). The final shift moves every element so the smallest x and y are 48; width and height are the largest extent plus 48.
- **Caption:** at box x + 16, box y + 16 (UI-SPEC); its visual fit is part of the backstop layout check (plan 51-11).
- **Aria level part:** ', level {a} to {b}, {band}', or ', level {a}, {band}' when the range is one level. Uncharted places carry ', uncharted' and no level part.
