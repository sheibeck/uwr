These starting values passed in the prototype; tune them only as far as the tests allow.

The steps:
1. **Collect.** Gather the shown-region places, the border places (other-region places with an edge to a shown-region place) and the drawn edges.
2. **Seed.** Take the breadth-first order from the start over the drawn graph, with neighbours ordered by compareNames then compareBigint. Unreachable shown-region places continue the order, starting from the next one by name and then id. The k-th node in that order starts at radius sqrt(k) and angle k x π(3 − √5).
3. **Solve.**
   - Compute all-pairs graph distances by breadth-first search. An unreachable pair gets the longest finite distance plus 1.
   - Each pair's target distance is d to the power BEND, and its weight is 1 / target².
   - Run 200 Gauss-Seidel stress-majorization sweeps in seed order. Separate coincident points along a fixed direction derived from their seed indexes.
   - Then run up to 60 sweeps that push apart, symmetrically, any pair closer than 1 unit.
   - Keep the positions in Float64Array.
4. **Orient.** Rotate about the shown-region centroid so that the principal axis lies along the canvas's longer side, or horizontally with no canvas. The principal-axis angle is ½·atan2(2Sxy, Sxx − Syy). Then flip x and y so the start place is in the top-left quadrant.
5. **Sides (MS-04).** For each border place:
   - Let v be the mean of its in-region neighbours minus the centre of the shown-region unit box, divided by the box's half extents.
   - If the length of v is under 0.35, use the border place's solved position minus that mean instead.
   - The side is left or right (by the sign of vx) only when |vx| > 1.25·|vy|; otherwise it is top or bottom (by the sign of vy).
6. **Scale (MS-02, MS-03).**
   - kx = k and ky = k·r, where r is the canvas's aspect relative to the region's unit box, clamped to [1/MAX_STRETCH, MAX_STRETCH]. With no canvas, r is 1.
   - kMin is the smallest k that puts every shown-region pair at least the minimum gap apart (MIN_NODE_GAP, or COMPACT_MIN_NODE_GAP when compact).
   - With a canvas, bisect k over [kMin, max(kMin, MAX_EDGE)] in 12 steps for the largest k whose full placement (steps 7 to 11) succeeds and fits inside the canvas. If kMin does not fit, use kMin. With no canvas, use kMin.
7. **Region labels.** Place them greedily, the start first and then in reading order. Try these slots in order: right, left, below, above, downRight, upRight, downLeft, upLeft.
   - Offsets: the right box starts hit-half + 4 to the right of the centre, with its top at centre − 10 (centre − 8 when compact). Below and above are centred, hit-half + 4 off the centre. The diagonals start 8 from the centre.
   - A slot is valid when it keeps LABEL_CLEAR from every hit box and every placed label.
   - Among the valid slots, pick the lowest score, where score = 10 x (the number of edge sample points inside the box, 15 per edge, ignoring the place's own edges) + the slot index.
   - align is 'right' for left-side slots, 'center' for above and below, and 'left' otherwise.
8. **Outline.** Take the box around the shown-region hit boxes and labels, padded by REGION_PAD (REGION_PAD_TOP at the top). With a canvas, grow it symmetrically to the canvas minus 2 x PLANE_MARGIN minus the bands of the sides in use. A top or bottom band is OUTER_GAP + hit-half + 8; a left or right band is OUTER_GAP + LABEL_WIDTH / 2 + 4.
9. **Caption (desktop only; MS-07).** The caption box is CAPTION_WIDTH x CAPTION_HEIGHT, inset CAPTION_INSET in the first corner (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right) that keeps 8px from hit boxes, LABEL_CLEAR from labels and GATE_CLEAR from every gate's first candidate box (step 11). If no corner is clear, add a strip of CAPTION_INSET + CAPTION_HEIGHT on top of the outline and use the top-left corner.
10. **Border places.**
    - Each sits OUTER_GAP outside its side. Its coordinate along the side comes from the scaled solve, clamped to the side.
    - Sort them along the side, then spread them. Top and bottom: at least hit + 20 + LABEL_WIDTH + 16 apart. Left and right: at least 2 x hit-half + 2 x label height + 24 apart.
    - If the last one runs past the end of the side, pull the group back.
    - Label slots by side:
      - top: right, left, above, upRight, upLeft;
      - bottom: right, left, below, downRight, downLeft;
      - left and right: above, below, the outer side, then the outer diagonals.
    - A border label is valid only if it does not intersect the outline and keeps the clearances.
11. **Gates (CR-01).** The candidates, tried in order:
    - (1) the point where the edge meets its side of the outline;
    - (2) points along the edge in 8px steps, alternating toward the far and the near node, strictly between them;
    - (3) points along the outline side in 8px steps, alternating directions.
    The first candidate whose gate box keeps GATE_CLEAR from every hit box, label, the caption and every placed gate wins. The gate box is GATE_WIDTH x GATE_HEIGHT, or GATE_WIDTH x COMPACT_GATE_HEIGHT when compact, which covers the pill and its ::after tap slop.
12. **Grow.** If any label or gate found no valid spot, multiply k by 1.2 and redo steps 7 to 11, at most 8 times. Return the last attempt.
13. **Finish.**
