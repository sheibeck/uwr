<objective>
Wire the segment contract into `llm_apply.ts`: NPC replies are normalized into Keeper narration and NPC dialogue segments; creation, arrival, skill and renown text is stored as Keeper narration segments; every Keeper-voice fallback and failure line on a narrative route carries one Keeper narration segment; `message` is always `flattenSegments(segments)`.

Purpose: SEG-01 and SEG-02 for every narrative route except combat (46-03), and the apply half of SEG-04. Plumbing only: no prompt, route block or wording changes (SEG-03 approval comes in 46-06). The current NPC prompt still asks for a `dialogue` field, so live NPC replies salvage cleanly through the legacy step until 46-07 lands the approved reply shape.

Output: updated `llm_apply.ts`, new NPC cases in `llm_apply.test.ts`, a reviewed characterization re-record.
</objective>

<interfaces>
From 46-01 (`spacetimedb/src/helpers/segments.ts`), used here:
- `segmentsFromReply(resultText, { present, playerNames, fallbackLine, legacyDialogueSpeaker? })` returns `{ segments, source, parsed }`; `parsed` is undefined when the reply is not a JSON object.
- `keeperSegments(text)` (paragraphs to at most 6 Keeper narration segments), `keeperFallback(line)` (exactly one Keeper narration segment), `flattenSegments(segments)`.
- `appendPrivateEvent(ctx, characterId, ownerUserId, kind, message, segments?)`, `appendCreationEvent(ctx, playerId, kind, message, segments?)`.

Local helpers this plan adds inside llm_apply.ts (NOT exported, NOT added to events.ts):
- `writePrivateSegments(ctx, characterId, ownerUserId, kind, segs)` calls `appendPrivateEvent(ctx, characterId, ownerUserId, kind, flattenSegments(segs), segs)`.
- `writeCreationSegments(ctx, playerId, kind, segs)` calls `appendCreationEvent(ctx, playerId, kind, flattenSegments(segs), segs)`.
</interfaces>
