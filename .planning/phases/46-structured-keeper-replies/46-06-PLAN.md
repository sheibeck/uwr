Owner voice rules (binding, from the orchestrator and project memory):
- The Keeper narrates what happens around the player in the second person, as in the Ledger console mock (narration "The Keeper": "You peer into the well. It is deep, dark and wet..."; dialogue "The Ferryman": "Mind the current, traveller.").
- The Keeper is male (he/his) and NEVER speaks in the first person (the owner retracted the 44 Fix 1 "I" direction).
- Every NPC is male or female; never it or they for an in-world person.
- The player is always "you", never a third-person pronoun or a gendered noun.
- The player's own speech is never a segment speaker and is not echoed back as "You say".

Block marker convention (used by 46-07 to 46-10 to check that the applied source equals the approved text): every change is a pair of fenced `text` blocks, each preceded on its own line by an HTML comment marker:
- `<!-- voice:before id=<id> file=<repo-relative path> -->` followed by a fenced text block holding the exact current source text (template-literal source form, including any `${...}` placeholders and escapes).
- `<!-- voice:after id=<id> file=<repo-relative path> -->` followed by a fenced text block holding the exact proposed source text in the same form.
- Id prefixes by applying plan: `bible-` and `route-` (46-07), `combat-` (46-08), `string-` and `fallback-` (46-09), `fix2-` (46-10). A computed proposal that cannot be compared verbatim (for example a per-call budget line built from numbers) uses `<!-- voice:proposal id=fix2-... file=... -->` instead and is verified by tests.
- At recording time a change the owner rejects gets its marker changed from `voice:after` to `voice:rejected`.
