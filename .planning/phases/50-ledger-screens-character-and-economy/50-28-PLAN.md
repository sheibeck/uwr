## Findings (planner, code read 2026-10-06)

### planCraft today (spacetimedb/src/data/crafting_rules.ts:430-557)
- `need(templateId, count)` merges requirements per template. Materials are checked first (reason 'materials', message 'Missing materials to craft this recipe.'), then for gear the essence gate, the catalyst count, the reagent slots by quality and the reagent counts.
- The catalyst and each reagent are checked as `countOf(id) - consumed(id) < 1n`. With a count n the check becomes `< n`, and each `need(...)` call is multiplied by n. The rule stays linear, so the batch need of a template is exactly n times its per-craft merged need.
- Callers: craft_recipe (items_crafting.ts:178) and the client crafting model (src/crafting/craftingModel.ts:512-557). Neither passes a count, so both keep today's behavior.

### Salvage yield today (spacetimedb/src/reducers/items_crafting.ts:373-435)
- Material: `getMaterialForSalvage(slot, armorType, tier)`, then `SALVAGE_YIELD_BY_TIER[tier] ?? 2n`, capped by `itemValue / materialValue` when materialValue > 0, then capped by the matching recipe's consumption of that material when it is above 0 and below the yield.
- Reagent: the statKeys of the item's non-implicit affixes filter CRAFTING_MODIFIER_DEFS. The roll is `(timestamp + instanceId * 13) % 100` against a hard-coded 12.
- Material templates are upserted from MATERIAL_DEFS with the same vendorValue (helpers/items.ts:699-717), and all nine salvage materials are MATERIAL_DEFS entries. So the client can value them from the shared data.

### Generated descriptions today (spacetimedb/src/data/recipe_rules.ts:544-614)
- `generatedOutput` writes a fixed two-material sentence at line 596. The tests that pin it are recipe_rules.test.ts:297 and recipe_discovery.test.ts:224-225 (`generatedTemplates` filters on that sentence).
- The facts available there: category, `chosen` (slot, armorType, weaponType, baseDamage, dps, armorClass, buffType), primary and secondary (name, key), level, the accessory stat, and the food magnitude `levelStep(level)`.
- Kinds: weapon primary is metal and secondary is hide, cloth or wood. Armor primary is hide or cloth and secondary is hide, cloth or metal. Accessory primary is a trinket and secondary is cloth, hide or metal. Consumable primary is edible and secondary is base or edible (recipe_rules.ts:94-111).

### Food labels (spacetimedb/src/reducers/hunger.ts:1-7, used at 75)
