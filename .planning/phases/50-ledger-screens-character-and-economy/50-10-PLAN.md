| client -> reducers | Object arguments only; no prices or stats sent |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-50-33 | Information disclosure | item_instance, item_affix public tables | low | accept | Pre-existing public tables (not widened); the hub subscribes filtered to the active character; the last sale comes only from the per-sender view |
| T-50-34 | Denial of service (client memory, subscription size) | template and affix OR chains | medium | mitigate | Keys limited to ids the screens need (owned, rolled or equipped, open vendor, known recipes); empty lists never subscribe; A2 noted for UAT |
| T-50-35 | Tampering (desync) | optimistic state | low | mitigate | No optimistic state; reducers null while disconnected; rows drive every change |
| T-50-36 | Denial of service (double submit) | reducer spam | low | mitigate | Action runner ignores repeats per key until settled |
