## Decisions (planner, within the owner's rule; the owner may revise the numbers at UAT)
- **Chance by material tier** (MATERIAL_DEFS tier through `primaryMaterialTier(name)`; an unknown material counts as tier 1): tier 1 is 50%, tier 2 is 25%, tier 3 and above is 10%. Both sides read the tier from the name, so the server and the client always agree.
- **Which components.**
  - An item some recipe makes: the inputs of the lowest-id recipe that outputs its template. Generated recipes each insert their own output template (items_crafting.ts:89-97), so there is exactly one recipe per output today. The lowest id is also the recipe the client hub keeps (ledgerData.ts:271-276).
  - An item no recipe makes: the slot material, as today.
- **Amounts.**
  - Half, rounded down, with a minimum of 1 when the base is at least 1. The base is the recipe count per item for an input, or the tier and value count for the slot material.
  - Then the strict cap: for every recipe that outputs the template and requires that material, at most (req − 1) / outputCount.
  - Then the full-luck caps, so the sum of all amounts:
    - is at most (total required − 1) / outputCount for every such recipe
    - is worth no more than the item's vendor value
  - The caps trim from the last component backwards, and components left at 0 are dropped.
  - Generated recipes then give: weapon and armor primary (req 3) 1, accessory primary (req 2) 1, every secondary (req 1) never.
- **Rolls.**
  - `salvageSeed(timestampMicros, instanceId, characterId)` is `BigInt.asUintN(64, timestampMicros × 1000003 + instanceId × 7919 + characterId)`.
  - `salvageRoll(seed, index)` is a splitmix64 step over BigInt, giving a value from 0 to 99:
    - z = asUintN(64, seed + (index + 1) × 0x9E3779B97F4A7C15)
    - z = asUintN(64, (z xor (z >> 30)) × 0xBF58476D1CE4E5B9)
    - z = asUintN(64, (z xor (z >> 27)) × 0x94D049BB133111EB)
    - z = z xor (z >> 31)
    - the result is z mod 100
  - Component i hits when its roll is below its chance.
  - The reagent roll `(ts + instanceId × 13) % 100` and the scroll roll `(ts + characterId) % 100` are unchanged.
- **Feed lines and card.**
  - One or more components returned: "You salvaged {item} and received 1x A and 1x B." (one line; a list reads 'A, B and C').
  - No component, but a bonus or scroll: "You salvaged {item}." and then the existing bonus and scroll lines.
  - Nothing at all: the existing "You salvaged {item}, but nothing usable was left.".
  - The result card already shows 'Nothing usable was left.' for a salvage row with no lines (src/ledger/resultCard.ts:217, plan 50-31, tested at resultCard.test.ts:302). It says so plainly and matches the feed line, so it is kept unchanged.
- **Confirm wording** (`confirmText`, used by the Inventory confirm from plan 50-34 and by the Crafting salvage detail in plan 50-38). The lead is 'Salvage destroys this item.', followed by:
  - likely components (25% or more): "It may return some A and B."
  - rare components (under 25%): "C rarely comes back." when likely components exist ("C and D rarely come back." for two or more), else "It may rarely return C."
  - a possible reagent: "It may also give a reagent." (or "It may give a reagent." with no component)
  - no component and no reagent: "Nothing usable will come of it."
  - while the recipe parts are not loaded (not knowable): "It may return some materials." plus the reagent sentence
- **No new reducer, table or column.** The bindings are unchanged.
