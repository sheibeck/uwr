### Claude's discretion (documented choices)
- **Stock display.** '×n' shows for every n of 1 or more, so '×1' tells the player it is the last one. A sold-out row keeps its place in the sort, so rows never jump under the pointer or focus.
- **Picker default.** The picker opens at quantity 1, the safe default, matching InlineConfirm's focus on Keep it.
- **Picker placement and exclusivity.** It opens as an extra table row (desktop) or list item (mobile) directly under its row. Only one picker is open at a time, and it is exclusive with the Sell all junk confirmation.
- **Copy:**
  - prompt `Sell {q} of {n} {name} for {gold} gold?`
  - confirm `Sell {q}`
  - visible controls '1', the minus icon, the number field, the plus icon, 'All'
  - aria-labels 'Set to one', 'One fewer', 'Quantity, 1 to {n}', 'One more', 'Set to all {n}'
  - the group label 'How many to sell'
- **Number field.** A text input with inputmode numeric, not type number, so no native spinners appear and only digits are accepted.
