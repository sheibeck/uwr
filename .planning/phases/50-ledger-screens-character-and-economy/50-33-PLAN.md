## Focus and keys (existing frame behavior)
- **Drawer and Sheet** (src/frame/Drawer.vue:13-31, Sheet.vue:13-31): Tab is trapped at the document by trapTabKeyAtDocument, and Esc closes unless `defaultPrevented`.
- **InlineConfirm** (src/ledger/InlineConfirm.vue:36-55) is the Esc model: a capture-phase document listener that prevents the default.
- **The card** traps Tab on its own element with trapTabKey and stops propagation, so the drawer's document handler never moves focus out of the card.
- **The runner** (src/ledger/actionRunner.ts) sets the pending key synchronously before awaiting the call, and the SDK applies the transaction's rows before it resolves the reducer promise (node_modules/spacetimedb/src/sdk/db_connection_impl.ts:1055-1089). So arming always happens before the row arrives. Opening on the row (not on the promise) also covers a view update that lands later.
