Extract the roughly 700 lines of per-domain apply logic from `submit_llm_result` into `helpers/llm_apply.ts`, keyed on the stored player, and reduce the reducer to a thin wrapper with unchanged behavior.

Output: `helpers/llm_apply.ts`, `helpers/llm_apply.test.ts`, and a slimmed `submit_llm_result` in `index.ts`. The Plan 40-05 characterization tests run unchanged and green.
</objective>
