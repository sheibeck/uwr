> **What this file is.** On 2026-10-07 the owner split Social out of Phase 51.1 into Phase 52.2 "Social and Guilds". The sections below were part of the approved 51.1 UI design contract. They are moved here **verbatim**, so nothing decided for Social is lost.
>
> **What this file is not.** It is not an approved contract for 52.2. It is **input for the 52.2 UI-SPEC**, which will be rebuilt from the owner's new Social mock (`UWR Social.dc.html`, etag `1791379321728978`). Where the mock and these sections disagree, the 52.2 discuss and UI-SPEC decide.

> - **Section B**, section-by-section diff against the sections below (layout, drawer header, mobile tabs, friend rows, requests, empty copy, footnotes, toasts, destructive confirms, status dot, offline rows, menu-open state).
> - **Section E**, the copy deltas (new strings for the 52.2 copy contract).
>
> **What stays in 51.1** (do not re-specify in 52.2 without reason): the vitals rail party block, the in-combat party cards and self target, pets, Travel with leader, the party and player menus, the incoming invite card, outgoing invites in the rail and the mobile Party sheet, the status-dot and name components, and the security fixes (private `user`, CR-01, `join_group` invite rule). 51.1 ships `StatusDot` with three states (`online`, `busy`, `offline`) but feeds only `online` and `offline`; it ships one name component with a slot for the guild tag; `playerMenuEntries()` returns extensible groups so 52.2 can append a guild group; `character.lastOnlineAt` is written by the server from 51.1 on, with no UI.
>
> **Server items carried to 52.2** (ROADMAP 52.2 notes, from `51.1-RESEARCH.md`): private `friend` and `friend_request` with the `my_friend_list` and `my_friend_request_list` views; the online count view `online_player_count`; the party chat filter (`createdAtMicros >= joinedAt`); the `NoticeLine` `kinds` value `friend` (51.1 adds the prop with `system` and `group` for the mobile Party sheet); recorded character ids on friend rows. ## Social Contract (LDG-06, LDG-07)

### Party header

- `h6` `Party` and, in a party, the meta (Label 12, `var(--color-neutral-400)`, tabular): `{n} / 5 · you lead` or `{n} / 5 · led by {leader}`. The 5 is `MAX_GROUP_SIZE` through `@game-data`.
- Buttons (right):
  - `Invite` (`.btn .btn-primary`, `PhUserPlus` 14, row-button size) when you can invite (solo, or the leader with room). It toggles the invite field (`aria-expanded`). When you can't invite, the button stays `aria-disabled` with a reason: `Only the leader can invite.` or `Your party is full.`
  - `Leave` (`.btn .btn-secondary`) in a party.
- `Leave` opens `InlineConfirm` in the header's place: the prompt is `Leave the party?`, plus ` Leadership passes to {name}.` when you lead and others remain. Confirm is `Leave` (`leave_group({ characterId })`); the keep label is `Stay`.
- The follow summary and the stamina warning follow the header (as in the rail).

### Invite field

- An inline row (gap 8) under the header: an `.input` (placeholder `Character name`, `aria-label="Character name to invite"`, autofocus on open) and `Send invite` (`.btn-primary`). Enter submits.
- It calls `invite_to_group({ characterId, targetName })`. On success the field clears and closes, and the invitee appears under `Invited · waiting`. Refusals arrive in the notice line (server text).
- With an empty name, `Send invite` stays `aria-disabled` with no reason line. Esc closes the field (`preventDefault`) and returns focus to `Invite`.

### Party table (desktop)

- `<table class="table">` with the `.sr-only` caption `Party members`. Headers (`th scope="col"`, Micro 10): `Member`, `Where`, `Health`, `Stamina`, and a `.sr-only` `Actions`.
- **Rows.** Leader first, then by join time (`joinedAt`), then by id. Your own row is included.
  - **Member:** the 8px online dot, the name (Label 12 / 500; yours with ` (you)` in `var(--color-neutral-500)`), the crown for the leader (`PhCrownSimple` fill 12, accent, `aria-label="Party leader"`), the follow icon, then a second line (Micro 10, `var(--color-neutral-500)`, ellipsis): `Lv {n} {race} {class}`.
  - **Where:** `Here` (your place), the place name, or `Offline` (Label 12, `var(--color-neutral-400)`, ellipsis).
  - **Health:** a 4px bar, `role="progressbar"`, `aria-label="{name} health {hp} of {max}"`.
  - **Stamina:** `{s}` (Label 12, tabular), with the 51 low mark (`PhWarningCircle` and con-red) below the member's within-region cost. `aria-label="{name} stamina {s} of {max}"`, plus `, too low to travel`.
  - **Actions:** the `⋯` button (28px, `PhDotsThree` 16, `aria-label="Actions for {name}"`, `aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded`) on every row, yours included.
  - **Offline rows:** muted (Color).
  - **Pet sub-row:** directly under its owner, a `tr` with one `td colspan="5"` holding `PetRow`.
  - Right-click on a row opens the same menu at its `⋯`.
- **Unknown member row** (the character row is not subscribed yet): `Member` in the name cell, other cells empty, and no `⋯`.

### Invited · waiting (leader only)

- `h6` `Invited · waiting`, then one row per pending outgoing invite (inset 1px `var(--color-neutral-800)` ring, `--radius-md`, padding 8, gap 8):
  - `PhHourglassMedium` 14 accent-300;
  - the name (Label 12);
  - `Expires in {m:ss}` (Micro 10, `var(--color-neutral-500)`, `aria-hidden`; `.sr-only` `Expires in about {n} minutes`);
  - `Cancel invite` (`.btn .btn-ghost`, row-button size, `aria-label="Cancel invite to {name}"`), which calls the new cancel reducer.
- The section is hidden when there are none. An expired invite disappears when the server removes it.

### Loot

- `Loot: personal` (Label 12, `var(--color-neutral-300)`) with `Each fighter rolls their own loot.` (Label 12, `var(--color-neutral-500)`). It is not interactive. The `Travel with leader` switch (members) follows it.

### Party chat

- A rule (`.hr`), then `h6` `Party chat`.
- **Lines.** This session's `event_group` lines from the feed store, oldest at the top, in a bottom-aligned scroll region (`flex: 1; min-height: 0; overflow-y: auto`; gap 8; Body 14).
  - A chat line `Name: message` renders the name (weight 500, `var(--color-line-whisper)`; yours `var(--color-text)`) and the message (`var(--color-neutral-300)`).
  - Other group notices render in `var(--color-neutral-500)` Label 12.
  - All text nodes; `overflow-wrap: anywhere`.
  - New lines keep the view pinned to the bottom unless the player has scrolled up.
- **Input.** An `.input` (placeholder `Message your party…`, `aria-label="Message your party"`) and `Send` (`.btn-primary`, `PhPaperPlaneRight` 14). Enter sends; it calls `group_message({ characterId, message })`. The field clears on success; empty text keeps `Send` `aria-disabled`.
- **Empty:** `No party messages yet this session.` **Not in a party:** `Join a party to chat with it.`
- The list is `role="log"` with `aria-label="Party chat"` and `aria-live="polite"`.

### Not in a party

The Party column shows:
- the header (`Party`, `Invite`);
- your pet row (if any);
- then `EmptyState` with `PhUsersThree`, `You're travelling alone.` and the body `Invite someone by name, or use the menu on a player in Nearby.`

The invite field works as above.

### People column: friends, online count, requests

- **Header.** `h6` `Friends · {n} online`, then `{n} players online` (Label 12, neutral-400, tabular) from the count-only view; it is omitted until the count applies.
- **Add friend.** An `.input` (placeholder `Add a friend by name`, `aria-label="Add a friend by name"`) and `Send request` (`.btn-primary`, `PhUserPlus` 14). It calls `send_friend_request_to_character({ characterId, targetName })`. **No email field and no email anywhere in the UI.**
- **Friend rows** (`ul`, gap 4). Order: online first, then by name, then by id. Each row has padding 8, gap 8 and min-height 44 on mobile:
  - the 8px dot (`aria-hidden`);
  - the name (Label 12 / 500) and the sub-line (Label 12, neutral-500): online `Lv {n} · {place}` (the `Lv` part is omitted when the view gives no level), offline `Offline`;
  - actions: `Whisper` (`PhChatCircle`, 28px, online only) and `⋯`, with the `aria-label`s `Whisper {name}` and `Actions for {name}`;
  - the row's accessible text includes `online` / `offline`.
- **Friend requests** (`h6` `Friend requests`; hidden when there are none):
  - Incoming: `{name}`, then `Accept` (`.btn-primary`, `aria-label="Accept friend request from {name}"`), which calls `accept_friend_request({ fromUserId })`, and `Decline` (`.btn-secondary`, `aria-label="Decline friend request from {name}"`), which calls `reject_friend_request({ fromUserId })`.
  - Outgoing: `Request sent to {name}` (neutral-500), with no action.
- **Empty friends:** `No friends yet. Add one by name above.`

### Mobile social

- Tabs `Party`, `Chat`, `Friends` (tablist `Social view`). The sheet meta shows `{n}/5` in a party.
- **Party tab:**
  - the invite card (while pending);
  - the header row (`Invite`, `Leave` at 44px), the summary and the warning, and the invite field;
  - member **cards** (`ul`; background `var(--color-bg)`, `--radius-md`, padding `8px 16px`, gap 8). Each card has the dot, crown, name (Body 14 / 500), follow icon, `Lv {n} · {Here | place | Offline} · {s} st` (Label 12, neutral-500, with the low mark), the 4px health bar, the `⋯` (44px), and its pet row;
  - then `Invited · waiting`, `Loot: personal` and the switch (44px).
- **Chat tab:** the log fills the scroll region; the input and `Send` dock at the bottom (`flex: none`, 44px).
- **Friends tab:** the header and count, the add-friend field, friend rows (48px; Whisper and `⋯` at 44px), and requests.

Note for 52.2: until 52.2 ships, 51.1's mobile Party sheet (the `SocialScreen.vue` mobile branch rendering `PartyBlock`) is the mobile home of the party. SOCIAL-DIFF A2 must be decided before 52.2 replaces it.
