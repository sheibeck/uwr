## What was built

- **Server (`groups.ts`):** `set_follow_leader` returns early when `member.followLeader === args.follow`. On a real change it updates the row and calls `appendGroupEvent(ctx, character.groupId, character.id, 'group', ...)` with `{name} is now following the leader.` or `{name} is no longer following the leader.`. The 'Not in a group' and 'Group membership not found' refusals are unchanged. No schema, signature or bindings change.
- **Rail (`VitalsRail.vue`, CRLF kept):** the switch element and its import are gone (rail reads self block, rule, party block). A `selfFollow` computed returns null unless group and character exist, you are not the leader, your member row is present and the leader's character row is known. Otherwise it calls the shared `followState` (absent flag reads as following; online is a literal true; leader place compared with yours). `FollowIcon.self-follow` renders in `.name-row` after the name; it is decorative while the self target button is on (in a fight). The button's aria-label ends with `, {followPhrase}` for a non-leader member only.
- **Self menu:** `playerMenu.ts` and `PlayerMenu.vue` are untouched. New VitalsRail tests prove the entry end to end (the ⋯ and right-click, both flag states, leader has none) through `setFollowLeader({ characterId, follow })`.
- **Docs and tests:** TravelSwitch header comment; `TravelSwitch hosts` describe (PartyBlock sheet is the only element host, VitalsRail has no reference outside comments); 51.1 UI-SPEC updated in 10 places.
