<objective>
Tap (or click) a party member anywhere to make them your ally target, in and out of fights, on mobile and desktop. Mobile member chips also get small health, mana and stamina bars.

Owner, 2026-10-08, verbatim: "on mobile ... clicking party opens up the party menu, but clicking a member of my party also opens up the party menu, when instead it should target that party member. It would be nice to see a small health/mana/stamina bar under each party members name. not too big, but enough to know if they need a heal or something."

Owner, asked where targeting should work: "Everywhere, this was already part of the mock to be able to target by clicking on any given member or pet. So, #1." (#1 = in and out of fights, mobile chips and desktop cards.)

What is built today (checked by the planner):
- Ally targeting exists only in fights. `src/combat/ally.ts` `allyTargetFor` needs an active combat participant; `allyResetNeeded` resets when the fight ends; `src/hotbar/HotbarRow.vue` `useSlot` sends `targetCharacterId` only in combat.
- Mobile out of combat: in `src/frame/VitalsStrip.vue` the Party chip AND each member chip call `openSocial`. In combat, the grid cards call `controller.selectAlly`.
- Desktop: `src/social/CombatMemberCard.vue` (in combat) is already a target button beside its ⋯. `src/social/MemberCard.vue` (rail, out of combat) is a div with no click. `src/frame/VitalsRail.vue` has a self target only in combat.
- Server: `use_ability` (`spacetimedb/src/reducers/items.ts` ~line 872) passes `targetCharacterId` straight to `executeAbilityAction` out of combat, with NO check. Any character id anywhere can be healed or buffed, and a heal on a character at 0 HP raises the HP (a revive with no corpse flow). The cast path (`character_cast` row, completed by `tick_casts` in `spacetimedb/src/reducers/combat.ts` ~line 1648) has the same gap. In combat, `submitCombatChoice` already checks the ally ('That target is not in this fight.').

Purpose: party healing and buffing works out of combat the way it does in fights, from the same tap; the server stops trusting an out-of-combat ally id.
Output: a shared out-of-combat ally rule (`@game-data/ally_target_rules`); a server check in `use_ability` and `tick_casts` with real-handler tests and a local publish; the client controller keeping the selection out of combat; the hotbar sending it; target buttons on the mobile chips, the rail member cards and the self blocks; updated tests; and a 51.1 UI-SPEC owner amendment.

NOT in scope:
- Pet targeting. It needs server work the owner placed in backlog 999.4 (999.27 was merged into it). Note it in the SUMMARY.
- The mobile Party sheet member cards stay non-target cards (the owner named the chips and the desktop cards).
- No schema change, no reducer signature change, no bindings regeneration.
</objective>
