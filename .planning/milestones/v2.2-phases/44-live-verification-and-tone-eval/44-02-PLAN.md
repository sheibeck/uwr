<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| provider reply to executor | Status, headers and body are untrusted and may be empty or malformed |
| executor to player-facing text | Only in-voice lines may cross; raw errors must not |
| player jobs to shared ledger | A refund or charge must apply exactly once per job |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-44-02-01 | Information disclosure | a failure line exposes provider, status, key or which cause (switch or ceiling) stopped the job | high | mitigate | Leak-pattern assertion over every recorded line; resting line asserted identical for both causes; expected lines imported, not retyped |
| T-44-02-02 | Tampering | double refund or double charge when two causes hit one job, or when jobs fail in different orders | high | mitigate | Simultaneous-cause and two-order tests assert exactly one refund and identical end state; bigint equality |
| T-44-02-03 | Denial of service | a lock never released after a failure, stranding the player | high | mitigate | Every class by every lock-holding route asserts the lock's failure state; sweeper boundary test asserts stuck jobs release |
| T-44-02-04 | Repudiation | a spied failure apply hides a real defect | medium | mitigate | The real applyLlmFailure is used; the wrapper only calls through |
| T-44-02-05 | Information disclosure | test fixtures hold a key-shaped literal | medium | mitigate | Fake key built from fragments, as in llm_executor.test.ts |
</threat_model>
